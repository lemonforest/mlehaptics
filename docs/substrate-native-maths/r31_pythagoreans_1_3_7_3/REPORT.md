### The 3 — Triad (τριάς; the "perfect" number)

The Pythagoreans called **3** the "perfect number" — the first to have beginning / middle / end. Per Aristotle *On the Heavens* I.1: "We say that 'all' and 'whole' and 'perfect' are three; the triad is 'the all'." Triadic structures in Pythagorean cosmology:

The **Heptad** is the Pythagorean "special" number. 1. **Arithmetic mean (ἀριθμητική μεσότης)**: a = (b + c) / 2 — equal-difference midpoint

3. **Harmonic mean (ἁρμονική μεσότης)**: a = 2bc / (b + c) — equal-musical-pitch midpoint
