### The 1 — Lever-principle (foundational mechanical axiom)

Per *On the Equilibrium of Planes* I postulates: the **lever-law** ("magnitudes are in equilibrium at distances reciprocally proportional to their weights") is the foundational axiom. Per Archimedes' famous "Give me a place to stand and I will move the earth" — the lever-principle is the foundational anchor.

**A-N**: Class A.

### The 3 — Three foundational principles

Archimedean tradition exhibits three explicit foundational principles per Heath 1897 + Dijksterhuis 1956:

1. **Lever-law** (mechanical equilibrium; *Equilibrium of Planes*)
2. **Buoyancy / Archimedes' principle** (hydrostatic equilibrium; *On Floating Bodies*)
3. **Method of exhaustion** (geometric approximation; *Quadrature of the Parabola* + *Measurement of a Circle*)

These three are the canonical Archimedean trio of physical-mathematical principles. They span mechanics + hydrostatics + geometric-analysis.

**A-N**: I + C + J.

### The +3 — Three method-types

Archimedean methodology canonically distinguishes three method-types per *Method of Mechanical Theorems* preface + Dijksterhuis 1956:

1. **Mechanical method** — the famous "Method" using physical balance to discover geometric theorems (the 1906 Heiberg rediscovery)
2. **Geometric proof** — rigorous Euclidean-style demonstration once truth was discovered
3. **Exhaustion / approximation** — the limit-process for proving area / volume / circumference results

The three method-types are explicitly meta-operations on geometric substrate: mechanical discovery → geometric proof → exhaustion-approximation. Each is a META-procedure operating on the underlying mathematical content. Direct match for R30 +3 = projection-enablers.
