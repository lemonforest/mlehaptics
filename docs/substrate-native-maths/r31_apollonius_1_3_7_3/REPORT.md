## §1 1:3:7:3 partition mapping (Apollonius attested)

### The 1 — Circle (the degenerate / foundational conic)

Per Apollonius *Conics* Book I.4-9 (Heath 1896 ed): the **circle** is the degenerate conic — the limiting case of the ellipse as eccentricity → 0. The circle is foundational because every conic section is obtained by tilting a circle's plane relative to a cone's axis. Pre-Apollonian Greek geometry (Euclid + Archimedes) treated circle as primary; Apollonius unified circles + ellipses + parabolas + hyperbolas under one framework with circle as foundational.

**A-N**: Class A.

### The 3 — Three non-degenerate conics

Apollonius' canonical contribution: the **three non-degenerate conic sections** named by him (Greek terms preserved):

1. **Ellipse** (ἔλλειψις — "falling short") — closed bounded curve
2. **Parabola** (παραβολή — "comparison/application") — open unbounded with single axis
3. **Hyperbola** (ὑπερβολή — "exceeding") — two-branch open with asymptotes

The three names + three structural classes are Apollonius' explicit primary contribution. Per Heath 1896 + Knorr 1986: this is the canonical 3-classification of conic sections in Hellenistic mathematics, transmitted directly to Kepler / Newton / modern geometry.

**A-N**: I + C + J (substrate-projection triad — each conic is cyclic-symmetry-class + oriented + numerically anchored).

### The 7 — Apollonius' eight books (seven extant)

Apollonius wrote **8 books of Conics**; **7 survive** (Books I-IV in Greek; Books V-VII in Arabic translation; Book VIII lost). Per Wikipedia + Heath 1896 prefatory material.

The 7-slot reading: 7 surviving books of the canonical text on conic sections. This is **slot-via-survival** rather than slot-via-structural-design — Apollonius designed 8 books, not 7. The 7-attestation is a historical-attestation accident.

**Verdict for 7-slot**: present as historical-attestation count (7 surviving books) but NOT structurally designed by Apollonius as a heptad. **REFINED** at this slot.

**A-N**: D + E + F + G + K + L + M (cascade-detection heptad) — slot-via-survival rather than slot-by-design.

### The +3 — Three method-categories (geometric / algebraic / locus)

Apollonius' three methodological approaches per Heath 1896 + Knorr 1986:

1. **Geometric construction** — projection-based proofs from cone-and-plane intersection
2. **Algebraic relation** — defining equations relating focal-parameters + directrices (the symptoma equations)
3. **Locus method** — points-satisfying-condition characterization

These three method-categories are explicitly **meta-operations on conic substrate** — they're not conics themselves but RULES FOR HOW conics are characterized + manipulated. Direct match for R30 projection-enablers.
