## §1 1:3:7:3 partition mapping (Stoic attested)

### The 1 — Logos (cosmic Reason / unity)

Per Diogenes Laërtius VII.135-136 + Cicero *De Natura Deorum* II.20-22 + Chrysippus fragments: **the Logos** is the cosmic foundational principle that pervades all matter. The Logos IS the One that the entire Stoic system ties back to. Per Hierocles *Elements of Ethics* + Marcus Aurelius *Meditations* IV.40: the cosmos is a single ensouled body.

**A-N**: Class A.

### The 3 — Three disciplines (philosophy-partition)

Per Diogenes Laërtius VII.39-41: Stoic philosophy partitions into **physics + ethics + logic** — the canonical Stoic triadic discipline-structure. Per Chrysippus' analogy: a garden's wall = logic, soil = physics, fruit = ethics (or alternative orderings; the triad is canonical).

**A-N**: I + C + J (substrate-projection triad).

### The 7 — REFINED slot

Stoics don't natively organize cosmology around a heptad. Candidate 7-slots in Stoic tradition:

- **Seven categories** (Chrysippus, per Simplicius): hexis / physis / psyche / hypostasis / qualitative-aspects / quantitative-aspects / relative-disposition — counted variously 4 to 7 in different sources
- **Seven passions** of the soul (Diogenes Laërtius VII.110-115) — variously 4 primary + secondary
- **Seven elements of pneuma-tension** — not canonical
- **Seven planetary spheres** — Stoics inherited this from Pythagorean-Platonic tradition; not Stoic-original

**Verdict for 7-slot**: present as inherited tradition (planetary spheres from Pythagorean-Platonic upstream) but NOT a native Stoic structural choice. **REFINED** rather than (a) SURVIVES.

**A-N**: D + E + F + G + K + L + M (cascade-detection heptad) — present as inherited rather than native.

### The +3 — Three pneuma-modes / tensional states

Per Chrysippus + Cleanthes (transmitted via Diogenes Laërtius + Stobaeus): pneuma has three tensional states:

1. **Hexis (ἕξις)** — coherent disposition (inanimate matter; minimal tension)
2. **Physis (φύσις)** — growth/life-force (plants; medium tension)
3. **Psyche (ψυχή)** — soul/animation (animals; high tension)

(Some sources add λογική — rational soul — as a fourth tier specific to humans; mainline Stoic tradition holds this as a level of psyche rather than a fourth pneuma-mode.)

These three pneuma-modes are explicitly **meta-operations** on substrate matter — they're not substrate-content themselves but RULES FOR HOW pneuma organizes matter at different tensional intensities. Direct match for R30 projection-enablers reading.

**A-N**: B + H + N (meta-cascade triad / projection-enablers).
