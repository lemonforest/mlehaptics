Almagest I.5: the Earth at the center is the immobile reference for all astronomical computation. Per Toomer 1984 ed: Ptolemy's entire framework reduces to relations measured from a single central observation point. The 1-anchor is structural.

### The 3 — Three classes of astronomical phenomena

Almagest I.8: Ptolemy partitions astronomical phenomena into three classes:

1. **Fixed-star sphere** (daily diurnal motion; outer sphere; uniform rotation)
2. **Sun + Moon** (luminaries with their own motion against fixed stars; period 1 year + ~28 days)
3. **Five wandering stars** (Mercury / Venus / Mars / Jupiter / Saturn — combined inferior + superior planets; epicyclic motion required)

The three-class partition is canonical in Books I-II of the Almagest.

**A-N**: I + C + J.

This is THE cleanest +3 = projection-enablers reading in all antiquity. Ptolemy's three modeling-mechanisms operate on planetary positions:

1. **Deferent** — primary circle on which a secondary circle's center moves
2. **Epicycle** — secondary circle on which the planet moves
3. **Equant** — third reference-point about which the deferent's center moves uniformly (Ptolemy's innovation beyond pre-existing deferent+epicycle)

Per Toomer 1984 + Pedersen 2010: the equant in particular is the most important Ptolemaic innovation — it's the projection-enabler that allowed planetary positions to be predicted with greater accuracy than uniform-circular-motion alone could achieve.
