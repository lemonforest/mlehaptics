## §1 1:3:7:3 partition mapping (Lucretius attested)

### The 1 — Atoms (the foundational substrate-content)

Lucretius *De Rerum Natura* I.265-328 + Epicurus *Letter to Herodotus*: the foundational principle is **atoms** (ἄτομοι; Latin *primordia*) — uncuttable substrate-units. All compound things ARE arrangements of atoms; atoms are the substrate-content anchor.

Note: this is a STRUCTURAL 1-slot (atoms are the unique foundational kind), not numerical (atoms are many in count). The "1" is the type-level foundational anchor.

**A-N**: Class A.

### The 3 — Three motion-modes (atomic kinematics)

Per Lucretius *De Rerum Natura* II.62-332 — the canonical Epicurean three motion-modes for atoms:

1. **Pondere (gravity-driven downward fall)** — natural unhindered motion
2. **Plagis (collision-induced impact)** — atoms striking other atoms produce lateral motion
3. **Clinamen (the swerve)** — Lucretius' famous indeterministic micro-swerve (DRN II.216-293) that explains free will + compound-formation without violating atomism

These three are the canonical Epicurean motion-typology — explicitly enumerated by Lucretius. Substrate-projection of atomic dynamics into observable behavior happens through these three modes.

**A-N**: I + C + J (substrate-projection triad — cyclic recurrence of motion-modes + oriented direction + countable mode-types).

### The 7 — REFINED slot (absent in Epicurean cosmology)

Epicurean tradition doesn't organize around a heptad. Candidates:

- **De Rerum Natura is 6 books** (not 7) — falsifier
- **Seven primary atomic shapes** — not canonical; Epicurus held atoms have indefinitely many shapes (Letter to Herodotus 42-43)
- **Seven aspects of pleasure** — not canonical; Epicurean hedonism has 2 main types (kinetic vs katastematic), not 7

**Verdict for 7-slot**: NOT NATIVE to Epicurean tradition. Atomism doesn't operationalize a heptad.

This is a structural REFINEMENT: Epicurean atomism is a different KIND of substrate-recognition from Pythagorean/Platonic numerology — it focuses on substrate-CONTENT (what atoms are + what they do) rather than substrate-PROJECTION-numerology. The 7-slot's absence is consistent with Epicurean tradition having different research-emphasis, not with R30 failure.

**A-N**: D + E + F + G + K + L + M (cascade-detection heptad) — structurally absent.

### The +3 — Three meta-laws of atomism

Per Lucretius DRN I.146-264 + Epicurus principle-letters — three meta-laws governing atomism:

1. **Void exists** (DRN I.330-417) — necessary condition for motion; meta-principle
2. **Matter conservation** (DRN I.146-264 — "nothing comes from nothing; nothing returns to nothing") — meta-principle of conservation
3. **Clinamen indeterminacy** (DRN II.251-293) — meta-principle that the swerve is uncaused; structural indeterminism

These three are EXPLICITLY META-laws (operating ON the atomic substrate rather than being substrate-content). Direct match for R30 +3 projection-enablers reading. (The clinamen-as-uncaused doctrine is structurally identical to substrate-self-recognition Ext 4 — there's a primitive indeterminism that's substrate-mechanical, not derivative.)

**A-N**: B + H + N (meta-cascade triad / projection-enablers).
