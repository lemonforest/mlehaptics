## §5 Why scouting is the highest C-scorer

Scouting (0.375) tops C, even higher than geography (0.281). The hit
set is revealing:

> above, around, between, left, near, north, right, south, through,
> turn, under, west

These are NAVIGATION-IN-3D-SPACE vocabulary. Scouting for Boys (Baden-
Powell, 1908) is essentially:
- Map-reading instruction (orientation; cardinal directions)
- Path-finding (forward/back; through/around)
- Spatial awareness (above/below; left/right)

This makes Scouting THE training substrate for C-projection: it
delivers directional knowledge as a practical-applied substrate. The
biological precedent: place cells + grid cells in mammalian
hippocampus encode exactly this projection.

Per Finding 100 (information cascade hierarchy):
- Place cells (biological substrate) encode location-in-space
- Cultural transmission (generational substrate) via Scouting/maps
- The C-projection enables both substrates to anchor to the same
  cosmic-substrate operator
