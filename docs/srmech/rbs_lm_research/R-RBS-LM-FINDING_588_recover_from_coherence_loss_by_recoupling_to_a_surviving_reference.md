## Verdict
**A missed note re-coheres by re-coupling, not by restarting.** A few perturbations into a strong coherent field dip then return — the perturbed oscillators re-lock to the surviving majority. That is the etak move (F580): re-acquire the bearing from the moving reference; no absolute frame needed.

**The conductor is the crux — "if you can still couple with something" (shown cleanly).** Even a fully-scrambled section re-coheres (0.98) when a pinned beat (the conductor) stays coherent; the same total scramble with no reference + weak coupling stays incoherent (0.41), and a few misses into a too-weak field also fail (0.10). So **recovery requires something coherent to couple to** — the surviving field or the pinned conductor; remove both and coherence does not heal.
