## Verdict
**Living dirt/sand drawing does two things the dead cave board could not.** (1) It partially **lifts the F645 ceiling**: cave art's makers are gone (structure only), but living sand-drawing has *practitioners* — so the structural key (F645) *plus* the community gives **meaning**, not just structure; the "expert" (F282) is the living tradition-holder, dignity-first, the community the authority. (2) **Vanuatu is a living attestation of the framework's core**: one graphic system communicating across ~80 spoken languages **is** a shared-invariant meaning-class IR *above* the language-boards (F613/F627/F637/F645) — the bit-exact-IR-above-languages has been *run by people for generations*, not posited; and their **imagined grid IS the board lattice** (F632/F633, verified spectral object), the continuous line a walk on it.

**Disconnected convergence** (Warlpiri / Ni-Vanuatu / global cave art, no contact, all landing on a small sign-lexicon for person/place/water/path) ⇒ the shared invariant is **universal, not a lineage artifact** (the whole-corpus-is-the-proof shape). And **ephemerality is etak made literal**: the board is drawn-and-erased, the meaning held in the practitioner — the clearest demonstration that the board ≠ the meaning.

**Dignity + scope (load-bearing):** these are living cultural / often sacred practices of the Warlpiri and Ni-Vanuatu peoples. The framework reads **structure only**; the **meaning belongs to those communities**; the tradition-holders are the authority (F282, dignity-first).
