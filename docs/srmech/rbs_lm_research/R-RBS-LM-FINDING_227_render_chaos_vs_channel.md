## §1 The test, stated precisely (the render-side complement)

F221/F224 measured the **addressing** side — *which* continuation is legal and *how* candidates rank — and found recipient/depth is **render-only**: the depth anchor does not steer retrieval (F221 0/3 retrieval NULL; F224 condition-(C) labeled-key Tier-2 re-weight sep 0.0409 < p90 0.0500, pctile 0.12, **below its own matched-random floor**). F227 runs the **render-side complement**: holding the **context (legal candidate set) FIXED** and varying **only** the channel band, is the actually-**EMITTED render** — the sequence the F166 autoregressive loop (R-RBS-LM-129 `gen_substrate`) generates under each channel anchor — systematically **channel-tuned** (its emitted surface tracks the recipient/register band) or **random**?

- **The hypothesis the design is built to be able to reject (the POSITIVE):** what a black-box LLM calls **chaos** in its output is real **render-axis channel-choice** — under a fixed context, the F166 loop's emitted surface (Klein-4 sector-occupancy of the emitted tokens, F129 length/structure stats, lexical/argmax distribution) varies *systematically* with the channel band (ELI5-shallow `[0,1]` vs peer-neutral `[0,1,2,3]` vs expert-deep `[2,3]`) **beyond** the F224 matched-random-label floor.
- **Fixed context, varied channel (the integrity of the test):** the channel anchor is applied at the **scoring step only** — the F224 condition-(C) Tier-2 labeled-key re-weight:
  `score = (1−α)·sim(M⊕ctx, cand) + α·sim(ctx⊕band_key, cand)`, `p = softmax(score, T)`.
  The candidate set `cands = next_after[last]` is computed **before** the re-weight; the channel only re-orders/re-weights *within* that fixed legal-set (an `assert len(p)==len(cands)` per step guards FIXED-CONTEXT integrity). That is what holds the context/legal-set fixed while varying the channel.
- **Strongest-working anchor (so a null is informative):** the band key is `enc("__band_{shallow|neutral|deep}__")` — the F165/F146 labeled-store key, bound the **exact** way F224 condition (C) bound it. Because (C) was a *clean retrieval-null*, a render-null here means even the strongest working anchor leaves the emitted render at the noise floor — not a weak-anchor artifact.
- **The render meters (4 families):** (F1) **emitted-sector-occupancy** — for each emitted token, `encode_word_k4(sector=s)` argmax under the channel-conditioned probe → per-channel emitted-sector histogram; cross-channel L1 (the **F168-native** depth readout, the EMITTED-sequence mirror of F221's `frame_retrieval` sector readout). ## §4 Why the lexical-overlap clear is the construction artifact, not channel-choice (the F214 §4 / F224 trap, re-encountered on the render side)

The design anticipated this exactly (the F227 risks block + §VII gate). Binding distinct channel labels into the Tier-2 score drives the **emitted-token-set divergence** (F3) toward the **orthogonality ceiling regardless of channel meaning** — a free-orientation floor is unbeatable and is the wrong gate, which is why F3 is gated against the **matched-mechanism random-LABEL** floor and is **not sufficient alone** for a positive. The data confirm the trap is live: the random-label floor for F3 is **0.922–0.936** (saturated near 1 − chance-Jaccard), the structured spread is **0.9341**, and the margin is **+0.00057**. A +0.0006 nick of a saturated ceiling is the orthogonal-binding residue, not depth-channel meaning — the same shape as F214's single "hit" (top-k Jaccard, §4 attributed to construction) and F224's (B) marginal +0.0022 floor-clear. The depth-relevant families (F1 native sector-occupancy, F2 structure, F4 spectrum) all sit at-or-below their floors. So the lexical clear is read as the construction artifact and the finding reads as a render-NULL on the channel-meaning axis.
