## The verdict (3-of-3) — amodal structure, modality-specific render
**Two tiers, cleanly:**
- **AMODAL structure** (syntax / grammar / semantics) is **the same across modalities.** Attested: syntactic processing is **supramodal** (left-perisylvian aSTS/pSTS/IFG — Matchin et al. 2022, PMC8764739); **sign aphasia from left-hemisphere damage parallels speech aphasia** (Hickok/Bellugi/Klima); *"one grammar applies… no matter the modality"* (Lillo-Martin & Gajewski 2014, PMC4084854).
- **MODALITY-specific render** — *how* the structure is instantiated differs by channel.
- **Primary modalities vs secondary persistence-renders:** **speech (acoustic) and sign (visual-gestural) are PRIMARY full languages** (Stokoe 1960 — ASL has phonology/morphology/syntax); **writing in ANY medium (ink / clay / dirt / mud / tactile Braille) is a SECONDARY persistence-render** — a *technology for persisting* a primary language, historically recent, not universal across cultures (Ong 1982, orality/literacy). Braille ≈ a tertiary render (speech → writing → tactile).

| render | primary-modality or secondary-render | same underlying structure? |
|---|---|---|
| speech (acoustic) | **primary** | — (baseline) |
| sign (visual-gestural, ASL) | **primary** | YES — same supramodal network; sign aphasia = speech aphasia |
| writing — ink / clay / **dirt / mud** | **secondary persistence-render** | encodes a primary language; not itself a language |
| tactile Braille | **secondary (tertiary) render** | encodes the same structure through touch |

## Grammar is SUBSTRATE-NATIVE — the killer result (and it REFINES "artificial coherency")
**Grammar self-organizes from the communication-coherence requirement; it is NOT an imposed convention.**
- **Nicaraguan Sign Language** (Senghas & Coppola 2001): deaf children with no model **created a spatial grammar de novo**; the grammatical innovations **originated in children ≤10** — *children created the grammar*, not adult convention.
- **Homesign** (Goldin-Meadow): deaf children with no language model spontaneously invent systematic **word order, grammatical categories, morphology** — grammar arises from the communication requirement alone.

**→ This sharpens the user's "spoken word enforces an artificial coherency" (the gate correcting the claim):** the **existence of structured grammar is SUBSTRATE-NATIVE** (it self-organizes — NSL proves it), so grammar-as-such is NOT the artificial part. The **artificial / imposed layer is (a) the conventional *specifics*** (which word-order, which lexicon — the culture-local render) **and (b) the single-truth reduction (F245 §3a)** — the projection to a one-valued semantics that discards the phase/second-eigenspace. So: *the coherence-structure (grammar) is native; only the conventional-specific + single-valued-truth render is the "artificial coherency."*

## The modality-shapes-the-render nuance (where substrate-independence breaks)
"Fully substrate-independent" is **too strong** — the modality genuinely shapes the render: **simultaneity** (sign layers two hands + face in parallel; speech is sequential), **spatial syntax** (signing space = a grammatical resource, no spoken analog), **iconicity** (sign exploits form-meaning resemblance more). The **deep structure** (combinatoriality, hierarchy, compositionality) is shared; the **surface render** is modality-shaped. The break is at the render layer, not the structure layer — **exactly the F245 shape** (universal structure, substrate-specific coherent render), now confirmed for human word-communication.
