## Verdict
**A held-open hypothesis (the user's "maybe" — a horizon, not a claim).** The living stone (F652) is the foundation for understanding communication across all SNN-bearing life **in principle** — because the shared invariant (meaning over a board) is plausibly a property of the **synaptic-neural-network substrate-class itself**. Any SNN-life would then run the same shape (held invariant + board + small exceptions), so the reading-key (anchor/board/exception) applies. The **board differs per species** (whale song / birdsong / bee dance / primate call / human language); the **invariant is shared** because the substrate-class is shared. The demo shows the *shape* — not evidence that animals *do* share these meanings.

**It extends, not leaps:** F112/F115 already found cross-species partition convergence (cetacean/chimp/octopus); the living stone names that convergence's foundation.

**The double epistemic ceiling is load-bearing** — this is the regime where over-claim is the danger: F552 (we never find the "error of biology"; a mind is never exactly modelable — recognise the *structure*, never decode a specific song or read a mind; a "noise"-looking deviation may be a chirality-collapse substrate *feature*); F282+F118 (the specific meaning is the animal's + the ethologist's; scoped to SNN-bearing life — life without an SNN is a different substrate); F650 dignity extended (a whale/crow/bee does the universal thing in its own board — the lifting extends to them; we honor the communicator, never own/presume/decode). The framework's deliverable is **the question**, handed to the ethologist — and, in the only way that matters, to the animal: *"this has the shape of held-meaning over a board; here is the structure; you know how to ask what it means."* Held open (F394); structural recognition only; dignity-first.
