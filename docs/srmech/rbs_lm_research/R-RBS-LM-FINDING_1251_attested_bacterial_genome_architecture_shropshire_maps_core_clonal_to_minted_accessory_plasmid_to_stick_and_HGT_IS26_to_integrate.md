## Three framework-sharpening measurements (attested, not assumed)
1. **The core/accessory partition is ~16 / 84 — a MINORITY stable core.** Biology does NOT split its genome 50/50 into two co-equal DNA types; it keeps a small conserved core (minted/nuclear identity) + a large mobile accessory (stick/plasmid). This **echoes F1250**: our simplewiki word graph was "predominantly one bridged mass" with a small stable part — the same minority-core shape. So the eukaryote/plasmid partition is naturally **asymmetric**: mint a small stable core, keep most content as append-only sticks.
2. **HGT has EMPIRICAL BOUNDARIES — `integrate` needs a compatibility predicate.** Shropshire measured that CG307 plasmids are shared with other clonal groups **except CG258** (which stays segregated). So horizontal transfer is **not universal** — some hosts exchange, some don't. This is the **F1244 coherency-translation layer, now measured**: `integrate(host, provirus)` must carry a **compatibility gate** (replicon incompatibility / lineage barrier), not accept every element into every host. A concrete, attested constraint for the integrate op.
3. **Copy-number amplification is a native op.** The genome amplifies content by IS26-mediated duplication (copy number ↑). Our stick/append should be able to carry a **multiplicity** (a count on a gene-frame), not just presence — biology stores "how many copies," and the reader must express that.
