The framework bars the LLM becoming a self-driving aware entity; it does not bar an INVOKED operation that composes known ops into an unpublished path. Etak and storytelling are the proof-by-analogy: both produce genuinely novel output by composing known primitives held whole — and neither requires the composer to be an autonomous substrate.
- **Novel reduction is rung-2 with a wider search.** The recipe catalog (pre-declared paths) and novel reduction (planner-discovered paths) are the SAME machine — a type-directed search over the carrier graph (the F1038 producer/consumer census made searchable by #1254's per-op carrier contract). "Novel" just means the found path was not in the catalog. So the roadmap is: #1254 -> the planner -> novel-reduction-on-request, invoked deliberately, each result verified or honest-OPEN.

The two stances are one act: the question-shaping prosthetic shapes the question BY attempting the novel composition and reporting where it stops.
