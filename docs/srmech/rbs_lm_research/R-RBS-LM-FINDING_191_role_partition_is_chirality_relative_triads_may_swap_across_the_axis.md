## §1 The conjecture, precise
Separate two things:
- **The operator SET** — the 14 A–N. This is **chirality-INVARIANT** (= G₂ = the triality-fixed core, F183). It does not change with your pole.
- **The role-PARTITION** — which triad is the *operating / time-imaginary* 3 (paired with A into the time-quaternion = "our imaginary DoF," F187) vs which is the *substrate-projection* 3. **This may be chirality-RELATIVE.**

**For us:** B/H/N = our operating/imaginary triad (the act-of-ordering / projection-enabler ops, F185/R30); I/C/J = the substrate we project onto.
**At the mirror pole (the other Weyl projection, F176):** the conjecture is that the roles **swap** — **I/C/J becomes the operating/imaginary triad** (their "time"), and **B/H/N becomes the substrate.** What we *run with* would be what they *project onto*, and vice versa.

## §2 Why it's fitting (not paradoxical)
**C (chirality) sits in the swappable set.** Chirality is exactly the self-referential operator whose role *should* be pole-dependent (F133/F185). So "C is substrate for us but an operation for the mirror" is consistent with the whole observer-locking picture, not a contradiction — the operator that defines the pole is the one whose role the pole flips.

## §3 The falsifiable test (the discipline — not "everything mirrors")
This is **not** an automatic relabel. It is testable:

> Does the actual **chiral-flip operation** (γ₅ / Class-C; equivalently the F129 `4:3:(4:3) ↔ 4:3:(3:4)` dual) **map I/C/J ↔ B/H/N operator-by-operator**?
> - **If the flip exchanges them** (I→a B/H/N op, C→…, J→…) → the role-swap is **real**; B/H/N and I/C/J are genuine chirality-duals.
> - **If I/C/J and B/H/N transform *differently* under the flip** → the conjecture is **FALSE**: they are two structurally distinct triads, not duals, and their roles do **not** swap.

**Tautology guard** (`[[feedback_dont_pre_commit_spike_query_operators]]`): the test must be able to come back **"no swap."** Gated on the chirality/octonion ops landing in srmech (W10 / SO8 build).

The set is invariant; the *reading of roles* is pole-locked (F133).
