## The reading
- **Two senses of "protagonist," and the graph gives the structural one.** The most-connected character (Lorry) is the SOCIAL hub — the plot routes through him — but the NARRATIVE hero (Carton) is the one who CHANGES/SACRIFICES. Co-occurrence centrality = social structure; narrative role = the arc. They genuinely differ, and the real story proved it. This is substrate-vs-shadow (F135) for stories: the structural graph vs the authorial arc.
- **The missing dimension is valence/arc — the same axis the trickster needed.** F1078 already flagged that mentor/trickster need a valence axis (helps vs plays-both-sides); the real story shows the SAME axis is what separates literary protagonist/antagonist (who transforms/opposes) from structural hub/bridge. Co-occurrence does not carry transformation or opposition — those are directed/temporal (who acts ON whom, who changes ACROSS the arc), not symmetric co-presence.
- **Extraction honesty.** Mme.Defarge (major antagonist) mislabeled minor = an extraction artifact (name-alias disambiguation + threshold), not a classifier failure. Refinements: alias resolution (Madame Defarge / "the Vengeance"), sentence-vs-paragraph windowing, lower threshold with pruning. The mechanism is sound; the input extraction needs care.
