## §1 The neuron is ONE fiber, not the mechanism — and that is WHY it hid the others (LENS)

F248 established, "centered on nothing," that retain-and-recall is physically held in at least **six non-privileged media**, none of which is "the real one the others approximate":
1. **absorbed/internalized chemical** — *Physarum* sodium uptake as "circulating memory" (Boussard 2019);
2. **externalized / internal physical structure** — *Physarum* extracellular slime-trail (Reid 2012) + tube hierarchy; centriole template geometry; fungal cord anisotropy;
3. **transient ionic / membrane-potential** — Venus flytrap cytosolic Ca²⁺ leaky-integrator "count" (Böhm/Hedrich 2016); *Bacillus* biofilm K⁺ waves (Prindle 2015);
4. **epigenetic / chromatin** — vernalization FLC/Polycomb cellular memory; trained immunity;
5. **sequence / clonal** — CRISPR-Cas spacer arrays; adaptive-immune V-region selection;
6. **protein-conformational / prion** — CPEB/Orb2 functional prion (Si 2003); yeast Sup35 [PSI⁺].

The neuron's synaptic/electrochemical store is **one more medium on this list**, not its summit. What distinguishes it is not *kind* but **render-quality on three axes**: it is the **highest-salience** (a spike is loud and legible), the **fastest** (millisecond, not the hours-to-vernalization or generations-to-clonal), and the **most spatially precise** (addressable per-synapse). Those three render-virtues are **exactly the trap**: a store that is loud, fast, and precise becomes *the* thing an observer centers on — and **centering on the neuron renders the other five media invisible fibers.** This is the hidden-fiber discipline turned on its sharpest case: the most salient render of an action is the one most likely to be mistaken *for* the action.

**The decisive structural reason the neuron cannot be the center (F248, literature-owned):** neural and aneural stores **INTERLEAVE rather than nest.** A brainless slime mould habituates with **no neurons** (Boussard 2019); a box jelly does **associative learning in a rhopalial ganglion with no central brain** (Bielecki 2023). "Has a brain," "has neurons," and "can store" are **three separable axes** — there is **no single shared physical store** that the neuron sits atop. A nesting picture (store ⊂ neurons ⊂ brain) would license "the neuron is the mechanism"; the *interleaving* picture forbids it. The neuron is a fiber; the bundle has no privileged fiber.

> **What is the lens vs the literature here:** the SIX media, the interleaving, the behavioral cases = **literature-owned** (F248 + sources). "The neuron is one fiber, not the center, and its render-quality is *why* it hid the others" = **framework-reading** — a recognition of what the landscape already is under the no-privileged-class discipline, asserting no new biology.
