**The corpus is not narrative-shaped.** The local-coherence gate — a period-1 z-check built in *precisely* to detect this — shows 2 of 3 windows have consecutive lines *less* similar than a shuffle (z ≤ 0). The TLA slice has no document boundaries and is, in most windows, a mixed-source collection (funerary formulae, ritual instructions, offering texts from many monuments), not a single sequential story. The (x)EC arc (F1171/F1172) is about **within-narrative** recurrence — oral-formulaic repetition inside one text (the Parry–Lord connection, F1160). A bag of unrelated sentences is the wrong object, so this slice cannot *fairly* test the arc. The gate doing its job (surfacing the confound instead of reporting a spurious null) is itself the methodological win — a false-null would have been the easy, wrong story.

**But the identity tracks coherence monotonically — a directional positive, not a refutation.** The F1172 identity z rises with document-order: **2.0** in the one ordered window (local z=3.9), **1.3** and **0.6** in the two unordered ones. This is exactly what the mechanism predicts: the low-eigenmode↔recurrence-period identity is a *sequence* property (it needs the line-order intact — a period-P recurrence only lowers the period-P mode if the lines are actually P apart in sequence). So where the Egyptian text is coherent, the identity appears at the same marginal-positive level as a single Sumerian tablet; where the sequence is scrambled (mixed sources), it collapses toward chance. That is **consistent with cross-family generality**, conditioned on narrative coherence — the effect is not Sumerian-specific vocabulary or script, it is a property of coherent narrative sequence that showed up in Egyptian too when the sequence held.

(Note the F1171 "periodic energy above the aperiodic floor" is positive in all three windows, +12–17% — but in the unordered windows that reflects cross-document **formula** recurrence, e.g. the offering formula "ḫꜣ m tʾ ḥnq.t kꜣ ꜣpd…" recurring across monuments, which is real formulaic redundancy but *not* the within-sequence recurrence the identity needs. The identity z — the sequence-dependent measure — is the one that correctly separates ordered from unordered.)
