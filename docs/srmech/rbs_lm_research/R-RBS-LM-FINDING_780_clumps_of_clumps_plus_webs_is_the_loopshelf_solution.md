## The two ideas are ONE structure (and the demo already produced it)
1. **Clumps-of-clumps = the recursion dendrogram.** Recursive spectral bisection (F779) doesn't make a flat partition — it makes a TREE: super-clumps → clumps → sub-clumps (4 topics → 13 leaves in the demo). **That tree IS the multi-resolution map** (the planet→continent→city→street zoom). etak FIND descends it; each level is a coarser/finer map. "Clumps of clump maps" = the dendrogram, already built.
2. **Webs through the clumps = the cut edges.** Spectral bisection SEPARATES communities by cutting the sparse inter-community edges. Those cut edges are not garbage — **they are the web of bridges between clumps.** Measured (8k articles, 32 seeds): within-clump edge weight **38,720** vs cross-clump (web) **12,663** — **3.1× denser inside than across** (dense clumps, sparse web, exactly community structure). The strongest bridges are *meaningful*: `singer(music) ── star(space)` (the **polysemy highway** — "star" = celebrity ∩ celestial), `moon──singer`, `planet──singer`, `dog──singer`.

**Together:** dense **clumps** (communities, the cities) + sparse **webs** (the cut-edge bridges, the highways), organized **hierarchically** (clumps-of-clumps, the recursion tree). This is the textbook *hierarchical community structure + inter-community edges* of real graphs — and recursive spectral bisection produces BOTH for free: the tree (clumps-of-clumps) AND the cut edges (the web).

## Why this is the solution shape (resolves F758/F777/F778)
- **Storage** = the clump TREE (each leaf a bounded tome — no over-capacity because a leaf-community is small, F778) **+ the WEB** (the cut edges per level — sparse, so cheap to keep). Bounded, hierarchical, lossless-per-clump.
- **Navigation (etak)** = **FIND** descends the tree (which super-clump → clump → leaf — the zoom); **RIDE** is *dense within a clump* (the coherent city, F779) OR *crosses a web-bridge* between clumps (the F776 reasoner relating two topics = a walk on the web). The within/cross 3.1× ratio IS "loss is viewport-width" (F778): dense coherence locally, sparse bridges globally.
- **No forced caps, no overload** (F778): each leaf holds one community; the planet is never in one bundle; multiple etak walks = descending the tree + crossing web-bridges cover everything.
- **The polysemy bridges are a feature:** `star` linking music↔space is exactly how a word in two clumps is navigated — it lives in one clump but the web carries its other sense. (Connects to the F773/F769 sense-resolution: which clump's sense applies is the context read.)

## Honest scope
- **Validated at small scale** (32 seeds → 100% pure clumps, F779; 3.1× web density, meaningful bridges, here). It is a **candidate solution architecture**, demonstrated in miniature — not yet built at 244k-vocab. The full build (#223) is the engineering: recursive bisection over the full graph (the tree) + retain the per-level cut edges (the web) + store leaves as tomes.
- **Open:** the full-scale recursion still needs the hierarchical method to stay under the n≤256 dense Laplacian per node (it does, by construction — each bisection works on a sub-graph) — but the TOP super-graph (244k nodes) needs a coarse first cut (sparse Fiedler / a coarsening pass), possibly an UPSTREAM Class-L sparse-partition ask.
- Bridges are weighted co-occurrence (real), not curated; the web is emergent. srmech-native (Class-L); no numpy/abs/CAD; data outside the repo.
