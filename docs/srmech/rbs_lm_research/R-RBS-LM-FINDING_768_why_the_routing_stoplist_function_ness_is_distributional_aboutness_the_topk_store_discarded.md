# F768 — introspection: why the routing stoplist exists, and why function-ness ISN'T read from meaning today (the top-K store discarded the signal)

**Date:** 2026-06-15 · **srmech:** 0.7.5rc155 · **Composes:** F751 (the ROUTING_STOPLIST + its own note "principled successor: judge salience against the big wiki corpus once wired"), F752/F753 (the FRAME vs TOPIC role channel — the contextual resolver), F172 (the co-occurrence Laplacian eigenspectrum IS the storage signature), F758 (top-K truncation = a lossy projection that discards a signal), the CLAUDE.md §2 STOP-list (`Counter()` co-occurrence → use the Class-L Laplacian) · **User direction (2026-06-15):** "introspect to understand why we need a routing stoplist, why it isn't understood from the meaning of the language."

## The question
`ROUTING_STOPLIST` is a hand-coded list of function/scaffolding words ("made", "way", "answer", "how"…) that must NOT route a query as a topic. Why a hand-list — why isn't "this is a function word, not a topic" read from the meaning of the language?

## What introspection found (grounded in the live genome, not theory)
Function-ness **is** a distributional/meaning property — *aboutness*: a content word's contexts are topically CONCENTRATED, a function word's are DIFFUSE (it co-occurs with everything). That difference is measurable in principle. But three concrete reasons it isn't derived today, each visible in the data:

1. **The cleanest signal was thrown away at storage.** The sharpest aboutness cue is **co-occurrence SPREAD** (a function word touches a huge, diverse neighbourhood). But the assoc store caps every word at **top-K = 16** neighbours — so **degree = 16 for EVERY word**, function or content. The top-K projection (a `Counter`-style edge-list — the very anti-pattern the STOP-list warns against, F758-style truncation) **discarded the spread**. The framework-native object that *keeps* it is the **Class-L co-occurrence Laplacian eigenspectrum (F172)** — spectral centrality/diffuseness literally IS function-ness — but the store isn't that; it's the truncated list.

2. **Frequency alone conflates "common content" with "function."** Measured corpus freq: `music` 6942, `computer` 2009 (CONTENT) are *more frequent* than `answer` 253, `question` 294, `thing` 746 (SCAFFOLDING). A frequency/IDF threshold mis-fires — the classic "function = high-freq" intuition fails for common content words. (The freq distribution is also wildly skewed: median 1, p90 17 — a tiny high-freq head over a 213k rare tail.)

3. **It's a GRADIENT, and partly CONTEXTUAL — not a fixed word property.** A salvageable proxy, *neighbour-average-frequency* (does the word live in the high-freq soup?), separates the extremes — `tomato` 1203, `mitochondria` 626, `photosynthesis` 1560 (clustered content) vs `answer` 4189, `thing` 5274, `way` 5910 (scaffolding) — but `computer` 4907 and `music` 6635 sit in the overlap (frequent content), and generic categories (`city` 8270, `country` 9304) behave function-ishly. So it's a gradient with a fuzzy middle. AND "way"/"answer" are function in a *question* but content elsewhere ("the Milky **Way**", "the **answer** to life") — so function-ness is finally a **role in THIS sentence**, which is the **FRAME-vs-TOPIC channel (F752)**, not a per-word label. A per-word measure can only ever be a *prior*.

## The synthesis (the honest "why")
The stoplist is a **hand-coded prior standing in for a meaning-derived aboutness signal that the framework knows how to compute (Class-L spectral centrality, F172) but the current top-K assoc store cannot express** — it discarded the spread (1) and kept only a conflating frequency (2) — **and that even when computed gives a gradient the contextual frame/topic role must resolve (3).** So "it isn't understood from the meaning of the language" because the **storage shape threw away the part of distributional meaning that encodes function-ness**, and because function-ness is ultimately **role-in-context**, not a word's intrinsic meaning. It is the same recurring pattern as the elaboration keyword-list and the markup-strip: a hand-list substitutes for a meaning-mechanism we haven't yet measured — here because the substrate that would carry it was truncated away.

## The meaning-derived replacement (the path, not yet built)
- **Cheap, available now:** a *neighbour-frequency / corpus-aboutness* gate (route a word iff its aboutness percentile is content-like) — replaces the *extremes* of the hand-list, leaves the fuzzy middle to the role channel. Partial, but principled and growable.
- **Framework-native, principled:** restore the co-occurrence **spread** (not top-16) and read **Class-L Laplacian spectral centrality** (F172) — high-centrality/diffuse nodes = function; peripheral/clustered = content. This is the real "aboutness from the spectrum of the language." Needs the spread the top-K store dropped (composes the F758/loopshelf storage-shape work).
- **The contextual layer (already present):** lean the FRAME-vs-TOPIC role channel (F752/F753) harder for the gradient middle — function-ness as the word's *role*, the only fully-correct answer for "way"/"answer".

## Verdict
The routing stoplist is a hand-coded **prior** for *aboutness*, needed because (1) the top-K=16 assoc store discarded the co-occurrence SPREAD that distinguishes function from content (degree is a constant 16 for all words), (2) the frequency it kept conflates common content (`music`/`computer`) with scaffolding, and (3) function-ness is a contextual ROLE, not a fixed word property. The meaning-derived replacement is **Class-L co-occurrence spectral centrality (F172) + the frame/topic role channel (F752)** — meaning *can* decide this, but only once the storage keeps the spread the truncation threw away. Grounded in the live 213k-word genome; the generating probe is in the session exchange.
