**The readout, and the calibration fix it required (the F214 §4 / F221 §3 construction-artifact, re-encountered and corrected).** The mission asked for a band-conditioned ranking-divergence with real dynamic range, gated against a random floor. A *raw* shallow-vs-deep divergence, however, **saturates** near the two-independent-random-orientation ceiling (Spearman ρ≈0 → divergence≈0.5) for the (A) and (B) mechanisms, because binding two *distinct* orientations into a probe drives the two rankings toward orthogonality **regardless of whether the bands carry depth meaning** — exactly the F214 §4 / F221 §3 trap. > **separation** = | rankdisp(shallow, baseline) − rankdisp(deep, baseline) |,  where rankdisp = (1 − Spearman ρ)/2, sign-free via `cascade.magnitude` (never `abs()`).

A depth axis that STEERS moves shallow and deep by DIFFERENT amounts (separation large); a render-only axis moves both like a random label does (separation ≈ its matched random-label floor).
