## What the prototype shows (live 0.7.3, stock primitives)
1. **Random-access-by-NAME (Demo A):** K `(slot→value)` pairs packed into **one** hypervector; read any slot by its sedenion address (`bind` self-inverse + nearest-codebook). **100% to K=64**, 99.2% at K=128. The capacity is D-bounded (HDC crosstalk) — **not** the octonion split; the 16 named slots all read cleanly. *This is the "larger named structure containing the pieces."*
2. **Bit-exact reversible working word (Demo B):** the **F459 coupler** binds 7 values into the octonion's imaginary slots; `inverse=True` recovers them at **err 6.7e-16** — bit-exact, **classically**, reversible **only ≤𝕆** (the 8th value cannot enter the octonion; the coupler caps at the Hurwitz boundary). The overflow spills to the **EC block** as a Hamming(7,4) word and a 1-bit corruption is **corrected**. *Working set + carry, exactly the front-loader (F442/F449).*
3. **Turn it on itself (Demo C):** the instrument stores its **own** operations (`A:hash, I:cyclic, C:chiral, M:bind, M:bundle, M:couple, L:laplacian, K:pin`) at sedenion addresses and reads them back **8/8**. 4. **The hyper-LOOP (Demo D):** the 16 addresses are **not** independent — they close under sedenion × (`e1·e2=e3`, `e1·e10=−e11`, `e4·e15=−e11`). Sedenion multiply IS structured address navigation (pointer arithmetic over the named container); "loop", not "ring" (the substrate-vocabulary depth-shift).
