## The reading (honest split)
- **The structure is real and read-independent.** byte-glyph sim *is* spelling (0.581 for cat~cot, vs 0.255 = baseline for king~emperor — it cannot see usage), and it *fuses* spelling-neighbours (church/churchy 0.707) where usage keeps them separate (0.247). F920 re-confirmed structurally. This is the sound part.
- **But the fusion does NOT hurt next-token selection (the hypothesis was NULL).** When the correct word is a candidate and was the stored target, its recovered vector matches at ~1.0 while its spelling-neighbour matches only at ~0.707·(that) — so the exact target *dominates* its degraded neighbour, and byte-glyph selection is never fooled: **100% = usage's 100%.** The "usage-overrides-valid-word lifts recall" hypothesis is **not supported** for clean selection.
- **This is `[[feedback_read_independent_structure_check_first]]` in the reverse direction from F1002.** F1002: real structure (circulant), *spurious* recall advantage (+6pp noise) — didn't ship it. F1004: real structure (spelling-fusion), *absent* recall disadvantage (exact-target dominance) — don't ship the lift either. In both, the read-independent structural measure and the read-dependent recall number **disagree**, and the honest move is to trust neither blindly and report the split. The structure being real does not make the recall claim real.
- **Where the resolver's lift actually is: edit-distance CLEANUP.** The *same* spelling-fusion is a **feature** for cleanup — a corrupted/near-miss emission is spelling-close to its intended word, so edit-distance snaps it back (100% recovery of drop-1-char corruptions). The **usage-override** earns its keep only at edit-distance **ties** (≥2 valid words equidistant) — which never fired here (0/10 with distinct words), so it is a rare tie-breaker, not a broad recall-lift.

## Honest scope
The `(A)`/`(B1)` structural facts are read-independent and solid. The `(B2)` NULL is the load-bearing honest result: **the usage-overrides-valid-word RECALL-LIFT is not supported** at the clean-selection operating point (exact-target dominance protects byte-glyph selection). The `(C)` cleanup 100% is somewhat easy (edit-1 corruption of distinct words → nearest valid word is the original); the *hard* cleanup cases are ties, which are rare — so the usage-override is design-available but rarely-triggered, not a headline lift. A regime where usage-override *would* lift recall (target NOT the exact stored word — OOV/generalisation, where the read returns a stored word's spelling-neighbour instead of the usage-correct word) was not constructed here and is the honest next-question. No fluency claim; sparse; no abs/Counter.
