## Verdict
**A story is not maximal sync — it is the dynamics of coherence-and-separation.** Maximal sync is a featureless drone (verified: everyone-in-the-flock → global spread ~0, no protagonist). A story needs the flock to **separate for the one**: the flock coheres (a chorus) *while* the one stands apart (the protagonist, gap +1.5). De-sync *is* the tension; the arc is separation-against-coherence.
