## Verdict
**No distinct clause-scale phrase unit at the raw-chord grain.** Within-sentence consecutive coherence (0.0376) ≈ across-sentence-boundary coherence (0.0401), both far above random (0.0015), with a negative "drop" (−7%) — the sentence boundary is a **soft seam**, not a coherence break. The story-teller wave's coherence is **article-topic** (consecutive chords share a topic whether or not they cross a sentence boundary), confirming F605 (topic persistence, not clause/harmonic structure).

**Honest confound:** within-sentence similarity rises with lag (0.038→0.062), but that is a sentence-length selection effect (only longer, content-dense sentences have far-lag chord-pairs, and they are more topically coherent), not coherence growing with distance.

**This sharpens F601** — its >8-chord "length" was a *topic* scale, not a clause scale. **Genuine clause/grammar structure, if captured, lives in the function-word/positional/order signal (F569) and the seam-aware parse bind (F604), not in content-chord coherence** — which is topical at every scale measured. Null findings count (no-leaning rule); favored not privileged (F398); held open (F394).
