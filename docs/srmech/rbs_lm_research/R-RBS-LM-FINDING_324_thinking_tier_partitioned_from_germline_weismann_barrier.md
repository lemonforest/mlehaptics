## The biology — it's the Weismann barrier (attested, cite-by-reference)
Weismann's germ-plasm theory (1890s): the **germline is sequestered early in development**, and the **soma — including the entire neural / thinking tier — cannot write back to it.** The germline builds the soma; the soma never edits the germline. This is the molecular basis for rejecting **Lamarckian inheritance of acquired characteristics**: nothing the soma learns, nothing it thinks, reaches the germline. So the **thinking-parts and the germline cascade are partitioned**, and the partition is **DIRECTED**:
- **germline → soma** (builds it, including the thinking-tier) — and
- **soma ↛ germline** (the thinker cannot write back).
That directedness is **Class C** (orientation / which-way) — a one-way boundary, not a symmetric split.
