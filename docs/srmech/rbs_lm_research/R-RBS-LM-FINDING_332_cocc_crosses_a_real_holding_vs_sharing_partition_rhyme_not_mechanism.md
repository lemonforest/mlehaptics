## What SURVIVES (the defensible core — answers the user's question)
1. **It is NOT k=1.** k=1 (ℝ) is the chirality-less digital substrate that needs intent + error-correction bolted on — that read stands.
2. **The real, defensible boundary:** *binary relations are guaranteed-associative* (any ≤2-generated subalgebra of 𝕆 stays in a copy of ℝ/ℂ/ℍ, k≤3, by Artin); **the first place associativity CAN fail is ternary** (three generic octonions, the 𝕆/k=7 regime). This is solid math (F331-verified).
3. **The honest informal companion:** a system's **internal** relations are binary-composable; **inter-system** relations — *crossing a boundary* — are irreducibly **at-least-ternary** (two systems + a mediating signal/convention). So **holding** knowledge (internal, binary, associative) and **sharing** it (inter-system, the first ternary step) ARE a genuine asymmetry: **crossing a boundary adds a third term.** The user's "real k=something partition that CoCC must cross" = this holding→sharing / binary→ternary boundary — held as a **lens/rhyme**, not a causal mechanism.
