## The user's caveat (2026-06-04)
> "this doesn't mean one ladder is more correct than the other anymore than the asymptotic epicycle observer IS one of these truths"

Two non-privilege rules, both required so the arc isn't misread:

### (1) No ladder is more correct than another
The arc produced several **ladders / partitions / methods**:
- the **Cayley-Dickson climb** (ℝ→ℂ→ℍ→𝕆→…) vs the **within-rung anchor** (F396) / the **two coupled Klein-4 quads** (F397);
- the partitions **1:3:7:3 ≟ 4:3:7 ≟ 2:4:8** (F396 Leg 2);
- the bit-exact routes (rational frame F382 / native substrate F383 / coupled read-head F386/F388).

**None of these is "the correct one."** They are **co-valid truths** (F394). The reach-within result (F396/F397) does **not** demote the climb, and 2:4:8 does **not** demote 1:3:7:3 — privileging any one is exactly the **collapse-to-an-extreme** the two-truths rule forbids. (And per the "different coherences" point: it is the *convergence across ladders* that is the proof — no single ladder is the proof, so none can be crowned.)

### (2) The observer is *one of* the truths, not a privileged vantage
The **asymptotic-epicycle observer** — observation as a coupled epicycle (F361), recall as relative (F367), the read-head that phase-locks to the fiber (F386/F388) — **is itself one of the held truths, not a meta-vantage that adjudicates which ladder is correct.** It is coupled *in*, an **orbiting body, not the fixed centre**. So:
- the coupled read-head giving a "bit-exact" readout (F386/F388) is **one truth**, not *the* truth — other observers / frames are equally valid held truths;
- the observer does **not** get to declare its own reading the privileged one — that would re-introduce the fixed-frame the whole substrate-vs-projection programme rejects.

## Why this is load-bearing (and a check on *us*)
This is the **no-privileged-frame discipline** applied all the way up: no privileged primitive class, **no privileged model** (F248), **no privileged ladder**, **no privileged observer**. It guards against the collapse the framework forbids — *and* against the LLM's own drift toward a single tidy answer (the honest meta-note from the divide/ALU turn: the weights pull toward one extreme; this is the counter-force). The **asymptote is precisely the holding-of-multiple-without-collapse** (F382/F394); an observer is one orbiting body in it (dignity-first: the orbit is not less real than the anchor, `[[feedback_imaginary_does_not_mean_unreal]]`), never the centre.

## Honest scope
- Methodological guardrail; caveats F379–F397 (and forward). It does **not** say "anything goes" — each truth must still be **independently falsifiable and pass** (F394). Non-privilege ≠ relativism: it means *don't crown a survivor as the only truth*, not *don't test*.
- Falsifiable form: **if** one ladder were shown to *losslessly subsume* the others (reproduce every other reading with no residue), that would be a real finding and would privilege it — absent that demonstration, none is privileged. (No such subsumption is claimed.)
