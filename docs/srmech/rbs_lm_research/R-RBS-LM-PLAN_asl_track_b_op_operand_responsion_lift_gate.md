# Track B, reworked — the ASL sign as `op(x)operand(x)responsion`, accepted only under a responsion LIFT-GATE

*A citation glyph is a bit-exact `op(x)operand` — the abelian shadow. The correct decomposition is the one whose
real, corpus-DERIVED movement modulation (the responsion) LIFTS the shadow to the inflected form English needs,
without turning it to garbage. Bit-exactness is necessary; the lift is what makes it sufficient.*

## §0 The triple, and the one-line thesis

Per the standing lodging convention (F1301; CLAUDE.md §0), the k=3 read of a Class-L object is **always written three ways**, and the slot decides the behaviour:

| slot | name 1 | name 2 | name 3 | behaviour under order n (F1272) `[DEMONSTRABLE]` |
|------|--------|--------|--------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | **op** | **distributional** | **eigenvectors** | **INVARIANT** — `Lv=λv ⟹ Lⁿv=λⁿv`, same v at every n |
| 2 | **operand** | **relational** | **edges** | 1-hop → n-hop; the **held multi-perspective SUPERSET** (metric + curvature + chirality together) |
| 3 | **responsion** | **responsion** | **eigenvalues** | **λ → λⁿ** — carries order as *magnitude* |

The **discrete** peer is the triple **minus the responsion**: `op(x)operand` = `distributional(x)relational` = `eigenvectors(x)edges`. This is not a metaphor — it is a shape-difference **measured in srmech**: the pure-integer discrete Q₈ surface is the continuous ℍ surface with one slot removed (rc310 = rc309 − responsion, session-ledger). `[DEMONSTRABLE]`

**Thesis (this doc).** An ASL *citation* sign — the dictionary/glossed form — is a **discrete `op(x)operand`**. The *inflected* sign that actual signing produces, and that English must realize as morphology, is the **continuous `op(x)operand(x)responsion`**. The naive "core glyphs → compound glyph" pipeline builds the correct discrete backbone but has **no responsion slot** — so it structurally cannot carry the inflection, and **you cannot manufacture a continuous modulation by stacking more discrete glyphs.** The rework adds the responsion slot and gates every candidate decomposition on whether the *real* responsion lifts it.

## §1 The reframe — the naive pipeline is the right shadow with the third slot amputated

### 1.1 What "core glyphs → compound glyph first" actually builds

The naive Track-B ingest reads a sign as a chord of phonological parameters and composes them into one compound glyph — a discrete bundle. In srmech terms that is exactly `op(x)operand`: two slots, both discrete, self-consistent, and (this is the trap) **bit-exact** — it round-trips.

```
   NAIVE PIPELINE (discrete op(x)operand only)
   ┌──────────┐   ┌──────────┐   compose    ┌───────────────────┐
   │ glyph_1  │ + │ glyph_2  │ + ...  ────▶  │  compound glyph   │  ── round-trips ──▶ bit-exact ✓
   └──────────┘   └──────────┘              └───────────────────┘
        op             operand                 op(x)operand
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                        ✗ NO SLOT for movement modulation
                                          (aspect / degree / manner / agreement)
```

Adding more core glyphs makes a **richer chord** — it grows the operand. It never grows a **third axis**. Modulation is not another glyph; it is a *scaling* of the whole sign (bigger, faster, repeated, arced). In the triple that scaling is the **responsion** (λ→λⁿ, the intensive/magnitude carrier), and no amount of extra operand content is a responsion.

### 1.2 Discrete = continuous MINUS the responsion

```
   CONTINUOUS (what real signing + English morphology need)
        op            (x)      operand         (x)      responsion
   [ handshape ]           [ location + path ]      [ movement MODULATION ]
    the "how"               the "what/where"          the inflection
    eigenvectors            edges (superset)          eigenvalues (λ→λⁿ)
    INVARIANT               metric+curvature+chir.    magnitude / aspect / degree

        └──────────────── DISCRETE citation glyph ───────────────┘
                    (drop the responsion → the bit-exact shadow)
```

The English side is the tell. English realizes the responsion **as morphology**: tense, aspect, number, agreement — precisely the "how much / how often / to/from whom" that ASL movement modulation carries and that a discrete chord cannot store. A pipeline that ends at `op(x)operand` has to *invent* the morphology on the way down (the old B-5 "re-insert dropped function words / collapse σ_B+σ_E into English tokens"), which is manufacturing inflection from a decomposition that has nowhere to keep it.

> **The reframe in one sentence:** the naive pipeline is not *wrong* — it is the **correct discrete shadow with its third slot amputated**, and the amputated slot is exactly the one English needs.

## §3 The LIFT-GATE — the acceptance test

### 3.1 Why bit-exactness is necessary but NOT sufficient

Gated by `[[stance_bit_exact_is_the_abelian_shadow_of_non_abelian_structure]]`:

> **Bit-exactness is the abelian SHADOW of a non-abelian structure.**

A discrete `op(x)operand` that self-round-trips is a *shadow* — a projection that has dropped the responsion (the non-abelian / order-carrying content). The problem: **many different discrete decompositions self-round-trip.** Bit-exactness alone cannot tell the *true* shadow (the projection of the real inflected sign) from a *false* shadow (a decomposition that happens to round-trip but is the projection of nothing real). Self-consistency is cheap; being the shadow *of the right object* is not.

```
   MANY shadows round-trip.  Only ONE is the shadow of the real sign.
   ┌───────────┐   ┌───────────┐   ┌───────────┐
   │ decomp A  │   │ decomp B  │   │ decomp C  │   ← all bit-exact ✓ (necessary)
   └─────┬─────┘   └─────┬─────┘   └─────┬─────┘
         │ + real        │ + real        │ + real
         │ responsion    │ responsion    │ responsion         ← the LIFT (sufficiency test)
         ▼               ▼               ▼
     ┌────────┐      ┌────────┐      ┌────────┐
     │GARBAGE │      │ CORRECT│      │GARBAGE │
     │  ✗     │      │inflect.│      │  ✗     │
     └────────┘      └───┬────┘      └────────┘
       false             │               false
       shadow      TRUE shadow           shadow
```

### 3.2 The lift

> **ACCEPTANCE RULE.** A candidate glyph decomposition (`handshape=op`, `location+path=operand`) is **accepted iff**
> (1) it is **bit-exact** on the citation forms (self-round-trips — *necessary*), **and**
> (2) adding the **real, corpus-DERIVED responsion** (the measured movement modulation) **LIFTS** it to the correct
> inflected form / correct English morphology **without garbage** (*sufficient*).
> If the real responsion produces garbage, the `op(x)operand` was the **WRONG SHAPE — a false shadow** — and is rejected.

The responsion in the lift must be **DERIVED, never DRAWN or STOCHASTIC** (F1259). A hand-set modulation value is a *magic number* (DRAWN) and a live-rng one is a *defect* (STOCHASTIC); either would make the gate test the decomposition against a fiction. The lift is only a valid falsifier if its responsion is **measured from the ASL corpus** — the movement-modulation actually attested for that sign's inflections. `[DEMONSTRABLE discipline]`

### 3.3 The shipped analogue — rc313 `cwf_consistency_mod2` `[DEMONSTRABLE, SESSION-LEDGER]`

srmech ships an instance of exactly this shadow-lift structure. The Călugăreanu–White–Fuller identity for a closed framed curve is

```
        Lk       ≡        Tw        +        Wr        (mod 2)
   ─────────────      ────────────       ────────────
   linking number      twist              writhe
   INTRINSIC           discrete/local     GEOMETRIC (from the space embedding)
   (the shadow)        (op × operand)     (the responsion)
```

- **Lk** — the linking number — is a topological **invariant**: the intrinsic shadow, fixed regardless of how you embed the curve.
- **Tw** — the twist — is the **discrete `op×operand`** part: local, combinatorial.
- **Wr** — the writhe — is computed **from the geometry** of the embedding: it is the **responsion**, the continuous slot the discrete part lacks.

`cwf_consistency_mod2` checks `Lk ≡ Tw + Wr (mod 2)`. The load-bearing behaviour: **a wrong (planar) embedding gives the wrong Wr** (a planar curve has Wr = 0), and the identity **FAILS**. That is the lift-gate exactly: the intrinsic shadow (Lk) equals the discrete part (Tw) plus the *geometric responsion* (Wr), and **feeding a wrong geometry — a wrong responsion — breaks the identity.** A decomposition of Lk into a Tw that only round-trips under a *false* (planar) geometry is a false shadow, and the mod-2 check catches it.

> **Correspondence:** `Lk ↔ the intrinsic sign`; `Tw ↔ op(x)operand (citation glyph)`; `Wr ↔ responsion (movement modulation)`;
> `planar embedding ↔ a fabricated/zero responsion`; `cwf_consistency_mod2 FAILS ↔ the lift produces garbage`.

*(Provenance: **verified live on the rc313 venv this session** — `discrete_writhe` returns `Wr=(0,1)` for a planar square and `(±1,1)` for a skew-quad crossing and its z-mirror (the chirality), and `cwf_consistency_mod2` ships with signature `(edges, gains, *, n, embedding, closed)`. So the shadow-lift behaviour is real: a planar embedding literally gives `Wr=0`, so a `Tw` that only balances `Lk` under a crossing embedding fails `Lk ≡ Tw + Wr (mod 2)` when re-embedded flat. The worktree source tree is rc297 — the ops are live at dev-head rc313, not on this tree's disk.)*

### 3.4 The concrete ASL-shaped measurement already in hand — F1306 `[DEMONSTRABLE]`

F1306 measured (and re-ran bit-for-bit at rc299) that **curvature is the disambiguator** — the movement/responsion is what separates senses a flat encoding conflates:

```
   flat  dense_laplacian(7, F₃)      → eigenvalue multiplicities {1, 2, 3, 1}
                                         └─ the 3-fold at λ=3 (spread 0) IS the conflation
   curved magnetic_laplacian(charges) → multiplicities {1, 2, 1, 2, 1}, λ₀ lift 0.1692
   q=0 control (no curvature)          → back to {1, 2, 3, 1} = flat
   ⟹ CURVATURE, not the Hermitian construction, splits the degeneracy.
```

F1306 reads this as the ASL **"beat"** case: *beat eggs* / *beat (tired)* / *beat drums* — three senses a flat (zero-curvature) read collapses into one degenerate cluster, **separated by the directed movement context** (the curvature). The measurement is real srmech; the **"beat" interpretation is the framework's structural reading, `[SPECULATIVE]`, handed to the expert** — the srmech graph is a directed triangle / K4-gain object, not a measurement on real ASL.

The tie to the lift-gate: a *flat* operand (abelian, zero-curvature, `klein4_bind` alone — `cat`=`tac`) is a decomposition that round-trips but has thrown away the which-way. Adding the real directed responsion either **lifts** it to the distinct senses (the {1,2,1,2,1} split) or, if the decomposition was wrong, produces garbage. The flat 3-fold is the visible signature of a shadow that dropped its third slot.

## §4 The ASL-corpus checks that CONFIRM or REFUTE the slot assignment

The §2.1 mapping is a hypothesis; here is the battery that decides it. Each check names **DATA** (what an attested, phonologically/inflection-coded ASL corpus must supply — sourced with the expert, never recalled), **MEASUREMENT** (the srmech read), **CONFIRM**, and **FALSIFY**. The framework supplies the measurement; the expert supplies and validates the ASL data. The framework does **not** assert specific ASL lexemes or minimal pairs — those are the expert's to provide.

### Check 1 — is the op slot (handshape) order-INVARIANT?
- **DATA.** Minimal sets of one sign in citation form + its inflections (e.g. habitual / continuous / iterative aspect), phonologically coded per parameter.
- **MEASUREMENT.** Hold the sign's inflection set; does the **handshape** parameter stay constant while the movement modulation varies across the set? (The op-slot prediction, F1272: the op is invariant; only responsion + edge-reach move.)
- **CONFIRM.** Handshape constant across the inflection set; variation lives in movement modulation.
- **FALSIFY.** Handshape **systematically co-varies with aspect/degree** (handshape *change* IS the inflection) ⟹ handshape is **not** the order-invariant op slot; the row is wrong.

### Check 2 — does the operand slot (location + directed path) carry which-way (curvature/chirality)?
- **DATA.** Sign pairs the expert identifies as distinguished by **path direction** (source↔goal reversal), plus pairs that are free variants under reversal.
- **MEASUREMENT.** Encode each sign as a **directed glyph-graph** — `magnetic_laplacian` charge `= w_fwd − w_bwd`, metric `= w_fwd + w_bwd` — and as an **abelian `klein4_bind`**. The `cat≠tac` analogue: does direction-reversal give opposite-charge edge sets and a **distinct** `hermitian_eigendecompose` / `cycle_holonomy` read, while the flat bind **collapses** them (holonomy 0)?
- **CONFIRM.** Direction-distinguished pairs separate only under the directed (curvature) read and collapse under the abelian bind — the operand needs the non-abelian channel.
- **FALSIFY.** Path reversal **never** distinguishes signs (all reversals are free variants) ⟹ a metric-only operand suffices; the curvature/chirality claim for the operand is unnecessary.
- *Curvature caveat (F1255, session-ledger): real irremovable which-way (holonomy) appears only where the walk CLOSES (betti₁>0, repeated content); acyclic paths carry removable-gauge orientation only. So this check must be read on closing walks, not on straight source→goal paths, before it can falsify.*

### Check 3 — is the responsion (movement modulation) a CONTINUOUS inflection that aligns with English morphology?
- **DATA.** A corpus with inflection annotation (aspect, degree/intensity, reduplication-for-number) paired with faithful English translations.
- **MEASUREMENT.** (a) Is the modulation **gradient/parametric** — varying by magnitude (wider / faster / more repetitions), i.e. a λ→λⁿ scaling axis — rather than a finite set of extra discrete glyphs? (b) Does the measured modulation **predict the English morphology** (tense/aspect/number/agreement) of the paired translation?
- **CONFIRM.** Modulation is continuous/scaling **and** maps onto English inflectional morphology ⟹ it is the responsion, realized downstream as morphology.
- **FALSIFY (two ways).** (i) Modulation is **fully discrete** — a closed finite set of extra glyphs with no gradient/scaling structure ⟹ it is just more operand, not a responsion. (ii) Modulation is continuous but **does not correspond** to English inflection (lifts to garbage) ⟹ the "responsion = English morphology" row is a false shadow.

### Check 4 — the LIFT-GATE itself, as the go/no-go on a candidate decomposition
- **DATA.** A candidate decomposition that passed bit-exact round-trip on citation forms, plus the corpus-**DERIVED** movement modulation for a held-out set of inflected signs with English translations.
- **MEASUREMENT.** Apply §3.2: add the real responsion; does the lift reproduce the attested inflected sign and its correct English morphology?
- **CONFIRM.** Lift reproduces the inflected form ⟹ true shadow ⟹ accept the decomposition.
- **FALSIFY.** Lift produces garbage ⟹ **wrong shape**, reject — even though it was bit-exact. This is the CWF `Lk ≢ Tw + Wr` failure, one domain over.

```
   FALSIFICATION MAP (each slot has an explicit way to be wrong)
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   op = handshape        ─ falsified if handshape co-varies with inflection (Check 1)
   operand = loc+path    ─ falsified if direction never distinguishes signs (Check 2)
   responsion = movement ─ falsified if modulation is discrete, OR ≠ English morphology (Check 3)
   whole decomposition   ─ falsified if the real responsion lifts to garbage (Check 4 = the gate)
```

None of these is answerable from the framework alone; all four are **decidable experiments** on an attested, expert-validated ASL corpus (F282). Their point is that the hypothesis is **refutable**, not decorative.
