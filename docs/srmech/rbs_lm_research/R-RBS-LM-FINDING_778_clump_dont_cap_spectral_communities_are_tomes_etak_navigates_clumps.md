**Date:** 2026-06-15 · **srmech:** 0.7.5rc165 · **Composes / corrects:** F777 (the "size-around-capacity via bigger D" framing — this REPLACES "force more capacity" with "partition into clumps"), F584 (the loop-bookshelf of TOMES — re-derived: tomes are SEMANTIC clumps), F542/F544 (tome routing; the loop shelf), F172 (the co-occurrence Laplacian eigenspectrum IS the storage signature — the clumps are its communities), F768/#221 (the SAME spectral structure gives aboutness AND the clumps), F704/etak (find the clump → ride within it), F119/F529 (two-tier; here the "tail" is *every other clump*, coarse) · **Queue:** reshapes #223; unifies #221 · **User direction (2026-06-15):** "we don't need to force caps, we need to let knowledge become partitioned into related things … start with entire planet earth, accept a lossy wide viewport, but zoom to something city-sized and have coherence to navigate this city without needing to know any other city … an etak navigation exercise … figure out how to let knowledge form in structured clumps … multiple etak walks to cover all the things might be how we keep from losing data by overload."

## The correction
F777 read the over-capacity read-out as a sizing knob (bigger D + IDF → cram more into one bundle). That is **forcing** capacity. The truer move: **don't put the planet in one bundle at all.** Let the co-occurrence structure **partition into clumps** of related things; hold each clump as a tome that is bounded *because it is one community*, not because a cap was imposed. **Capacity becomes emergent (per-clump), not enforced.**

## The architecture (your viewport intuition → the framework)
- **Clumps = spectral communities of the co-occurrence Laplacian** (Class-L / F172). Knowledge partitions by its OWN structure (emergent, discovered — the opposite of a hand-set cap).
- **Each clump = a tome.** A coherent community is naturally small → it fits a fixed-width bundle without over-capacity. (F584's loop-bookshelf-of-tomes, re-derived: tomes are *semantic clumps*.)
- **etak FIND = route to the clump** ("which city?"); **etak RIDE = navigate within it** at full resolution ("the coherent city map, without needing any other city").
- **Scale = resolution (LOD):** the **wide cross-clump view is necessarily coarse** (the planet — lossy *by nature*, and acceptable, not a failure); the **zoomed within-clump view is coherent** (the city). Loss is a property of viewport WIDTH, not of the storage.
- **Multiple etak walks cover the whole** without overloading any one bundle — because no bundle ever holds more than its community. *This* is how data isn't lost to overload: never fold the planet into one tome; walk city by city.

## Honest refinements
1. Clumping doesn't *eliminate* capacity — it makes it **natural** (size to the community), so F777's D-sizing becomes "the clump already fits," not "crank D."
2. **Cross-clump detail is the accepted coarse view.** The F776 reasoner relating two topics is fine *within* a clump, coarse *across* clumps (different cities); coarsening/declining across clumps is honest (F408-adjacent), not a bug.
3. **Clumps emerge** (spectral, F172); they are discovered from the data, not imposed.
4. **Unifies #221:** the same Class-L pass that yields function-ness/aboutness (spectral centrality) yields the community partition (the clumps) — one spectral structure, two reads.

## The genuine open problem (the "figure out how")
**Spectral-partitioning a 244k-word co-occurrence graph exceeds srmech's n≤256 dense Laplacian** (`jacobi_eigvals` / `symmetric_eigendecompose` cap at n≤256). So clumping at corpus scale needs a **hierarchical / coarse-to-fine** method (partition a coarse super-graph → recurse into each clump until each fits ≤256 / one tome) OR a sparse/streaming spectral method (Fiedler via iterative methods). This is the real research step — and likely an UPSTREAM ask if srmech should ship a sparse/hierarchical Class-L partitioner. Demonstrable now at tractable scale: Class-L `dense_laplacian` + `fiedler_vector` on a ≤256-word co-occurrence subgraph → show it splits into coherent topical clumps with within-clump coherence (the "city" navigability).

## Verdict
