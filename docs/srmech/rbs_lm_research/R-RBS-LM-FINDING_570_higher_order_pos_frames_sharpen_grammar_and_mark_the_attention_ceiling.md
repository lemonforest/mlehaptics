## Verdict
**Higher-order POS frames sharpen the grammar (SS-3).** The real-vs-shuffled discriminator rises 1.27× → 2.08× → 4.37× as the POS class-n-gram order goes 2 → 3 → 4. F569's soft bigram frame becomes a genuinely discriminating grammar at trigram/4-gram order — the form layer now rejects bad word order decisively, still from the **discarded function-word signal** (F569), still a **separate layer** (F311). Honest tradeoff: order-4 over-rejects real sentences (50% real pass = n-gram sparsity); **trigram is the sweet spot**, and high-order full coverage needs backoff/smoothing (the standard class-n-gram fix).

**The ceiling is honest and structural.** A bounded order-k frame cannot enforce a dependency longer than k, and ~52% of real subject→verb dependencies span past a 4-gram window. That long-range structure is the **attention job** (unbounded range) — naming exactly what current-gen LLM moving parts buy that a class-n-gram does not. The separated RBS-LM gets **local** grammar cheaply; **long-range agreement is SS-5** (attention vs the separated architecture). Favored not privileged (F398); held open (F394).
