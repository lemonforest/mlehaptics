## §1 The test, stated precisely (an INTRINSIC scalar read, not an IMPOSED label)

F227 measured the **render-side complement** to the F221/F224 addressing-null by **imposing** a depth-band LABEL on the F166 loop's scoring step and asking whether the emitted render separated the three bands beyond a matched-random-label floor — it did not (a render-null on the depth axis). F229 keeps that loop + floor machinery but makes the **load-bearing swap**: instead of imposing a label, it **reads an intrinsic context-fiber SCALAR** (computed BEFORE generation, frozen — anti-reverse-fit), and tests whether that scalar tracks the render's **variability** across **naturally-varying contexts at matched n_candidates**. The question is not "do 3 bands separate" but "does the conversation's own hidden state set the render's hotness?"

- **The dependent variable (render-variability meter):** `T_eff` — the **effective sampling temperature needed to reproduce the emission**. At each context, the F166 loop emits from `softmax(sims/T_op)` over the FIXED candidate set; the empirical emitted-token entropy is measured (over `n_draw=32` seeded draws), and `T_eff` is the temperature whose `softmax(sims/T)` entropy MATCHES it (bisection on T; H(T) is monotone in T). This is the **inverse** of the F128 `softmax(sims/T)` map. **Residualized on n_candidates** — MANDATORY, not optional: emitted entropy ↔ n_cand at the smoke's r ≈ 0.9991, and this run measures |Spearman|(emitted_H, n_cand) = **0.7064** and |Spearman|(T_eff, n_cand) = **0.2907**, so an un-residualized meter would spuriously "track" any feature correlated with candidate-count.

- **The context-fiber scalar (the independent variable; two DEFENSIBLE candidates, pre-registered):**
  - **F-A (primary):** bigram-parity Klein-4 **sector-occupancy entropy** of the window — route each ordered context bigram to a Klein-4 = Z₂×Z₂ sector by word-order parity (`sector = 2·forward_present + reverse_present`, the descriptor's `sector_routing='bigram_parity'` structural routing, F132/F168/F225), read the Shannon entropy of the 4-bin occupancy. The principled "intensity/variability of the local relational state" proxy — the Klein-4 sector-occupancy SPREAD the directive names.
  - **F-B (cross-check):** context **self-similarity spread** — std of pairwise `klein4_similarity` among the window-token vectors (low spread = lexically-tight/"cool" register; high = diverse/"hot").
  - Both are smoke-confirmed near-zero on the n_cand confound (this run: F-A ↔ n_cand = **0.0508**, F-B ↔ n_cand = **0.0182**) — clean candidates, not n_cand proxies.

- **The form ladder (the deliverable):** climb **scalar → bi-chiral → harmonic** and report the **minimal sufficient form** whose added explained render-variability clears its OWN matched-DoF floor. ## §4 Why the two apparent "clears" are NOT promoted (the conservative, monotone, matched-DoF guard)

Two cells in §3 read "yes" but neither survives scrutiny — and the design was built to catch exactly this:

**(a) F-B clears but F-A (the primary) does not, and F-B does not survive re-seeding.** F-B's single-seed |Spearman| = 0.1158 clears the seed-42 floor, but across three independent context-sample seeds the *primary* meter F-A clears **0/3** and F-B's own values wander near a wandering floor (0.049–0.116 vs floors 0.046–0.080). This is the textbook **seed-fragile near-floor blip**, not a stable fiber↔variability link. The directive names F-A the primary precisely so a robustness-failing cross-check cannot be elevated; the **robustness gate** formalizes it (majority-of-seeds required), and the verdict keys on the **robust** F-A clear, which is false.

**(b) The harmonic ΔR² = 0.0195 clears even a cardinality-matched floor — but is NOT promoted, by the monotone ladder.** This is the **FOURTH RISK** the directive flags ("more DoF always explains more"). Two guards apply:
1. **Matched-DoF floor (fixed mid-run):** the window co-occurrence spectrum is *low-cardinality* (`mode_count ∈ {2,3,4}`; `spectral_gap` takes a handful of discrete window-shape values). A first cut used a **uniform-continuous** random-feature floor (600 distinct values) — not identical-DoF — under which the harmonic spuriously cleared with a wide margin. The floor was corrected to the **permutation of the ACTUAL spectrum features** (identical value-multiset + cardinality + marginal, with the context↔render link destroyed) — the IDENTICAL-DoF control the FOURTH RISK demands. The structured ΔR² (0.0195) does still exceed this matched-DoF p90 (0.0097), so a *tiny* real-but-fragile association survives permutation.
2. **Monotone gate (the decisive one):** the minimal-sufficient-FORM criterion requires the **scalar foothold first** — the scalar establishes the basic fiber↔variability link; the bi-chiral and harmonic only refine its FORM. With the robust scalar **null**, a 0.02-ΔR² higher-rung effect over a base that includes a non-clearing scalar is **not** "what temperature is" — it is the residual a higher-DoF feature always scrapes up. So it is reported as the NULL **with the fragile higher-rung noted honestly**, never promoted to "temperature is a harmonic overtone." Declaring HARMONIC here would have been leaning the result against the pre-stated null.

The honest reading: the primary fiber↔render-variability link is **absent and robustly so**; the form-ladder sub-question (scalar vs bi-chiral vs harmonic) is **not even reachable** because there is no scalar foothold to build on.

## §7 One-paragraph plain statement

"Temperature" is the knob that decides how adventurous an LLM is — and the question was whether that knob is secretly *read off the conversation* (a real property of the text's hidden state) or just a free dial someone sets. On this small Klein-4 instrument over the KJV New Testament, the answer is the un-flashy one: it is a free dial. A defensible, principled measure of "how varied / intense the local relational state is" (the bigram-parity Klein-4 sector-occupancy entropy of the context, computed *before* the model writes anything) does **not** reliably predict how much variability the model's emission actually needed — once you remove the one thing that *does* predict it (how many words were even legal next, the n_candidates confound), the correlation collapses to the noise floor and stays there across re-samples (0 of 3 seeds clear). A higher-dimensional "fiber spectrum" feature scrapes up a tiny bit of leftover variance, but that is exactly what extra knobs always do; with no basic link underneath it, calling it "the real shape of temperature" would be fooling ourselves — so the finding refuses to. The honest verdict, matching what a quick 20k-token preview already hinted: on this testbed temperature is exogenous, the chiral structure the user expected to find is not (yet) here, and the magic number F228 flagged is still un-sourced. The measurement is bit-exact and the chiral hypothesis was given a real, fair, pre-registered chance to show up; the framework adds only the structural form-reading, and any affect/register reading belongs to the peer-reviewed literature, flagged to verify before asserting.
