## Why this, not three aligned text versions

The three ORIGINAL scripts (hieroglyphic + demotic + Greek) render one decree, and are a distance-3 code — F1177 measured that principle on a 3-parallel-version proxy. Aligned trilingual *text* is hard to source cleanly. But the Rosetta's *decisive* operand-EC — the one that actually happened — is **cross-NAME**, not merely cross-version, and it is fully attestable: a hieroglyphic **sound value** (the operand) was recovered because the **same sound recurs across many royal cartouches**, and Champollion cross-checked the shared letters between **Ptolemy** (from the Rosetta) and **Cleopatra** (from the Philae obelisk). His own statement is the operand-EC verbatim: *"if the characters which are similar in these two names express the same sound in each cartouche, their purely phonetic character is at once made clear"* — cross-name consistency = the **responsion**.
