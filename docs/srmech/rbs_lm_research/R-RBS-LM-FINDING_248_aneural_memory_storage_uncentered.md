## §1 The uncentered storage landscape (28 substrates, 6 non-privileged media)

Held without ranking. The six distinct storage media (the honest "no single store" reading — opus's, which the merge kept over sonnet's collapse):
1. **Absorbed/internalized chemical** — *Physarum* takes up + retains the repellent (sodium) as "circulating memory" (Boussard et al. 2019, *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B* 374:20180368).
2. **Externalized / internal physical structure** — *Physarum* extracellular slime-trail (Reid et al. 2012, *PNAS* 109:17490, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1215037109) + tube-diameter hierarchy; fungal mycelial cord anisotropy; centriole template geometry.
3. **Transient ionic / membrane-potential** — Venus flytrap cytosolic Ca²⁺ leaky-integrator "count to 2" (Böhm/Hedrich 2016, *Curr. Biol.* 26:286); *Bacillus* biofilm K⁺ depolarization waves (Prindle et al. 2015) + imprinted hyperpolarization.
4. **Epigenetic / chromatin** — plant vernalization FLC/Polycomb cellular memory (Bastow et al. 2004); H3K4me3 stress-priming; innate "trained immunity."
5. **Sequence / clonal** — CRISPR-Cas spacer arrays (explicit genomic memory); adaptive-immune memory B/T-cell V-region selection.
6. **Protein-conformational / prion** — CPEB/Orb2 functional prion (Si 2003); yeast Sup35 [PSI⁺].

Plus the **habituation/learning behavioral cases** that motivated the dive: *Physarum* habituation (Boisseau, Vogel & Dussutour 2016, *Proc. R. Soc. B* 283:20160446, OA PMC4855389) + periodic-event anticipation (Saigusa, Tero, Nakagaki & Kuramoto 2008, *PRL* 100:018101); *Cassiopea* sleep-like state (Nath et al. 2017, *Curr. Biol.* 27:2984); box-jelly associative learning **in a rhopalial ganglion with no central brain** (Bielecki et al. 2023, *Curr. Biol.* 33:4150 — the *neural-but-brainless* contrast case); *Stentor* avoidance hierarchy (Rajan & Marshall 2025) — flagged by its own authors as **not** habituation/conditioning. **Boundary/floor cases** (persistent trace undemonstrated): Trichoplax (Placozoa), sponge (Porifera), ctenophore.

**The decisive uncentered point:** the neuron does not sit at the center because **neural and aneural stores INTERLEAVE rather than nest** — a brainless slime mould habituates with *no* neurons, while a box jelly does associative learning in a tiny ganglion with *no* brain. "Has a brain," "has neurons," and "can store" are three separable axes. The settling-op (sculpt-attractor / relax-to-it) is the **lens** that lets you *read* all six media as one abstract operation — but it is the cited authors' own language for only a few rows (Gardner 2000 bistable circuits; Si 2003 prion; Biswas/Levin transcriptional-network learning) and is *our* mapping for the rest. Marked as lens, not mechanism.
