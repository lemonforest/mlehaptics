# R-RBS-LM Finding 600 (binding-order-aware composition in practice — the non-associative octonion bind is a PARSE/ORDER carrier, paid and USED only across the handedness seam) — **Pushed to the next rung after F597: what does binding-order-aware RBS-LM composition look like in practice? The practical point: a STANDARD HDC bind (`hdc.bind`, XOR-like) is associative AND commutative, so a bound record is a BAG — it LOSES grouping/order (bind(a,b,c)=bind(c,b,a)), and to encode order/parse you must add an explicit Class-M PERMUTE. But the OCTONION bind (carrying both handednesses, F597) is NON-ASSOCIATIVE, so (a∘b)∘c ≠ a∘(b∘c) — the bind ITSELF carries the bracketing/parse, for free. And F597's localisation holds: WITHIN one handed unit the octonion bind is still associative (a free order-less bag, like XOR), so the structural carry is paid ONLY at the handedness seam. Demonstrated with the genuine octonion (`cayley_dickson`): the associativity defect ‖(a∘b)∘c − a∘(b∘c)‖² is EXACTLY 0 within a handed unit (CASE A, factors in e1,e2,e3), 12 when factors cross the seam (CASE B, e4/e5 present), 32 when all three straddle (CASE C, e4/e6/e7) — and across the seam the two bracketings are DISTINCT vectors (so unbinding must respect order there). The RBS-LM reading: compose same-hemisphere/same-handed-unit factors order-free (a cheap commutative bag); compose cross-hemisphere (cross-seam) factors so the bracketing of the bind encodes their structural relation (subject-(verb-object) vs (subject-verb)-object) — for free, no permute. The cost (order-dependence) is LOCAL: paid only at LH↔RH crossings, never inside a unit. "Structure where you need it, free bag where you don't." This is the practical face of F597: the non-associativity is not a bug to avoid but a CARRIER of parse structure, localised to the seam.**
