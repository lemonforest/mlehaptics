## The answer (three self-selected modes)
The cascade reads its own margin and picks its effort — **it is self-aware about when to think harder:**
1. **FAST (k=1, margin ≥ θ).** Strong/recent knowledge is above the floor; one cue fetches it — automatic, no effort. (The margin says "I have this.")
2. **THOUGHT(k) — "stop and think" (k=1 low → escalate cues).** A faded/deeper item: one cue's margin is low (below θ but not pure noise), so the cascade **detects "it's in there, reach deeper"** and escalates — superpose k cues (√k lift, F976) until the margin clears. This IS pulling from the deeper reservoir, self-triggered. (Recovered at N=300 with k=3.)
3. **STOP — honest limit (k_max still low).** When even the deepest reach (k_max) stays below θ, the cascade reports **low confidence** rather than emitting confidently — the anti-hallucination honest-STOP (F934). Note (honest): at N=500/700 the target was *still* top-1, but the margin (0.017) was below the confident-threshold, so the cascade correctly says "I'm not sure" even when technically right — *it reports its confidence, not a guess.*

## Why this matters (the architecture)
- **The margin is the metacognitive signal.** The same collapse-margin that is the coherence/anti-hallucination readout (F943/F945) is *also* the "do I need to think harder?" trigger. Low margin at k=1 = "I don't have this from one cue" = escalate. This gives the recall **effort-adaptive metacognition for free** — no separate confidence model; the readout already carries it.
- **Two escalation axes.** "Think harder" can escalate **context-width** (`k_ctx`, F961 — more of the present) *or* **cue-count** (`k_cues`, F976 — more time-perspectives on the past). Both are triggered by the same low-margin signal; the cascade can spend either kind of effort to raise the target above the floor.
- **Bounded by the invariant rate (F976).** Escalation buys **altitude** (√k), not a lower forgetting-rate — so the STOP mode is *real*: past a certain depth, even max effort can't recover what the rung-rate erased. The cascade knows the difference between "reach deeper" (recoverable, escalate) and "genuinely lost" (honest-STOP) — because the margin tells it whether more cues are *helping* (rising) or *maxed* (flat below θ).
