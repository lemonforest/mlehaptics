## Verdict
**A pronoun is a cheap surface pointer bound to a held referent** — discourse-scale binding (Class M). Establish the referent once (the full noun), re-reference it cheaply by a pointer, resolve anaphora by following the pointer back to the held referent. The referent is the held etak invariant (the canoe); the pronoun is the cheap mark pointing back — **maintaining reference across a discourse is discourse-scale etak.**

**English's pronouns ARE its spatial loci** (the F637 echo): English binds referent → a pronoun-token; ASL binds referent → a spatial locus — the same Class-M bind, two boards (token stream vs signing space). A Deaf signer's loci and a hearing speaker's pronouns are the same operation (accessibility-native, F611). **Agreement** (she/he/it/they) is a coordinate-match on the referent's features (F633) keeping the pointer bound to the right referent; **case** (he/him/whom) is a small stored paradigm (F629). It is **self-similar** with the loci (F637), the bind (Class M / F638), and the etak invariant (F635): one mechanism — a held invariant + a pointer/bind — recursing through scale.
