## §3 Why I (cyclic) is weak at K-12

Math I-score: 0.029 (1 of 35 tokens: "hour")

Reasons for the I-weakness:
1. **K-12 algebra doesn't emphasize modular arithmetic** — clock
   arithmetic, modular residues, periodic groups appear later in
   number theory courses (college-level).
2. **I-vocabulary is distributed across subjects** — "season" in
   cooking, "period" in science, "round" in games. The cyclic
   substrate is delivered in scattered context, not concentrated.
3. **The 35-token I signature may be too generic** — "month",
   "week", "calendar", "hour" appear everywhere but are not always
   delivering the I-class semantically.

The I-component IS in the math irrep operationally (modular
arithmetic IS class I), but K-12 educational delivery is
J-heavy + I-light. Advanced number theory or cryptography
materials would show I-dominance.

**Highest I overall: science (0.057, "period" + "round")** — astronomy
periodically references orbital cycles. Geography, history, scouting
also touch I but very weakly.
