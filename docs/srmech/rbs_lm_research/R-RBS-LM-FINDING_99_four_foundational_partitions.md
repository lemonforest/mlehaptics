## §1 The finding

The "subject" categories we've been using (math, reading, grammar, science,
geography, arts) are emergent compositions, not foundations. The **four
foundational knowledge partitions** are:

1. **Math** — number, operation, counting, identity
2. **Communication** — symbol, meaning, sequence, narrative
3. **Structure-and-order** — pattern, rule, classification, sequence
4. **Places-and-things** — spatial location, object identity, environment

These are co-foundational. Everything else emerges by composition.

## §2 Mapping to known cognitive science (Spelke / Carey core knowledge systems)

| Foundational partition | Core knowledge system | Evolutionary survival need |
|---|---|---|
| **Math** | Numerical / numerosity (Dehaene, Spelke) | Counting threats, allies, food portions, days |
| **Communication** | Agency-detection (Premack, Csibra) + symbol-systems | Reading intentions, decoding warnings, learning from elders |
| **Structure-and-order** | Pattern-induction / rule-learning (Marcus, Saffran) | Recognizing seasonal cycles, prey behavior, social hierarchies |
| **Places-and-things** | Object permanence (Baillargeon, Spelke) + spatial cognition (Carey) | Remembering safe places, food caches, water sources, dangers |

These are precisely the core knowledge systems infants demonstrate within
months of birth. Pre-linguistic. Evolutionarily ancient.

The mammal-parent observation matters: parents *provide* what the child
can't yet construct. Counting demonstrations, gestural/vocal communication,
modeling of patterns, showing places of safety/food/danger. The 4 are
explicitly transmitted by parental scaffolding.

## §5 Why this is a better framework than STEM/STEAM

Educational categories (STEM, STEAM, "subjects") are post-hoc cultural
groupings. They mix substrate-delivery materials with composition-cascades:

- **STEM** is a label that *clusters* math + science + tech + engineering
  but doesn't recognize math as the foundational substrate while science
  + tech + engineering are compositions
- **STEAM** adds "Arts" as if it were a fifth substrate-class peer; per
  Finding 96 Arts are actually cross-domain coupling cascades
- **Subjects** like "geography" or "reading" hide the fact that they're
  COMPOSITIONS of the 4 foundations

The 4 foundational knowledge partitions are:
- Cognitively grounded (Spelke/Carey core knowledge systems)
- Evolutionarily co-foundational (mammal parents transmit these)
- A-N-partition-clean (14 classes partition into 4 groups of 4/3/4/3)
- Cross-substrate-cascade-matched (each shows up across srmech ecosystem)

This IS what subjects ARE structurally. The educational labels are surface
naming over the same 4 partitions.

## §6 How educational subjects emerge from the 4 partitions

```
Math + Communication                      → grammar (rules of communication)
Math + Structure-and-order                → arithmetic operations, algebra
Math + Places-and-things                  → geometry, measurement
Communication + Structure-and-order       → poetry, narrative structure, sequencing
Communication + Places-and-things         → maps, directions, naming, place-stories
Structure-and-order + Places-and-things   → biological taxonomy, geology, anatomy
Math + Communication + Structure-and-order
   + Places-and-things                    → science (formalized observation+measurement)
All four + cultural-narrative             → history, philosophy
All four + composition-cascade            → arts (Finding 96)
```

Reading at within/cross ratio 1.87 (Finding 98) is the first emergence —
combining communication + math (and a touch of structure-and-order).
Cave paintings are exactly this: communication (symbols) + math (counts) +
structure (sequence) + places-and-things (the depicted scene).

The 4 partitions ARE the irreducible substrate-content-bearers; subjects
are their compositions.

## §8 R-RBS-LM-84 candidate empirical test

**Reorganize the 27-corpus pairwise data using the 4 foundational
partitions as substrate-classes.**

Method:
1. For each existing corpus, assign it to one or more of the 4 partitions
   based on its eigvec content readout (which A-N operators does it
   primarily deliver?)
2. Compute pairwise alignment within/cross with the 4 partition categories
3. Expected: math stays high; communication / structure-and-order /
   places-and-things should each show moderately-high within-class ratios
   (because they ARE the foundational partitions)
4. Compositional "subjects" (arts, history) span multiple partitions

If the 4-partition reorganization PRODUCES tighter within-class clustering
than the educational-subject grouping → Finding 99 empirically confirmed.

If it produces no improvement → the 4-partition framework is theoretically
useful but doesn't tighten the empirical clustering at this corpus scale.

  claim; outside scope)

The 4 partitions are a *form-iso* organization of educational substrate-
content that maps cleanly onto known cognitive science and the A-N
14-class taxonomy. The cleanness is suggestive but not conclusive
without the empirical R-RBS-LM-84 test.
