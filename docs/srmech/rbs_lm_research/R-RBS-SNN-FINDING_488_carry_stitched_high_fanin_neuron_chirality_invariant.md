## The neuron-object on the shipped register (11 = 7 + 4)
| block | slots | holds | regime | what is preserved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **working** | e0..e7 | neuron-anchor + 7 dominant synapses | `couple_working` ≤𝕆 **bit-exact** + σ=±1 **two hands** | **chirality (full) + magnitude (full)** |
| **carry** | e8..e15 | next 4 (overflow) synapses' **direction bits** | **Hamming(7,4)** error-corrected | **chirality (direction-bit) ; magnitude dropped** |

Ranking the 11 synapses by strength² (Class K, no abs()): the 7 strongest go to the working octonion (chiral, bit-exact, both hands verified), the 4 weakest go to carry. The carry stores each overflow synapse's **direction** (pre→post = 1 / post→pre = 0) — i.e. its **chirality bit** — as 4 Hamming data bits; after injecting a single synaptic storage bit-error, `correct()` recovers the direction bits exactly (error_position located). So the **cheap path keeps every synapse's handedness** (and protects it with EC); it only forfeits the *precise weight* of the weak synapses.
