## §1 What I was trying to break

The bridge H177 needs at the molecular band is the **Vester–Ulbricht hypothesis** (Ulbricht 1959; Vester): that biological homochirality *descends from* weak-force parity violation, via the **parity-violating energy difference (PVED)** between enantiomers — weak neutral currents (Z⁰) make one enantiomer infinitesimally more stable, and that tiny deterministic bias, amplified, fixes life's L/D choice. If the cosmic handedness *sets the sign* of bio-chirality, the lifeless-cosmos driver and the biological driver are ONE (H177 fails-to-break here).

**The falsifier (second actor):** if bio-homochirality's sign is set **independently** of the weak-force sign — a **frozen accident**: chance symmetry-breaking + autocatalytic amplification (Frank 1953; the Soai reaction) — then bio-chirality has its OWN origin = a second actor (partial falsification of H177).

## §3 The verified evidence-state (MPM; arXiv IDs; what I could / could not verify)

### §3.1 The PVED / Vester–Ulbricht camp (the bridge)

- **Vester & Ulbricht 1959** — the original proposal that fundamental (β-decay) chirality biases biomolecules. *Verified as attribution* via multiple secondary sources (the homochirality-review literature uniformly credits Ulbricht 1959; Yamagata 1966 for the explicit PVED-selects-L form). **Not PDF-verified at primary source** (1959 vintage; flagged — textbook/review attribution chain per `[[feedback_paywalled_doi_cannot_be_attested]]`).
- **Mason & Tranter 1986** (*J. Theor. Biol.* 119:467, "Parity-violating energy differences and the origin of biomolecular homochirality"). The foundational ab-initio PVED calculation line. *Verified via ADS + the review literature* (abstract-level; the J. Theor. Biol. full text is paywalled — flagged abstract-only). **Result as reported:** PVED ≈ **1×10⁻¹⁷ kT** for α-amino acids, with **L-amino acids the lower-energy (more stable)** enantiomer for the series glycine/alanine/valine/serine/aspartate and for α-helix / β-sheet peptide fragments. *On its face this is the bridge: the calc says L is favored.*
- **The "conformation problem" (the first crack).** Later work (e.g. the *Origins Life Evol. Biosph.* "conformation problem" line) finds the PVED **sign is conformationally hypersensitive**: it is *decisively negative* (L favored) for **gas-phase** alanine, but the **sign in solution is not determinable** — it flips with conformation. So even the *sign-prediction* — the thing H177 needs — is not robust outside idealized gas phase.
- **Martínez-Gil, Bargueño & Miret-Artés 2025**, arXiv:**2502.12907**, "On the role of true and false chirality in producing parity violating energy differences." **Verified by direct WebFetch of the arXiv HTML.** Confirms the *mechanism constraint* (only a **truly chiral** influence — e.g. Z⁰ weak neutral currents — can produce a PVED; a *falsely* chiral influence cannot). **But explicitly:** "this PVED **has not yet been detected**"; the PVED→bio causation is presented as **"an unproven hypothesis rather than established science,"** requiring *both* PVED generation *and* subsequent autocatalytic amplification. So the most recent careful treatment frames the bridge as **open, not established**.

**Magnitude / dynamical-sufficiency verdict:** ~10⁻¹⁷ kT (some later calcs ~10² larger → ~10⁻¹⁵ kT scale, still "too little to significantly contribute to prebiotic evolution"). The consensus in the review literature is that the PVED is **negligibly small** and **dynamically insufficient on its own** — it *must* be rescued by autocatalytic amplification, which (next) is exactly the mechanism that *also* works from a random seed with no weak-force input.

### §3.2 The frozen-accident / chance camp (the second actor)

- **Frank 1953** — autocatalytic model: an enantiomer catalyzes its own production and suppresses the other; an arbitrarily small initial ee is amplified to homochirality. *No deterministic chiral force required.* (Attribution verified via the autocatalysis review literature; primary is 1953 vintage — flagged.)
- **Soai et al. 1995** (*Nature* 378:767) — the *experimental* realization: asymmetric autocatalysis with amplification of ee (diisopropylzinc + pyrimidine-5-carbaldehyde). **Verified as attribution** (Nature 378:767; widely cited). **Crucially:** started from **achiral reactants with no chiral additive**, the reaction yields high ee — i.e., chiral symmetry breaks **spontaneously**.
- **Saito, Sugimori & Hyuga 2006**, arXiv:**cond-mat/0612385**, "Stochastic Approach to Enantiomeric Excess Amplification and Chiral Symmetry Breaking." **Verified by direct WebFetch of the arXiv abstract.** With strong nonlinear autocatalysis from an **achiral (symmetric) initial state**, the final chirality probability distribution has a **double-peak (bimodal) structure** — **the system ends left or right with equal probability**, set by *random fluctuations*, **not** by any deterministic bias. (Companion: Saito & Hyuga arXiv:cond-mat/0612381, same group, rate-equation treatment — verified title/authors; the PVED-vs-noise discussion is in the full text, flagged not-extracted.)
- **The Soai stochastic signature (the crux):** repeated Soai runs from achiral starting material give a **near-bimodal L/R distribution** ("coin tossing" normal-distribution formalism for the initial step). **The sign of the outcome is random — equally L or R — and is *not* biased toward the weak-force handedness.** This is the experimental fingerprint of an **independent-sign origin**.

### §3.3 The authoritative open-access review (the field's own verdict)

- **Blackmond 2010**, *Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology*, "The Origin of Biological Homochirality" (open access, PMC2857173). **Verified by direct WebFetch.** States plainly: a **"relationship between biological homochirality and parity violation is not yet supported by experimental findings"**; the PVED is **"very, very small"**; **"the question is not yet settled."** Frames Frank/Soai chance-amplification as the live alternative, and in stochastic scenarios (e.g. Viedma crystallization) the sign emerges **"sometimes to the left and sometimes to the right,"** with equal probability. **The review's own verdict is: OPEN.**
