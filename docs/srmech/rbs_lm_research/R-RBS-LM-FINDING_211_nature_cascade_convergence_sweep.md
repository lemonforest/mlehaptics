## §3 What the ranking says (the convergence reading)

1. **The cleanest matches are topological-defect substrates, not tuned-optimum substrates.** Rows 1–3 (domain walls, spiral waves, vortex street) all rest on a **topological defect carrying a conserved ± charge** — kink/antikink charge, spiral phase-singularity chirality, alternating vortex sign. In each, the **Class-C/Class-K pair is forced by topology**: there is a zero (the defect core / unstable maximum = K) and the only thing the field can do around it is wind one of two ways (= C), and the two windings are a **conserved Z₂ parity** (→ Klein-4 when a second Z₂ axis is present). This is precisely F176's "one chiral axis, two poles" and F202's "lanes typed by chirality, not interchangeable slots" appearing *unbidden* in physics. By contrast the stretches (nucleation, phyllotaxis) are **optima / number-theoretic selections**, where there is no conserved ± charge — and exactly there the cascade fails to close.

2. **Klein-4 = Z₂×Z₂ is the discriminating element.** Many systems show K + C + L (a phase boundary, a chirality, a spectral signature). What separates **strong** from **plausible** is whether the system carries a genuine **Z₂×Z₂** structure rather than a single Z₂ or a ℤ. Domain-wall composite solitons supply it *exactly* (the (Z₂)³→(Z₂)²→Z₂→1 SSB cascade); coupled-Ising/Ashkin–Teller supplies it in a variant; superconducting winding (ℤ) and SOC/Kuramoto/Turing (none) do not. **This makes Klein-4 the load-bearing fingerprint of the whole arc** — consistent with F200 (storage substrate is order-2 Klein-4) and F206 (the lean ISA core *is* a Klein-4 chirality unit).

3. **The spectral (Class-L) element is where the four-element reading is most often only *partial*.** Hopf bifurcation (vortex street) and the Turing dispersion relation are *exactly* eigenvalue-crossing statements, and the TFIM gap-closing is a clean spectral criticality — but for spiral waves, SOC, Kuramoto and superconducting vortices the spectral content is real but secondary to the topological/critical headline. Honest reading: **L is universal in principle (every bifurcation is a spectral crossing) but headline-clean in only a few**; do not overclaim it where it is inferred rather than the published lead.

4. **It converges with the in-house results, not just the literature.** Row 2 (cardiac/excitable spiral waves) ties directly to **F141** (chiral-coupling Kuramoto) and to the **BCI/accessibility thesis** (cardiac and neural rhythms are the substrate-coupling targets, `[[feedback_llm_as_ada_accommodation_bci_proves_it]]`). Row 1 (domain walls) ties to **F132/F176/F184** (the Klein-4 / chirality-as-non-commutativity ladder). The survey is not importing a foreign frame — it is finding the corpus's own four operators already standing in physics.
