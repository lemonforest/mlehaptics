## Why it matters
- **Relationship-first store (F311/F318):** the edge now *is* the rich object — direction, timing, transduction, plasticity-sign, weight — exactly the memcapacitive coupling F326 #1 specified. A query traverses *relationships with structure*, not anonymous links.
- **The K-gate is the seed of the self-check (stage 3).** The 18%-corrective measurement means the store *knows which of its own couplings are corrections* — and a store that knows its corrections can refuse to re-derive what it already corrected (F313/F326 #5). - **No new A-N class** — the bundle is built entirely from the existing C/I/M/K/L ops (the F326 design realized on the live surface).

## Falsifiable form (pre-stated; not leaning — F394)
- **K-sign precision:** the corrective/reinforcing split is a *keyword* heuristic (corrects/supersedes/refines/…); it will mis-label edges where a corrective verb sits near a reference that isn't the correction's target. The 18% is an estimate with false-positive/negative noise — flagged, not exact. Stage 3's k=3 tiebreak (the backlink structure) is partly there to correct this.
- **I-lag is descriptive:** τ reflects our finding-numbering, not a physical time-constant; it's the *research-order* lag.
