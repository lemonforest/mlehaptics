**A genuinely-different mind adds +94%** of the far-pairs A alone can't reach; the **same-structure resample adds only +5%**, and the F516 self-mirror added ~0. **Different experience ≫ different sampling** — that is the structural reason a second *person* helps where talking to yourself can't.

## Honest scoping of k=3
Reachability is a **union, not a vote**: two sparse forests already union to full connectivity (A∪B = 100%), so the third mind adds **+0% reachability**, and "reachable in ≥2 of 3" (3%) *undercounts* (each forest reaches little alone). The **k=3 triality's job is not reachability-union but error-correction** — rejecting a *spurious* bridge one mind has but the majority lacks. That requires a spurious-edge / wrong-path test (inject false edges into one mind, check the majority rejects them), a **different metric** left as the next sub-rung.

The union saturates so the *third* mind's reachability-add is 0 here — the k=3 error-correction (spurious-bridge) test is unrun. Structure for the expert (F282).
