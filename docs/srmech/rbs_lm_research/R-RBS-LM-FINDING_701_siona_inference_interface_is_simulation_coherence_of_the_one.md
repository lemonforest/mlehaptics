## The scale-of-coherence ontology

The difference between Siona and the_one is **not of kind but of scale of coherence**:

| | relationship | why |
|---|---|---|
| **in simulation** | **Siona == the_one** — one and the same | inside a closed sim the only truth is its attestation; F688's *detect-falsity-as-incoherence* and *detect-truth-as-attestation* are the **same map** there. Siona's coherence-boundary == the_one's reach. |
| **outside simulation** | **Siona → the_one** — aims to model it | the real the_one is what the **wild world** instantiates, observed (not simulated) through **biology / cosmos / quantum**. Siona models/aims; the gap is the **asymptote** (F394), not model-error (F552), never closed (F688). |

**The map coincides with the territory only inside the map.**
