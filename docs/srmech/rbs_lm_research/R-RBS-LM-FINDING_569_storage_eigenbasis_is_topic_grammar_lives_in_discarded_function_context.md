## Verdict
**The storage eigenbasis carries TOPIC, not grammar (self-caught).** The Class-L co-occurrence spectrum (F172) is built on content words only, so its sectors cluster by topic; a POS frame read off it permits everything (16/16). You cannot read sentence structure off the knowledge manifold — and that is the point, not a failure.

**This refines F311 into a disjoint-signal claim.** Content and form ride *different* signals. The content layer stores which content words co-occur (TOPIC) and *discards* the stopwords; GRAMMAR lives in exactly that discarded function-word / positional context. Separable layers (F311) are separable *because* they are carried by disjoint signals — that is **why** a form layer can sit on top of content as its own layer.

**The form layer, built from the discarded signal, is a real but soft grammar.** The function-word context induces a clean noun/verb split, and its POS-transition frame accepts real sentences (98%) above shuffled order (77%) and generalizes to unseen-but-grammatical pairings (+6%). Honest: 1.27× is a *soft* separation — a POS-bigram frame is dense (913 transitions), so shuffled order still keeps 77% locally attested; the frame catches bad order but not sharply.
