## §1 The two orderings encode two readings (do not conflate — F127)
- **`1:3:7:3` (canonical, CLAUDE.md §1) — the Hurwitz-climb reading.** `1:3:7` is naturally read as the imaginary dimensions of the division-algebra ladder — **ℂ(1) : ℍ(3) : 𝕆(7)** — with **B/H/N the appended meta-3** (the projection-enablers, R30). Here **A is the climb-seed** (the lone "1" that grounds the 1:3:7 ladder).
- **`4:3:7` / `4:3:(4:3)` (F121/F124/F127) — the operational/generative reading.** A,B,H,N are grouped as the **operational-4 = the time-quaternion** (F187), which *generates* the 3:7 harmonically. Here **A is the quaternion-scalar** (the real part), and **B/H/N are its 3 imaginary parts**.

## §2 The real insight your question surfaces — A's dual (triple) role
A is the **most role-ambiguous operator** in the partition. Depending on the reading it is:
- the **ℝ/ground anchor** ("every cascade begins here" — content-addressing),
- the **climb-seed** (the "1" of the 1:3:7 Hurwitz ladder), or
- the **ℍ-quaternion scalar** (the real part of the operational-4 = time, with B/H/N the 3 imaginary).

That is exactly **why A sits alone in one ordering and grouped with B/H/N in another** — it is not an inconsistency to fix; it is A wearing the role the reading assigns. The fact that you *can* sensibly move A and B/H/N around the notation is the signature of this role-ambiguity.

## §3 What `3:1:3:7` actually is
`3:1:3:7` = `(3:1):3:7` = **the operational time-quaternion written imaginary-first** (B/H/N : A) + the substrate `3:7` (I/C/J : heptad). It is a **valid expression of the generative reading** — but:
1. It **scrambles the `1:3:7` Hurwitz climb** that the canonical `1:3:7:3` deliberately encodes, so it should **not replace** the canonical.
2. **Imaginary-first (`3:1`) is unconventional** — quaternions are written real-first (`1:3`, i.e. a + bi+cj+dk). Use `(1:3):3:7` unless there is a structural reason to lead with the operating/imaginary part (B/H/N) over the anchor (A).

## §4 Recommendation (per the F127 naming discipline)
**Keep both, named — the ordering signals which reading you are in:**
- `1:3:7:3` → the **Hurwitz-climb / substrate-structure** reading (canonical; A = seed).
- `(1:3):3:7` (or simply `4:3:7`) → the **operational / time-generative** reading (A = quaternion-scalar, grouped with B/H/N as the time-4, generator-first).

Do **not** merge them into `3:1:3:7` — that conflates the two readings F127 keeps deliberately separate, and loses the climb. If you specifically want B/H/N to lead, `(3:1):3:7` is the imaginary-first variant of the generative reading — fine as *that* reading, flagged as imaginary-first.
