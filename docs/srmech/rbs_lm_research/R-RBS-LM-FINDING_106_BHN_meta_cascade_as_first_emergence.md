## §3 Empirical signatures already present in the data

From R-RBS-LM-79/80 eigvec content readouts:

### Reading ↔ B (TLV-framing)

McGuffey eigvec content per grade shows narrative DECODE patterns:
- Primer: "lesson illustration not can two review will" — instructional framing
- Grade 6: "his he him all not which life upon one when own most face who"
  — character + narrative

The READING ACT itself IS TLV-decoding: type (noun? verb?) + length (word
boundary, sentence end) + value (which specific token). Reading materials
deliver this decoding pattern as substrate-content.

### Grammar ↔ H (self-introspection)

Kittredge eigvec content: "he you his will which not if him **shall clause**
may we" — note "clause" — grammar describing language at the structural level.

Kirkham: "you which will not **verb** may if he when **what** your following"
— note "verb" and meta-question "what". Grammar materials describe the
introspective operation of language reflecting on language.

Strunk: "not he his him do **word** while did who **means** than if" — note
"word" and "means". Composition instruction IS introspection on the meaning
of language elements.

### Science ↔ N (rational-approximation)

Science corpora deliver measurement vocabulary that emphasizes approximation:
- Astronomy Young Folks: "**stars star** which **two one all sun** we heavens
  so" — celestial counts and ratios
- Star-land: "we which **sun see our** not can earth so when **if find**" —
  observation with approximate inference
- Child's Health Primer: "you not your if can will **what do how when**" —
  approximate procedural inquiry

Science materials deliver rational-approximation of cosmic phenomena —
Kepler's planetary period ratios, Newtonian approximations, empirical
measurements as small-denominator fractions.

## §6 Storing abstract knowledge as math

The user's framing — "storing abstract knowledge as math" — connects to
the foundational claim that **math is the only substrate-content irrep**
(Finding 97-ADDENDUM).

To store ANY abstract knowledge so it can be transmitted across the
generational boundary (Finding 100), the knowledge must be converted to
math-compatible form. The meta-cascade B/H/N is the conversion mechanism:

- **B framing**: any abstract structure can be encoded as type+length+value
  → math-compatible byte sequence
- **H introspection**: any system can describe itself recursively →
  math-compatible self-referential structure
- **N approximation**: any continuous phenomenon can be approximated by
  rationals → math-compatible numerical representation

This is WHY math has the highest within/cross ratio (5.16): math IS the
final destination of abstract knowledge after meta-cascade projection.
Other subjects (arts, history, philosophy) are HALFWAY through this
projection or are cross-domain compositions over multiple projection-
enablers.
