## The two floors — and the gap between them is INTENT
- **Smallest WHOLE organism with robust operant:** insects/molluscs (*Drosophila*, *Aplysia* ~20k neurons, *Lymnaea*).
- **Most-reduced PREPARATION (the true floor):** a **single neuron — *Aplysia* B51 in culture.** Action = a self-generated plateau potential; reward = a **contingent** dopamine puff; **null = the same puff 40 s later (non-contingent)**; result = the cell becomes more excitable (↓burst threshold, ↑input resistance). The double-dissociation seals it: the *classical* analog moves excitability the **opposite** direction. This is the user's exact trio — do-a-thing→reward, with the null control — reduced to **one cell**.
- **It does NOT reach the aneural cell.** *Physarum*/*Stentor* habituate (F308) but no one has shown — perhaps cannot even define — operant contingency learning in them.

**Why the floor is a neuron and not just a cell (the framework reading):** habituation needs only **one self-modifying channel** (a stimulus→response that dials itself down) — a single aneural cell has that, so **memory is aneural-universal (F308).** **Operant needs more: a distinct ACTION channel, a distinct OUTCOME/evaluation channel, and a detector of the *contingency* between them.** That is a **two-channel partition + a coincidence-detector** — structurally a **k=2 (detect: are action and outcome coupled?) feeding a directed Class-C "this action→that outcome" orientation.** The B51 neuron is the minimal cell that internalizes exactly this: self-generated output + dopamine reward-receptor + molecular coincidence-detection. So **the neuron is the cell that adds INTENT (directed action→outcome) on top of the universal cellular MEMORY.** Memory is what *every* self-running cell does; **intent/operant is the neuron's specialization.** This sharpens §VIII.31.13 / F308: the neuron is a hidden fiber of *memory* — but the neuron's own *specialization* is **contingency-detection (intent)**, the first appearance of Class-C orientation in the learning ladder.

## The null / yoked control — the user intuited the field's decisive instrument
"Do a thing and get a **null**" is the **yoked (non-contingent) control**: the subject gets the *identical* reinforcement sequence but **decoupled from its own action**, so any contingent-vs-yoked difference can only be the **action→outcome coupling itself** — not the reinforcement per se. It is precisely what separates genuine operant from classical/sensitization, and it is **exactly where the small-end claims fail**: Horridge's original yoked design was statistically confounded (Church 1964) until Forman's 1984 redesign; the box-jellyfish "operant" has **no** yoked control; the aneural-cell claims have no contingency at all. **Framework reading:** the yoked control is the **experimenter's own k=2 detector** bolted on to test whether the organism's intent-detection is real — the same "you must add error-correction/contingency-checking by hand" move F308 found for k=1 substrates, here applied to the *experiment*. The user's three contingencies (reward / aversive / null) ARE the minimal operant design.

## Synthesis — the self-running ladder now has two rungs
- **aneural self-running cell** (amoeba, *Physarum*): **memory yes** (habituation, F308), **intent no** (no operant). Storage without orientation.
- **single neuron** (B51): **memory + intent** — the first rung that detects an action→outcome contingency (Class-C orientation + k=2/k=3 detect). The minimal "agent."
- This maps cleanly onto F308's digital reading: a k=1 substrate (binary, *or* the aneural cell) can **store** but has **no native intent** — intent must be *added* (a program for silicon; a neuron's contingency-detector for biology). **Intent (Class C) is the thing you add on top of storage**, and biologically it first appears at the neuron, not the cell.
