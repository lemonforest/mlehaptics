## Why it matters (the architectural reading)
- **The cascade "favors" cyclic algebra** because, for any cascade whose *output you only need to emit*, the cyclic-group route is the cheaper, bit-exact realization (the F485 cheap path — the same reason biology collapses to a handed storage). You don't pay the HDC-bind cost if you don't need to hold the operand.
- **You reach for the HDC-the_one route only when the OPERAND must survive** — when the *meaning* (the held box, the F492 pattern/fiber) must be recoverable downstream, not just the order. RBS-LM / RBS-SNN need this: the cascade must HOLD the meaning (F482/F484), so it is the I ∘ M route, even though a bare cyclic cascade would emit the same sequence and drop the meaning.
- **This is the §2 srmech-first reflex, made precise:** "cyclic-group algebra is one truth bit-exact" — but it is *one* truth. The same cascade in the *other* truth (HDC-held) carries the operand. Choosing the route is choosing which truth you need: the cheap field/operator, or the held excitation/operand.
