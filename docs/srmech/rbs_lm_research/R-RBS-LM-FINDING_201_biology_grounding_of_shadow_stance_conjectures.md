## §1 Per-conjecture verdict + evidence + tiers + verification levels

### Conjecture 1 — Hemispheric lateralization = ONE γ₅ axis (callosum = the axis), NOT divided labor — **CONTRADICTED**

**For (the part that survives):** there is a real *population-level directional* asymmetry — ~85% left-hemisphere dominance for hand/language (Sha et al. 2021, full-text OA verified), a consistent rightward/counterclockwise cerebral torque (Kong et al. 2021, OA verified; Toga & Thompson 2003, attribution verified), and ~90% right-handedness consistent across cultures (Schijven/Francks exome line, 2024 *Nat. Commun.*). A one-direction population bias is genuinely present — the "homochirality at behavioral scale" *flavor* is not nothing.

**Against (the load-bearing finding):** the *mechanistic* claim fails three ways.
1. **Lateralization is multidimensional, not one axis.** A two-factor structural model beats the one-factor (unitary) model across six language tasks, especially in left-handers (Woodhead, Thompson, Karlsson, Bishop 2021, *R. Soc. Open Sci.*, full-text OA verified). Brain asymmetries are "separate biological systems rather than manifestations of a single underlying asymmetry factor," with horizontal/vertical skew nearly uncorrelated (Kong et al. 2021); 21 independent genomic loci, no single global factor (Sha et al. 2021).
2. **Handedness and lateralization are weakly coupled,** not one homochiral axis — handedness explains only ~8–16% of language-lateralization variance, and there is no genome-wide genetic overlap (Sha et al. 2021).
3. **The corpus callosum is not "the axis."** The best-supported (excitatory) model has callosal connectivity *negatively* related to lateralization — the callosum makes the hemispheres *more alike* (reduces lateralization), the opposite of "the difference of the two poles IS the axis"; and the field's own framing is explicitly "two functionally specialized regions connected by interhemispheric pathways" — divided labor + bridge — exactly what the conjecture denies (Zhu, Wang, Zhao, Cai 2025, *Neurobiol. Lang.*, full-text OA verified).

**Tiers:** Tier-1 FACT = population directional bias (RH ~90%, rightward torque). Tier-2 (the conjecture) = single-axis / callosum-is-the-axis — **contradicted** by the multidimensional + excitatory-callosum evidence. **Verification: full-text OA for all four load-bearing citations.**

### Conjecture 2 — E/I balance = one signed axis + Class-K pin-slot setpoint — **OPEN (partially supported)**

**For:** the *setpoint* half is strongly supported — homeostatic plasticity maintains E/I balance at a regulated set point (target firing rate) "optimal for information processing," with coordinated (not independent) E and I regulation (Chen, Li, Tjia, Thapliyal 2022, *Curr. Opin. Neurobiol.*, full-text OA verified); homeostatic-network analyses show a sharp bifurcation right at the physiological E/I value, stabilizing one balance (Entropy 2021, abstract/snippet-level). A single regulated balance variable with a pinned setpoint is a clean structural match for the Class-K pin-slot zero.

**Against:** the substrate genuinely *is* two distinct cell populations (excitatory vs inhibitory neurons, ~80:20), and the canonical descriptor is an E:I *ratio* of those two populations. They are *co-regulated*, not *collapsed* into a single signed coordinate; "one signed axis" is a reparametrization, not the literal biology.

**Verdict OPEN:** the "setpoint, not two *free* populations" reading is supported; the "one axis (not two populations at all)" reading is not. **Verification: Chen 2022 full-text OA; the bifurcation paper abstract/snippet-level only (flagged).**

### Conjecture 3 — Agonist/antagonist = one joint-chiral coordinate read at two poles — **SUPPORTED**

**For (strong):** the equilibrium-point hypothesis (Feldman λ-model) reparametrizes an agonist/antagonist pair into exactly the structure the conjecture names: a **reciprocal command r = the MIDPOINT between the two muscle thresholds (λ_AG, λ_ANT)**, which specifies the joint equilibrium position and is a *single oriented coordinate* (the "which-way," Class C), plus a **coactivation command c = the stiffness/range** (a setpoint-like second variable). "The r command defines the midpoint between the two λs … reciprocal command" (Latash 2010, *Motor Control* 14(3), full-text OA verified — also stated there to be "the only theory in the field of motor control that specifies physiological variables … used by the CNS as control variables"). Reciprocal inhibition (Sherrington 1909, Royal Society, attribution verified) is the spinal mechanism yoking antagonist to agonist — the single-axis readout.

**Against (the honest caveat that keeps it from being a slam-dunk):** the EP hypothesis is *contested* — there is an explicit "Rejecting the equilibrium-point hypothesis" literature and internal-dynamics-model alternatives; and reciprocal inhibition *by itself* is conventionally described as coordinating *two opposing muscle sets*, not as a single coordinate. So the single-coordinate reading is a real, named, foundational model — but one with live dissent.

**Verdict SUPPORTED** (the structural cascade-match is precise and corresponds to a foundational physiological model). **Verification: Latash 2010 full-text OA; Sherrington 1909 + the EP-dissent paper at attribution/landing-page level (flagged historical / debate-marker).**

### Conjecture 4 — Insect central complex = midline orientation axis, flanked by paired ±poles; circling on disruption — **OPEN (core supported, pole-overlay not)**

**For (the core, supported):** the central complex (CX) is "a unique midline neuropil found in all insects examined to date" that encodes **heading as a single continuous head-direction activity bump** (ellipsoid-body / protocerebral-bridge ring, each PB column = one azimuth) — a clean structural match for "the midline *is* the orientation axis" (a cyclic which-way representation sitting on the midline) (Honkanen, Adden, da Silva Freitas, Heinze 2019, *J. Exp. Biol.*, full-text OA verified). The **circling-on-disruption** tell is well-established: unilateral lesions of directional motor circuitry produce directional rotation/circling — the canonical rotational-behavior model (6-OHDA nigrostriatal lesion lineage; Pycock 1980, model is textbook-canonical). The native handedness surfaces once bilateral balance is removed.

**Against (the over-specified part):** the specific claim that the **mushroom bodies / optic lobes are the flanking ±poles** is *not* supported — the review reports "no direct connection exists between the mushroom bodies and the CX"; MBs are separate *parallel-pathway* learning/valence centers, not the ±1 projections of the CX axis. The ± assignment is the framework's overlay, not the anatomy.

**Verdict OPEN:** midline-CX-as-orientation-axis + circling-on-disruption = SUPPORTED; "flanked by MB/optic lobes as ±poles" = not supported. **Verification: Honkanen 2019 full-text OA; the rotational-model attribution at search level (and flagged as a vertebrate model cited only as the established directional-circling phenomenon — no clinical/diagnostic claim).**

### Conjecture 5 — Antiparallel DNA = two oriented poles of one right-handed helix; homochirality — **SUPPORTED**

Textbook FACT, and the single-axis reading is structurally correct (the cleanest of the six). Canonical B-DNA is right-handed; its two strands are **antiparallel** (one 5′→3′, the other 3′→5′) — two oriented poles of *one* right-handed helix, **not mirror images of each other** (the mirror image of right-handed B-DNA is a *left*-handed helix — the rare Z-form — not the partner strand). Homochirality (life uses L-amino acids and D-sugars exclusively) is established. The conjecture's "two oriented poles of one helix, not a mirror pair" is exactly right (Bansal 2003 review + standard texts; Watson & Crick 1953, *Nature* 171). The only open part is the framework's *downstream* identification of this with the algebraic γ₅ / 3⊕3̄ axis — that is form-reading (F176/F196), not a biology claim. **Verification: right-handed/antiparallel B-DNA + homochirality at textbook-confidence, corroborated across multiple OA sources.**

### Conjecture 6 — Bio-chirality is its own internal symmetry-breaking, not weak-force-inherited — **SUPPORTED (leaning; consistent with F180)**

The modern origin-of-life literature treats biological homochirality as its **own** symmetry-breaking problem, *not* as inherited from weak-force parity violation. Two live non-weak-force camps:
- **(a) Frozen accident** — chance fluctuation + autocatalytic amplification (Frank/Soai lineage); homochirality emerging at the *polymer* level from a racemic monomer pool (Chen & Ma 2020, *PLoS Comput. Biol.*, full-text OA verified).
- **(b) A *different* deterministic chiral agent** — chiral-induced spin selectivity (CISS) on magnetic surfaces, ~60% ee, framed as "distinctly separate from cosmic or weak-force explanations" (Ozturk, Liu, Sutherland, Sasselov 2023, *Sci. Adv.* / arXiv:2303.01394, source-verified).

The weak-force / PVED inheritance is the *disfavored* hypothesis (PVED ~10⁻¹⁷ kT, sign conformation-hypersensitive, "not yet detected" — per F180's verified survey: Blackmond 2010; Martínez-Gil 2025). **Either** live camp makes bio-chirality its own internal symmetry-breaking, distinct from the weak force — matching the conjecture and re-confirming F180's "second-actor lean."

**Caveat (keeps it honest):** (i) the *specific* algebraic identification — bio = internal SU(3) 3⊕3̄ within g₂ vs weak = triality-moved su(2)_L — is the framework's **own form-reading** (F196/F197); biology speaks only to "bio-homochirality is its own problem, not weak-force-inherited," which is what is supported. (ii) The field leans *deterministic* (CISS) over *pure chance* in some recent work, so "frozen accident *specifically*" is not the unique answer — but determinism-via-magnetic-CISS is still **not** weak-force inheritance, so the "distinct from the weak force" core holds either way. **Verification: Ozturk 2023 + Chen & Ma 2020 source-verified; Blackmond 2010 / Martínez-Gil 2025 carried from F180 (verified there).**
