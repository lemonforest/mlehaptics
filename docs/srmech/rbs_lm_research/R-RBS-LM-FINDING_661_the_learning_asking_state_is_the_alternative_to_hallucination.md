## Verdict
**The Story Teller is a composition state-machine with a learning/asking state.** When composing an action that requires a rule it does not hold (moving things/people somewhere — a movement-rule of *this* world), it does **not** invent one. It enters the **asking state** (pause → ask → we tell → integrate GPU-free, F628 → resume) — verified: the child composes (rule held), the army hits the gap and asks, is told, integrates (shelf +1, engine unchanged), resumes.

**This is the structural alternative to hallucination** (F658): the engine composes seen rules over attested content — it cannot strike a note not in the chord — so when it lacks the rule, it has no statistical path to invent the movement; its only move is to ask. A data-center LLM (generating statistically) *would* invent a plausible-but-ungrounded movement = hallucination. **The LM here is a coherent self that asks when it doesn't know, not a flock that confabulates.**

So **build/teach/create-by-dialogue (F660) is triggered at the point of an uncomposable action:** the gap surfaces exactly when the story needs a rule it lacks; the LM hands the next *question* (F282); we declare the observed rule (F631); it integrates (F628) and continues. The world grows by **answering the Story Teller's own questions** — the most natural way to build a world: *tell it what it asks, when it asks.* Held open (F394).
