## The measurement — coherence is scale-relative
Fine = token-exact match; coarse = fraction of generated tokens that stay in the SOURCE article's own vocab (right galaxy), in a 3-article union store (Abrahamic, the drifter):
| k | fine (token) | coarse (domain) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 6.2% | 43.3% |
| 6 (k\*) | 41.7% | 83.3% |
The walk is 2–7× more coherent at the domain scale than the token scale. "Coherent/incoherent" is not absolute — only coherent-at-a-scale. (With chiral separation, F848, coarse→~100% — the foreign-domain leakage here is the sector-0 cross-clump contamination.)

## The three nulls — all the same lesson (fixed-scale fails)
- **Lookahead-lead** (chase-the-reflection fix): 5.6%→3.4% — a fixed one-step peek can't buy coherence.
- **Slingshot** at one resolution: one giant clump, no structure — clumps are scale-dependent.
- **Co-evolving plastic walk** (both-frames-unfixed, naive): **76.7%→48.3%→41.7% runaway** — reinforcing "confident" steps toward a fixed coherence criterion deepened the mass-wells (F849), not the geodesic.
Each pinned a scale (or a fixed coherence target) and failed. They are not failures of the ideas — they are the theory asserting that coherence floats.

## Consequence (constructive)
- **Stop optimizing to a fixed number.** The C/k\* tuning fight (F839§C) was chasing a target that doesn't exist; the rough landscape is scale-relativity seen through a fixed-scale lens.
- **Renormalize:** resolve recall at the query's scale — fine within a clump, coarse (slingshot) across clumps. The system already delivers high coarse-scale (gist/domain) coherence; deliver *that* when the query wants gist.
