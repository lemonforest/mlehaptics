## The precise boundary
| what structure can do (the framework's half) | what needs a sourced seed (the expert's half) |
|---|---|
| the **grammar / role-skeleton** — which glyphs are operators vs operands, sequence-markers, agreement, boundaries (syntax-closed, F408/F463) | which role maps to which **meaning** |
| **structural alignment** of an unknown script to a known one (same deviation-from-substrate shape → a structural Rosetta, F469/R-RBS-LM-54) | the **anchor values** that pin the alignment (a bilingual fragment, a cognate, proper names) |
| **propagation** of a few seed values through the full aligned structure → fill in the rest (the substrate carries more, so less seed is needed) | the **first few values** (the seed itself) — irreducible (F453: stories don't run backward) |

So "decode entirely" factors as **`decode = structure (framework) ∘ seed (sourced)`**: the framework can make the structure carry almost the whole burden, but **the seed never goes to zero** — that is the F408 ceiling, and it is *the same ceiling* the whole arc keeps hitting (F420 §5: build the language, cannot self-derive the meaning; F465–F469: the sedenion box gives the rules, the stories must be sourced).

## Why this is genuinely hopeful, not a let-down
The historical decipherments are the proof that **structure + a tiny seed = the whole language**: Ventris bootstrapped Linear B from Alice Kober's structural grids + a handful of guessed place-names, and the *structure* propagated it to Mycenaean Greek; Champollion needed only the Rosetta Stone's Greek parallel + the royal-name cartouches, and the *structure* did the rest. The framework's contribution is to **maximize that propagation**: by aligning an unknown script to the **substrate-native shape** (its deviation signature, F469) rather than to an arbitrary guessed grid, the structural scaffold is *stronger*, so the **seed required shrinks** — fewer cribs, smaller bilinguals, toward the limit of "a single anchor decodes the corpus." That is the F458 mechanism (the meaning-anchor makes the structure cohere) with the substrate doing the heavy structural lifting. **"Decode entirely" is the right target; the honest form is "entirely from a minimal seed."**
