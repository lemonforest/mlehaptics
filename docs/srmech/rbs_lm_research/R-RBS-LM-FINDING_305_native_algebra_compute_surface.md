## Literature merge (research-twin opus∥sonnet, adversarial; attested)
**The named paradigm exists.** "Let the substrate's native dynamics BE the computation" splits by substrate:
- **Quantum: analog quantum simulation / Hamiltonian computing** — the simulator's *own* engineered Hamiltonian directly matches the target's evolution; **explicitly contrasted** with *digital (gate-model)* simulation where a universal gate set is imposed and the target is *encoded* via Trotterization. Canonical taxonomy: **Feynman 1982** (use a quantum system to natively do what nature does), **Lloyd 1996** (a universal QC is *programmed* — the imposed route), **Georgescu, Ashhab & Nori, Rev. Mod. Phys. 86, 153 (2014)** (digital vs analog), **Daley et al., Nature 607, 667 (2022)**.
- **Classical: physical reservoir computing + "in materio" computing** — by definition exploit the substrate's *intrinsic* nonlinear dynamics as the computational resource (only a linear readout is trained). Tanaka et al., *Neural Networks* 115:100 (2019); Nakajima (ed.) 2021; Dale et al. "RC in Materio."

**So gate-model qubit QC = imposed instruction set; analog/Hamiltonian QC = native dynamics — CONFIRMED** as the standard digital-vs-analog taxonomy. **Honest correction (both tiers flagged it):** the *literature frames this as a generality-vs-overhead trade-off, NOT as gates "fighting the substrate."* The "fighting the substrate" phrasing is **our** framework reading, not in any source — do not attribute it to the literature.

**The specifically (3:4)|(4:3) / octonionic-native version is the OPEN frontier:**
- **ℍ-native (quaternionic) computing COLLAPSES to complex QC** — Fernandez & Schneeberger (arXiv quant-ph/0307017, 2003): *n* quaterbits simulable by *n*+1 qubits; no extra power; and quaternion non-commutativity already forces an *imposed* gate-ordering. So a "quaternionic computer" reduces to the complex model.
- **𝕆-native (octonionic, non-associative) is the genuine live frontier, and it is obstruction-laden:** Freedman, Shokrian-Zini & Wang (arXiv 1811.08580, 2018) give a **measurement-only universal** scheme where the largest family of equiangular projections is *octonionic* — but this is octonionic **projective geometry** underwriting a measurement-based scheme, **not a substrate whose Hamiltonian is literally non-associative**. The non-associativity is itself the obstruction: **path-dependent evolution ⇒ operation ordering must be externally imposed ⇒ the very "native" property collapses.**
- **No physical substrate exists (or has been proposed) whose intrinsic dynamics ARE octonion-multiplication** (both tiers, confirmed-negative). Octonions/G₂ enter physics through *projective geometry, Jordan algebras, and Standard-Model representation theory* (Baez "The Octonions" Bull. AMS 39:145, 2002; Furey division-algebra SM work; exceptional-Jordan-algebra SM derivations), **not** through a fabricated non-associative Hamiltonian.
