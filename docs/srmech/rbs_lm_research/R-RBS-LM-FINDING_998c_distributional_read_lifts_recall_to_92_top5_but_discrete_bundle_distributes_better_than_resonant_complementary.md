## The reading (two honest results)
1. **The distributional read lifts recall massively — the top-1 numbers undersold everything.** Both stores put the true next in the top-5 far more than top-1 (bundle 52→92%, resonant 53→78%). So the "39%/42%/53%/55%" top-1 figures across the whole session were the *hardest* possible metric (exact-strongest-edge); the stored knowledge *recovers the target* much more often than top-1 implies. **The honest recall measure is distributional** (top-K / rank / sample the out-flow), and by it recall is strong (up to 92% top-5). This is the `[[R-RBS-LM-54]]`/#193 "distribution-not-argmax" point, now grounded on the resonant store.
2. **The discrete bundle distributes BETTER than the resonant kernel — a genuine reversal.** The resonant kernel won *sharp top-1* (F998b, 55/53 argmax), but the bundle wins the *distribution* (top-3/5: 83/92 vs 73/78). The bounded high-band kernel resolves the single strongest edge crisply but its *tail ranking is noisier* (it commits to the dominant out-flow); the discrete bundle's full similarity ranking is *better calibrated* over the whole candidate set. **So the resonant store is not universally better — it is better at *sharpness*, worse at *coverage*.** Reported per `[[feedback_dont_pre_commit_spike_query_operators]]`: the resonant enthusiasm of F997/998b is refined, not inflated.

## The architecture this hands back (propose → resolve)
The two representations are **complementary, and the F998 translation-bridge becomes a two-stage pipeline:**
- **PROPOSE (sparse bundle):** the Class-M sparse-relationship store gives the *calibrated distribution* — a top-5 candidate set with **92% coverage** of the true next. Cheap, sparse, well-ranked over the whole set.
- **RESOLVE (bounded resonant bridge):** the bounded high-band magnetic-Laplacian kernel *re-ranks within the proposed set* to the sharp top-1 (its strength, F998b). Density only in the bounded bridge, applied to a small candidate set.
This is the honest realization of F998 (sparse storage + bounded translation kernel) *and* F978's coherence-trichotomy (propose a distribution, sharpen when confident): **sparse proposes, resonant resolves.** It uses each representation for exactly what it is best at — and the 92%-coverage propose stage means the sharp resolve only has to pick among ~5 candidates, where the resonant kernel is strongest.
