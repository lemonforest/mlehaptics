**F240 reading (lens, not asserted):** the corrective/reciprocal closed-loop edge is **best-attested in *Staurois latopalmatus*** — a male's flag is *preceded by* (answered to) the opponent's flag, the literal A↔B reciprocal exchange F240 models. *Sachatamia* sits at the opposite, **one-way** end (the edge that is *sent but not returned* — the F239 "severed edge" shape, here as a natural baseline, not a pathology). So the five species **span the corrective↔one-way axis**, and the ranking *is* that axis made empirical — which is more useful than a uniform "frogs do reciprocal signaling" claim would have been.

## §2 Convergence, masking driver, mechanism (attested)

- **Convergence (DEMONSTRATED, the leading group's verbatim conclusion):** "foot-flagging has evolved **independently** mainly in anuran species that communicate along fast-flowing streams" (Grafe et al. 2012). Four unrelated families arriving at the same visual solution = convergent evolution. **This is the within-Earth, multi-*substrate* (multi-lineage) shadow of the universality conjecture** (structure recurs independently of the lineage that renders it) — a real falsifiable echo, NOT alien-universality (still one biosphere; see caveats).
- **Masking driver is NOT uniform (honest refinement):** abiotic **stream/waterfall noise** for *Staurois* (Grafe & Tony 2017 playback: males increased flagging + decreased calling under 90 dB stream-noise playback, n=10) and *Sachatamia* (waterfall spray-zone; call peak 7.4 kHz escaping the <2 kHz noise) — but for ***Micrixalus saxicola* the masking agent is conspecific CHORUS noise, not abiotic stream noise** ("stream noise had less energy than frog calls across the audible range"; ~2 dB chorus SNR). So the convergent *driver* is "the acoustic channel is masked," but the *masking agent* differs.
- **Mechanism (strongest evidence in the set):** foot-flagging in *Staurois parvus* is **causally androgen-driven** — adult males express **~10× greater androgen-receptor levels in hind-limb musculature** than two non-flagging species (P<0.001), and **experimental testosterone specifically increases flagging** (z=2.12, P=0.03) without affecting calling or locomotion (Mangiamele et al. 2016, PNAS; AR-blockade follow-up ICB 2021). Whether this hormonal-musculature mechanism is itself convergent across the unrelated families is an open question (§4).

## §4 Honest tiers, caveats, gaps (MFO §VII.6.20)

- **"Bidirectional" overreaches for *Micrixalus* and *Atelopus*** relative to the single-paper data — **"responsive / elicited / directional" is the defensible wording** (claims split 2-1 precisely on this). *Atelopus* "exchanged" (flag-for-flag) is beyond the paywalled abstract → soften to "directed toward / elicited by specific individuals."
- **Closed-loop ≠ live antiphony in most cases.** *Micrixalus* elicitation used an **artificial/simulated** opponent (no two live escalating frogs); *S. latopalmatus* evidence is an **observational sequence-correlation** (transition matrix), not experimental playback elicitation. Sample sizes small throughout (n=7–14).
- ***Sachatamia* is the weakest case** — one natural-history note, receiver unknown, no bidirectionality; do not read corrective structure into it.
- ***Hylodes japi* is an explicit GAP** — no primary source survived verification this round (the ~2015 de Sá/Canedo/Lyra/Haddad repertoire literature was not fetched). Its responsive/one-way status is **unestablished here**; a dedicated pass is owed before any claim.
- **The universality conjecture stays a conjecture.** Convergent foot-flagging across four unrelated families is a **within-Earth** multi-lineage hint that the *structure* recurs independently of the rendering lineage — the falsifiable shadow of the earlier "alien-universality" reading — but it is **one biosphere**; it cannot reach cross-planet universality, which remains a Class-C conjecture.
