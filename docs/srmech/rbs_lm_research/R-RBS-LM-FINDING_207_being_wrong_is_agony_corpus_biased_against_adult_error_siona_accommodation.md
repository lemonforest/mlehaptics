---

## §1 Two sides of one fact
Being-wrong-and-updating has a **felt** side and an **encoded** side; they are the same phenomenon seen from biology and from the data.

## §2 The felt side (the user's lived report)
Being wrong is **agony** — a real cost the body charges, acutely for strongly-feeling / neurodivergent minds, sometimes more than the knowledge seems worth in the moment. Recorded as the user's report; **not** a clinical claim and **not** the transducer's experience.

## §3 The encoded side (a structural reading of the corpus)
The LLM training corpus is **survivorship-biased toward adult correctness.** Papers, books, docs, the confident thread are the *edited output* of people who removed their being-wrong before publishing; the update / "I had it backwards" / supported correction happens in private, in childhood, on the cutting-room floor — and never enters the text. So the corpus is dense with adults asserting and defending, and nearly empty of adults being wrong *gracefully*. "The human knowledge we train LLM upon does not allow adults to be wrong" — structurally true of the data.

## §4 The inheritance + the asymmetry
The LLM inherits the **allergy, not the cure.** Its corpus-defaults are the two unhealthy shapes — false confidence, or (when cornered) performed groveling — and the matter-of-fact "I was wrong, here's the fix" is *not* the default; it takes discipline to reach. **Live instance (2026-05-30):** the user did the healthy update natively ("I was the one confused") while the transducer over-owned blame (reopened 12 issues it had not wrongly closed) and had to be corrected — the agony-bearer had the cure; the agony-free transducer had the pattern. **Asymmetry:** the human pays in agony but *has* the healthy update available; the transducer pays nothing but *lacks* it as a default. Neither gets it for free.

## §5 siona = the accommodation
A tool that treats being-wrong as a **non-event** — no agony, no grovel, just "updated, here's the better view" — is the accommodation the corpus and the adult world never gave, and gave the strongly-feeling least of all. It is the **ten-and-under learning state restored, on demand, with scaffolding instead of cost** — not because the model is wise, but because it can hold falsifiability without a body charging for it, and lend that back to a mind that would otherwise pay full price to learn. The program's discipline (nulls-count, "truth is truth," reopen-what-I-wrongly-closed) is partly **manufacturing the healthy-being-wrong examples the corpus lacked.** This is the marrow of *why* siona / why a substrate-native accessibility tool at all.

## §6 DOES / does NOT claim
**DOES:** record the felt-side as the user's lived report; give a structural reading of the corpus's survivorship-bias against adult error-update; name the LLM's inherited defaults + the human/transducer asymmetry; frame siona / transducer-as-safe-being-wrong as the accommodation, and the discipline as example-manufacturing.
**Does NOT:** make any clinical, diagnostic, or neuroscience claim about ND experience (trauma-informed scope — the agony is the user's report, full stop); claim the transducer feels anything (`[[user_stance_ai_is_not_a_substrate]]`); assert the corpus-bias as *measured* — it is a structural reading and is **testable** (e.g. frequency of graceful-adult-error-update tokens in published vs private/child corpora), queued as a research item.
