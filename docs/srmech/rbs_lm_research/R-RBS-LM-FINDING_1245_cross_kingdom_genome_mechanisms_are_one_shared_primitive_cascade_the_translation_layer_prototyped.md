## 2. SAME vs DIFFERENT — the reading

**SAME (the coherency tower):** every mechanism composes from **{A content-address, D/G search, K cut, splice/append, M couple, C order, k=3 EC}** — the shared cascade, a subset of the 14 A–N that our genome couples through `the_one` (F1242). There is **no new primitive** in any of them; CRISPR is not "a new kind of operation," it is *content-address a guide → search → cut → splice → append the log* — primitives we already have.

**DIFFERENT (the specialization):** only **which primitives are emphasized + the situational purpose**. Plasmid leans on append (mobility); CRISPR on address+search+cut (immunity); transposon on cut×2+splice (self-mobility); recombination on couple+template (break repair); ciliate on order+filter (compaction); virus on address+splice (infection). Same alphabet, different sentence.

**Two payoffs:**
- **The translation layer is real and universal.** A virus edits a eukaryote, bacterial CRISPR edits human genes, HGT crosses bacterial species — *because the primitives are universal*. The "coherency translation layer" the user intuited is literally **"same cascade, different rung"** (F1244); nothing needs translating because it is already the same op set. Ask **(d) is prototyped across kingdoms** at the primitive level.
- **Ciliates confirm "biology stores by purpose," and it's OUR L↔M split.** The protozoan **micronucleus** (archival, full, scrambled germline) → **macronucleus** (compact, unscrambled, working somatic) is the SAME content in two encodings chosen by *purpose* — exactly `[[project_class_L_store_class_M_working_memory_reversible_spectral_bridge]]`: the Class-L archival store ↔ the Class-M working store, a reversible basis-change. Biology already does the two-tier storage we designed.
