## The reading (the hypothesis's first pillar)
- **Bonded knowledge is coherent when held as bounded relationship-tomes.** The same content that wandered flat (0.010, all BRANCH) recalls at 0.077 with real COHERENT verdicts once chunked into `recursive_cut` community-tomes. The knowledge *was there* and *is stable* — it just needed to be held sparsely and bounded (under the F896 wall), which is what the knowledge Laplacian's community structure provides.
- **The flat surface's incoherence is the communication projection, not the knowledge.** English-flat (0.006) is the worst — it carries the full function-word frequency prior (F946). The knowledge layer, chunked, clears it ~13×. So the "hard part" of generation lives in the **surface translation** (adding the function-word linear projection), exactly as the inference-as-translation hypothesis predicts: *the knowing is coherent; the communicating is the lossy step.*
- **This is the knowledge→communication hierarchy's bottom rung.** Chunked knowledge = the bonded base (coherent); the ASL-gloss content-dense layer (F959) and the English surface (F946) are the *communication* projections above it. Inference walks *down* this hierarchy, and each step down adds surface (and loses coherence) — the translation cascade.
