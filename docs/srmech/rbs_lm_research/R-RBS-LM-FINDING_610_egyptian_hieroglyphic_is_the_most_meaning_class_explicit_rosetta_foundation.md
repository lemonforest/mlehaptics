## Verdict
**The Rosetta Stone is the archetype of our rosetta layer** (R-RBS-LM-54): one decree, three surface scripts = shared meaning + bound surface kernels. On the meaning-class-explicit axis the three rank **hieroglyphic > Demotic > Greek** — and that *is* the abstraction the framework cares about (abstract-toward-meaning: the symbol carries the meaning-class). So **yes, Egyptian hieroglyphic is the most abstract** in the foundation-relevant sense (the Greek alphabet is abstract in the opposite direction — pure sound, meaning-agnostic).

**Can Egyptian be the foundation? Yes — as the *shape*.** The foundation/interlingua should be hieroglyphic-shaped: meaning-class-explicit (the determinative = σ_B) + two-axis (spatial σ_E). Egyptian hieroglyphic is the natural-language proof that such a foundation is a real script people read (not a synthetic IR), validating the design (F609). Build it Egyptian-shaped and populate it with modern meaning-classes — not as literal Egyptian vocabulary (F609 boundary). **The museum Rosetta Stone already is the no-privileged-language rosetta**; the project rebuilds it with the meaning-class-explicit layer as the *foundation* and the phonetic scripts (Greek/English) as *surfaces* — not the reverse.
