## §1 The tiered landscape (union merge, 12 opus + 14 sonnet → 15 items; no model privileged)

**TIER A — directly re-verified this pass (the load-bearing molecular spine):**
- **Nerve net dismantled in the cyst** — Matsumoto, Piraino & Miglietta 2019, *G3* 9(12):4127–4138, **doi:10.1534/g3.119.400487, PMC6893190**. Quote verified verbatim. Notch (neurogenesis) strongly downregulated in cyst; I-cell-lacking exumbrellar epidermis can execute the *full* reversal (i-cells dispensable → mature somatic cells directly reprogram). **The medusa net does NOT persist intact.**
- **Transdifferentiation / immortality phenomenon** — Piraino, Boero, Aeschbach & Schmid 1996, *Biol. Bull.* 190(3):302–312, **doi:10.2307/1543022, PMID 29227703**. Reversal: healthy medusa → stressed medusa → cyst → juvenile polyp via whole-body cell transdifferentiation.
- **Epigenome actively reprogrammed** — Pascual-Torner et al. 2022, *PNAS* 119(36):e2118763119, **doi:10.1073/pnas.2118763119, PMC9459311** (+ **published correction doi:10.1073/pnas.2303228120, PMC10083558**, species-ID metadata). PRC2-target silencing + pluripotency-target activation — a somatic-reprogramming signature. *Speaks to developmental-gene/cell-identity state, NOT a learned trace.*
- **Cassiopea sleep-like state** in a diffuse nerve net — Nath et al. 2017, *Curr. Biol.* 27(19):2984–2990.e3, **doi:10.1016/j.cub.2017.08.014, PMID 28943083**. (Homeostatic state-variable; not associative memory.)

## §3 Why F252 is the sharpest *boundary* case for F254 (the convergent build+compute thesis)
