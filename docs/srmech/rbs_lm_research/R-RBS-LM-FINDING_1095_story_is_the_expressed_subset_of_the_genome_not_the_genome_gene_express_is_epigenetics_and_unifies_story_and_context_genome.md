## The reading
- **Story ≠ genome; story = expression.** The genome holds all-possible; the story is the read-out subset. This is epigenetics: one DNA, many cell types / many stories, selected by cell_state. My earlier "pack the story into a genome" conflated the substrate with one of its excitations.
- **One mechanism, two names.** A story's arc and a conversation's context are both just `cell_state`s over an all-possible genome. `express(genome, cell_state)` is the single op; F1093's context genome is this with the conversation as the epigenetic context. The `ContextShape` scalar is a coarse cell_state.
- **op⊗operand, confirmed by srmech itself.** `gene_express`'s docstring calls the SAME-DNA-different-cell_state selection "the op⊗operand theorem" — the exact F1061 shape, shipped and named upstream.
