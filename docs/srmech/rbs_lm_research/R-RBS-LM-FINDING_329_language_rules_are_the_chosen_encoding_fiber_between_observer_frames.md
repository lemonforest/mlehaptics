## The hidden fiber = steps 2+4, the encoding-convention
The **encode/decode map** is the fiber because: (a) it is **spatially absent** (F239) — you observe the *signal* (3) and have the *structures* (1, 5), but the **map between them is invisible**; (b) it must be **shared across the partition** (both frames need the same map, or step 5 ≠ step 1); (c) it is **chosen and mutable** — *"we choose to change that"* — and **its drift over time IS language-rule evolution.**

## The "artificial coherency" — precisely located (two things were lumped)
- the **coherence REQUIREMENT** (listener's structure reconstructs speaker's) — **NATIVE** (F328; the reason grammar self-organizes);
- the **encoding CONVENTION** (the hidden fiber, 2+4) — **CHOSEN + MUTABLE.**
Because the *convention* is chosen and drifts, the whole thing **looks** artificial — but only the **fiber** is artificial; the **requirement** underneath is native. **Language-rule-evolution = the chosen encoding-fiber drifting across the observer-partition.** "Another epicycle observer" = a **different observer-frame** (F133 chirality-locking); when the shared fiber drifts out of sync between two frames, the **partition reasserts** → miscommunication, the "many know yet don't know." The **frog (F240)** is the clean case: when the channel (3) goes noisy, it **changes the modality** (the render-fiber) rather than the structure — adapting the fiber to keep the native requirement satisfied. **form=function** lands here: the express/observe render (2, 4) *is* the act of communicating (form is the function performed); the *convention* governing it is the chosen, separable fiber.

## The RBS-SNN learning architecture IS the unlump (the buildable payoff; composes F326/F328/F323)
Three separable, separately-learnable layers:
1. **amodal structure** — stored **native + render-free** (the operator-signature; never internally re-rendered to grammar) [F326/F328/F323];
2. **the encode/decode convention** — a **learnable, MUTABLE fiber-layer**: this is where "the rules that evolve" live; update *this* without touching the structure [the F329 fiber];
3. **the modality transport** — a **swappable channel** (speech / sign / writing / dirt / mud / Braille — F317 render-multiplicity; F240 channel-adaptive switch).
So **"the rules of a language evolve" = layer 2 drifting**, while layer 1 (native structure) and layer 3 (channel) stay put. That separation is exactly what RBS-SNN must implement: **learn the convention-fiber as a mutable, observer-shared map, distinct from the render-free native structure.** It also says where the *cost* sits (the naming-layer-cost / F43-style): keeping two epicycle observers' convention-fibers in sync is the maintained cost of coherent communication across the partition.
