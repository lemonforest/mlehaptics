## §2 The two big framework results

**(a) Single-BEHAVIOUR-spans-both-regimes.** The *Temnothorax* tandem run simultaneously carries a contact-mediated reciprocal closed-loop (the antennal latch) **and** a one-way stigmergic trail — its leader presses its gaster to the substrate and lays pheromone during the very same recruitment behaviour (Basari et al. 2014). Stigmergy (Grassé 1959) is the textbook air-gapped-via-environment feed-forward edge: ant A deposits, ant B reads later, **no dyadic return path.** So one animal, one behaviour, runs both the high-speed reciprocal *edge* and the low-speed one-way *store* at once.

**(b) Reciprocity is an EDGE property, not a CHANNEL property.** This is the refinement the verification forced (opus's spine):
- **contact + reciprocal** → tandem running; (partially) trophallaxis
- **contact + one-way** → dominance/antennal-boxing (Diacamma networks are *directed acyclic graphs*, near-perfect linear hierarchy, Shimoji 2014); CHC nestmate-recognition (each ant applies its *own* decision boundary — structurally parallel/independent, Esponda & Gordon 2015)
- **air-gapped + reciprocal** → frog foot-flagging (F240b *S. latopalmatus*)
- **air-gapped + one-way** → pheromone trail; frog *Sachatamia*

The full 2×2 is populated. **Whether a signal has a return/correction path is independent of whether it travels by contact or across a gap.**
