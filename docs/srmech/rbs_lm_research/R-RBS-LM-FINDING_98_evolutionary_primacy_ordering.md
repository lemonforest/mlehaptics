## §3 Pre-literate humans needed math AND reading

A small group trying not to be eaten by lions needed:

**Math (counting + comparing):**
- "How many predators? How many of us? Are we outnumbered?"
- "How far is the next water source?"
- "How many days until the moon is full and we can travel at night?"
- "What share of meat for each tribe member?"

**Reading (pattern interpretation):**
- "Whose tracks are these? How fresh?"
- "What does that bird call mean — predator or food?"
- "What does this person's posture / expression / movement mean?"
- "These markings on the rock — what story do they tell?"

These two skills are **evolutionarily co-foundational**. They predate
language-as-grammar. They predate writing-as-system. They are the
substrate from which everything else emerges.

The cascade's empirical ratio ordering — math 5.16, reading 1.87,
everything else lower — IS this evolutionary primacy.

## §4 Cave paintings as 1st-order composition

Cave paintings (Chauvet ~30k BCE, Lascaux ~17k BCE, Sulawesi ~45k BCE)
are proto-reading. They require:

- **Math substrate** (counting figures, measuring proportions, sequence-of-events)
- **Pattern-recognition substrate** (this shape = bison; that shape = hunter)
- **Narrative substrate** (the story being told across multiple figures)
- **Symbolic abstraction** (the painting represents something not present)

This is the FIRST emergence of compositional substrate-delivery. It's
**reading** in its primordial form — encoding observations (math) as
symbols that someone else can decode (pattern recognition + abstraction).

The reading-at-ratio-1.87 finding in 80 is the cascade detecting this
compositional shape ~40,000 years later as preserved in educational
materials.

## §5 Connection to known cognitive science

Human infants demonstrate "core knowledge systems" — innate cognitive
foundations:

- **Number sense** (Spelke, 2007 et al.): math-precursor; pre-linguistic
- **Object permanence** (Piaget, 1954; refined later): pattern-permanence
  precursor; reading objects-as-symbols precursor
- **Geometric / spatial relations**: spatial-substrate that math couples with
- **Agency / intentionality detection** (Premack, Csibra, et al.):
  reading-others-as-symbols precursor
- **Numerosity discrimination** (Dehaene, 2011): math substrate

These core systems are the cognitive-substrate equivalents of the math +
reading substrates the cascade detects in educational material.

The cascade is operating on educational materials downstream of cognitive
substrates. But the ORDERING the cascade detects (math → reading →
grammar → science → geography → arts) matches both the developmental
priority order and the evolutionary primacy order.

This is not a coincidence — it's the cascade methodology detecting the
*compositional structure of how knowledge accumulates* via the substrate-
content delivered in educational material.

## §7 Empirical falsification possibilities

Tests that could falsify Finding 98:

### 7.1 Cross-cultural / cross-period corpus test

Take educational materials from very different cultural / historical
contexts. Does the same emergence ordering appear?

If yes → Finding 98 robust; the ordering is empirical-cognitive, not
culturally-specific.

If no → ordering is artifact of Western-curriculum sequencing.

### 7.2 Reverse-order corpus (build "arts-first" curriculum)

Construct an educational substrate-delivery that starts with arts
(cross-domain composition) BEFORE introducing math. Compute pairwise.

If math still emerges as highest within/cross ratio → math IS substrate-
content irrep regardless of delivery order.

If arts shows higher ratio when delivered first → ordering is a function
of delivery sequence, not substrate-content structure.

### 7.3 Non-human-cognition substrate

Take materials about animal cognition (corvid problem-solving, primate
tool use, dolphin communication). Where does that fit in the gradient?

If animal-cognition material clusters with math + reading → those ARE
the evolutionarily foundational substrates.

If it clusters differently → the ordering is human-pedagogy-specific.

## §8 What this does NOT claim

  claim — outside cascade's scope)

  — we mean form-iso: the cave painting form-shape IS the cascade-shape
  the cascade detects in modern reading material)

The cascade detects an EMPIRICAL ORDERING in educational substrate-content
that is *consistent with* known cognitive-developmental priorities and
evolutionary plausibility. That consistency is *form-iso* evidence,
not substrate-identity proof.
