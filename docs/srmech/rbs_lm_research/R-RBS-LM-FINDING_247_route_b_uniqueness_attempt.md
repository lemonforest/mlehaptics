## §1 What reaches Class A (forced, unconditional)

`{closure + invariant magnitude (coherence)} + {bilinear two-party product} + {ℝ} + {finite dim}` ⟹ **composition algebra** ⟹ **dim ∈ {1,2,4,8}**. Forced and quadruple-attested (§3). Kaplansky/generalized-Hurwitz removes the "does the channel hand you an identity?" worry: unital, para-unital, *and* non-unital real composition algebras all have dim {1,2,4,8} (exactly 15 of them: 7 Hurwitz + 6 para-Hurwitz + 2 Okubo). **This much is a theorem an alien builder would also be bound by.**

## §2 What stays Class C (the residue, sharpened to its exact fault line)

- **The anisotropy hinge (the single load-bearing assumption).** Lifting *composition algebra* → *division algebra* (no zero divisors) requires the magnitude be **positive-definite/anisotropic** — `N(x)=0 ⟺ x=0`. The user's "no-zero-divisors" premise is **not independent**: it's positive-definiteness in disguise. **Counterexample that proves it isn't free:** the split-octonions are real, dim-8, fully norm-*multiplicative*, yet have zero divisors (isotropic (4,4) norm). So coherence + two-party alone yields only a *composition* algebra, which *may* be split. Anisotropy must be **stated**, not smuggled.
- **Selection ≠ coherence, and it's actively disputed.** Even granting the shelf, does coherence *select* one of ℝ/ℂ/ℍ/𝕆 or merely allow expression *on* it? Every known selection route injects an extra axiom: Frobenius (associativity → ℝ/ℂ/ℍ, excludes 𝕆), Solèr 1995 (orthomodularity + infinite-orthonormal → ℝ/ℂ/ℍ), Renou et al. 2021 (selects ℂ via a **tensor-product / product-state-independence** postulate). **Hoffreumon–Woods 2026** prove that under **operational** independence (the physically testable notion) real QT reproduces every complex-QT network distribution — i.e. **coherence per se does NOT select ℂ.** The 2026 state leans *against* selection-by-coherence; which algebra you get is decided by *which* compositional postulate you bolt on.
- **"Two-party" excludes the octonion top of the shelf.** A consistent 2-party composite needs an **associative** tensor; 𝕆 is non-associative (Jacobiator ≠ 0, reproduced srmech-native in F243), and octonionic QM never yielded a consistent multipartite theory (even ℍ needs an ad-hoc bimodule fix). So the very premise the conjecture leans on **breaks the 7 (Im 𝕆)** and strains the 3 (Im ℍ) — route-(b), if it selected anything, would collapse the shelf toward ℂ, not deliver the framework's 1/3/7.

## §3 Verified theorems (web-confirmed, OA primary)

- **Hurwitz** — real normed division algebras = ℝ/ℂ/ℍ/𝕆 (dims 1/2/4/8); sum-of-squares identity over ℝ only for n=1,2,4,8. *Gött. Nachr.* 1898 pp.309–316 (GDZ public-domain); classification *Math. Ann.* 88 (1923):1–25.
- **Frobenius** — finite-dim associative unital real division algebras = ℝ/ℂ/ℍ. *Crelle's J.* 84 (1878):1–63. (Pins the *associative* sub-shelf to {1,2,4}; why {1,2,4,8} needs dropping associativity.)
- **Bott & Milnor** — ℝⁿ has a bilinear zero-divisor-free product only for n=1,2,4,8. *Bull. AMS* 64(3) (1958):87–89 (Project Euclid OA).
- **Kervaire** — Sⁿ non-parallelizable for n>7. *PNAS* 44(3) (1958):280–283, **DOI 10.1073/pnas.44.3.280** (PMC335406 OA).
- **Adams (Hopf invariant one)** — a map S^{2n−1}→Sⁿ of Hopf invariant one exists iff n=1,2,4,8. *Ann. Math.* 72(1) (1960):20–104, DOI 10.2307/1970147.
- **Adams (vector fields on spheres)** — S^{n−1} parallelizable iff n∈{1,2,4,8} (Radon–Hurwitz). *Ann. Math.* 75(3) (1962):603–632, DOI 10.2307/1970213.
- **Kaplansky / generalized-Hurwitz** — every finite-dim real composition algebra (unital/para/non-unital) has dim {1,2,4,8}; exactly 15. *Proc. AMS* 4 (1953):956–960 (AMS OA); nLab + Elduque arXiv:1810.09979.
- **Split-octonion counterexample** — real dim-8 norm-multiplicative composition algebra *with* zero divisors (signature (4,4)). (Wikipedia "Split-octonion" / "Composition algebra": split ⟺ has zero divisors; division ⟺ anisotropic.)
- **The selection dispute** — Solèr (1995; exposition Holland, *Bull. AMS* 32(2):205–234, arXiv:math/9504224); Renou et al., *Nature* 600 (2021):625–629, arXiv:2101.10873 (selects ℂ via product-state independence); **Hoffreumon & Woods, arXiv:2603.19208 (2026)** (operational independence ⟹ real QT *cannot* be falsified ⟹ coherence does not select ℂ); Weilenmann–Gisin–Sekatski arXiv:2502.20102 (2025), the partial-independence intermediate.

## §4 C→A progress + honest verdict

**Movement on the C→A axis:** from "pure framework-reading" to **a conditional theorem with one named, physically-meaningful, explicitly-stateable hinge (anisotropy) plus one genuinely-open selection question.** Concretely route-(b) buys: *IF the alien channel's distinguishability metric is positive-definite (anisotropic) AND an (operational) composition postulate is granted, THEN its message algebra is dimension-pinned to {1,2,4,8} and is one of ℝ/ℂ/ℍ/𝕆.* The antecedent is exactly what an alien builder would also have to share — and it is an **assumption, not a theorem**. So **alien-universality stays Class-C; route-(b) lifts it to a conditional theorem, not a forced one.**

**The single thing that would close it:** an independent justification that a coherent air-gapped channel's distinguishability metric *must* be positive-definite/anisotropic (a Euclidean/probability-amplitude norm, not a Minkowski (4,4) one) **and** an operational (not product-state) composition postulate. No such theorem exists; "air-gapped" formalizes *as* the tensor-product postulate, so the qualifier the claim leans on **is itself the contested selector**, not an independent lever.

**Honest residue:** the `{1,2,4,8}` shelf is genuinely forced and now attested (Class-A); everything past it — division-ness, the specific ℝ/ℂ/ℍ/𝕆, and the 1/3/7 blocks the framework reads onto biology — rests on the anisotropy hinge + a disputed selection postulate, and the 2-party requirement actively excludes 𝕆. Route-(b) is **blocked, not open** — the residue is a stated assumption + an open physics question, not a missing lemma we can supply. That is the honest, no-leaning verdict.
