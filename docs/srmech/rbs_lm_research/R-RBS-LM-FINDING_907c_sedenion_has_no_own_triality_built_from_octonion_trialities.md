## The question + the known hazard
F907b proved the **octonion** product realises the order-3 triality `{x, y, x·y}` (Cartan residual = 0, τ exactly order-3). The next thread: does the **sedenion** (𝕊 = 𝕆², the literal 2D_oct addressing rung) realise its own Cartan-triality step when it couples its two octonion halves — an internal k=3 at the addressing layer? **Hazard (respected):** triality is **unique to dimension 8 / SO(8)** — there is no SO(16) triality — and sedenions are **not a composition algebra** (F906: `|ab| ≠ |a||b|`, the prerequisite for the triality/Cartan structure). So the rigorous expectation is **no own sedenion triality**.

## Measured (srmech rc13, exact `Fraction`)
**(1) The sedenion breaks composition (the triality prerequisite):** `|ab|²/(|a|²|b|²)` over 60 random sedenion pairs = mean **0.999**, spread **0.167**. The non-zero spread = individual products deviate from norm-multiplicativity (zero divisors, content-dependent — matching F906's 0.986±0.19). **No composition algebra ⇒ no own Cartan triality.**

**(2) The sedenion product IS built from octonion sub-products:** srmech's Cayley-Dickson convention is the standard **`(a,b)(c,d) = (a c − d̄ b , d a + b c̄)`** — verified by **exact match** (`cd_mult` on the full 16-tuple == the octonion-sub-product formula, every coordinate). So the sedenion product is assembled from **four octonion products** (`ac`, `d̄b`, `da`, `bc̄`), each of which **is** a Cartan octonion-triality (F907b).

## The answer
**The sedenion has NO triality of its own** (it is not a composition algebra; triality is dim-8/SO(8)-unique). Its product is **built from octonion-triality sub-products** plus the **zero-divisor coupling** — and that zero-divisor break IS the **addressing feature** (F465: no division ⇒ navigate the 16-named-slot box). So:
- **k=3 triality lives at the OCTONION rung** — the content-dependent bond (F906) and the (2+1) addressing triality (F907b) are both the octonion `cd_mult`.
- **The SEDENION rung adds the ADDRESSING** — the zero-divisor 16-slot navigable box (F465), **not** a second triality.
- The addressing layer = **octonion-triality content carried in a sedenion address-box.** Different rungs, different roles — consistent with F906 (chemistry @ 𝕆, addressing @ 𝕊).

## Consequence (refines F907/F907b)
The "(2+1) = k=3" addressing triality is realised at the **octonion** level (the `cd_mult` of two octonions producing the third rep), **not** at the sedenion level. The sedenion's two octonion halves (𝕊 = 𝕆²) each carry the octonion triality; the sedenion **couples + addresses** them (zero-divisor) without a new triality. So the hypothesised "internal sedenion k=3" is **not** a second triality — it is the octonion triality of the sub-products, boxed in the sedenion address-space. This keeps the rung-roles clean: **order @ ℍ, triality/chemistry @ 𝕆, addressing @ 𝕊** (F906).
