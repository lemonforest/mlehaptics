## The reading (why this closes F766)
- **The leads ARE definitions — structurally, read-independent.** 46% carry a copula in the first 6 tokens (the genus-differentia "X is a Y" form) and 33% open with the title word. That is a real (if imperfect — Wikipedia leads aren't *all* clean) definitional resource: the corpus already contains a broad word→definition map, no separate dictionary needed.
- **F766 depends on F768 — the gate is the enabling mechanism, not decoration.** Every definition shares the function-word skeleton ("… is a … of the …"), so *ungated* the definition-vectors carry a common component and fuse (Gram 0.337, well above the 0.25 orthogonal baseline). The F768 aboutness-gate strips that skeleton, pulling the vectors to 0.277 (≈baseline) — read-independently, ~70% of the excess correlation removed. This is the read-independent structural verdict *before* any retrieval: the gate makes the definitions distinguishable at all.
- **The depth read then works — and the gate lifts it.** Associative recall of the right definition from a 3-content-word cue (the "describe X → give me its definition" query) goes 63%→77% with the gate. The dictionary is retrievable by partial content, which is exactly the depth-read use (a why-asker gives a fuzzy description, gets the definition back).
- **Open-vocabulary is handled by usage-fallback.** A word with no article gets its depth read from gated co-occurrence — `ocean → pacific atlantic north island` is a genuine meaning-from-use. So the define-intent is *open*: title-words get the stored lead, non-title words get the usage-derived read. This is the "open-vocabulary intent" F766 asked for.

## Honest scope
Read-independent (A)/(B) are solid. (A) definitional form is *moderate* (46%/33%) — the leads are substantially but not universally clean definitions; a copula slightly past token-6 ("the pacific ocean is …") is missed by the strict window, so the true definitional rate is a floor. (C) associative recall uses each def's own first-3 content words as the cue (associative completion — the right test for a depth-read lookup, but IN-vocab, not open). (C-open) usage-fallback surfaces the right content (ocean→pacific/atlantic/island) **but leaks some function words** (`in/on/it/to`) — the lead-based doc-freq gate threshold (0.6·N) is too high for short 25-token leads (function words don't reach 60%-of-leads there); tuning the threshold or gating on full-body doc-freq fixes it (tunable, not structural).
