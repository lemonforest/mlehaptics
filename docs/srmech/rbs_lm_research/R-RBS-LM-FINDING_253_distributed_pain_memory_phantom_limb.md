## §2 Phantom limb — the brain-centering assumption is being walked back (the user's exact target)

- **Brain-centered (older):** Melzack neuromatrix (1990); Ramachandran cortical-remapping (1992/1995) — "maladaptive remapping causes phantom pain."
- **The correction (verified):** **Makin et al. 2015** (*Brain*) — *no* statistical relationship between cortical reorganization and phantom pain. **Kikkert et al. 2018** (*Cortex*) — worse phantom pain correlates with the **maintained/preserved** missing-hand representation, **not** reorganization (inverts the maladaptive-plasticity story). **Vaso/Devor 2014** — **DRG lidocaine extinguishes** the phantom (a peripheral origin). **Collins 2018** (JCI): "the PNS must now be considered a viable component of any theory of PLP." **Wu 2025 / Satala 2026** frame PLP as explicitly multilevel/distributed.
- **Honest residue:** the genuine open question is the *relative weight* of the levels, not "peripheral vs central" as a binary. Held both poles, ranked neither. Uncertainties carried, unresolved: spinal-LTP autonomy (Sandkühler does not claim full autonomy); sex-dimorphism is animal-model; DRG epigenome largely rodent; DRG-stim clinical evidence is case-series not RCT; **congenital-amputee phantom pain (no prior limb) is unaccounted-for by any current theory, neuromatrix included** — a clean open gap the twin surfaced.
