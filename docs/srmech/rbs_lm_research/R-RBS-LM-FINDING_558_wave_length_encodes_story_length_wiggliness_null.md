## Verdict
**The length half is confirmed and load-bearing.** A longer drive yields a proportionally longer story — story length tracks the wave length *exactly*. The **wave is the author**: the wave-driven live slice (not raw corpus fluency) selects ~90% of the tokens — the *signal*, not the stored corpus, authors the telling; the corpus only gates fluency. So the wave is the input **message**, the collapse-weave + manifold is the **decoder**, and the story is the **readout** — and the message's **length** sets the output's extent. A droplet (short) → a short story; a long song or continuous environmental noise → a long one (F556).

**The wiggliness half is an honest null here.** A wigglier wave of the same length does **not** measurably change story length or wave-influence (length flat at 80; influence 88–98%, no clean frequency trend) — the manifold + fluency carry the story regardless of how wiggly the drive is. So the user's "longer *wiggly* wave" splits: **length** is the dominant, confirmed encoder; **wiggliness** as a separate shape knob is washed out by the substrate at this scale (it may re-emerge at larger vocab / finer windows — a held-open next rung).
