## The question (F438's flagged gap)

## The mechanism
The cross-tile binding of two quaternion tiles `p`, `q` is their product `p·conj(q)`:
- **real part = alignment** — how much the two tiles *agree* (the 4-D inner product);
- **imaginary part = twist** — the *relational rotation* between them (the cross-product; the chirality of the binding).
