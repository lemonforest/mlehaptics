## The reframe (forward = wrap, recall = unwrap)
| direction | operation | on the geometry |

| **forward time** (inference, F964) | **wrap** — add a new NOW; older beats become higher `q^n` terms, receding at the rung-rate | the past nests backward (F972); memoryless-geometric (F975) |

Recall is not lookup; it is **running the recession in reverse** to reconstruct a past NOW — *simulated backward time-travel*.
