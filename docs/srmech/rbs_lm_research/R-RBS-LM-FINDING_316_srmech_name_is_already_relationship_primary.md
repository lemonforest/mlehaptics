## Linguistic analysis — word-order-first ≠ primary; the HEAD is primary (and English puts it last)
In an English compound, the **head** (the noun it is *about*) is the **rightmost** noun; earlier words modify it.
- **"Stored-Relationship"** — "stored" = past-participle **adjective**; "relationship" = **noun head** → **relationship is the operative/primary noun**, "stored" is its attribute. *A relationship, of the stored kind.*
- **"Relationship Storage"** — "relationship" = **noun-adjunct (modifier)**; "storage" = **noun head** → **storage becomes the operative noun**, relationship demoted to "what kind." *Storage, of the relationship kind.*
**So the user's "-ed" intuition is exactly right:** the "-ed" subordinates "store" to an adjective, **keeping "relationship" the head.** The current name is *already* relationship-primary; the proposed "fix" would **demote** relationship. Putting it first as a modifier loses it the head slot.
