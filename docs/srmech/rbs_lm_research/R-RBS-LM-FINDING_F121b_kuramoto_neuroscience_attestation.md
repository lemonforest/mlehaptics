## §2 What the literature DOES support vs what it does NOT

### The literature DOES support (Tier 3 — their claims):

1. **Neural oscillations are real and pervasive.** Cortical networks oscillate across ~five orders of magnitude in frequency, and these oscillations are phylogenetically preserved and behavior-dependent (Buzsáki & Draguhn 2004; book-length treatment Buzsáki 2006).
2. **Synchronization / phase-coherence is a real, actively-studied brain mechanism** — proposed for large-scale integration ("the brainweb," Varela et al. 2001), for flexible inter-group communication ("communication through coherence," Fries 2005; with empirical support in Womelsdorf et al. 2007), and as a relational code (Singer 1999). *These are influential, well-cited frameworks; "communication through coherence" specifically is an active research program, not closed consensus — see the tiering in §3.*
3. **The Kuramoto model is a standard model OF brain dynamics.** This is the load-bearing attestation for F121/F122: neuroscientists *do* describe inter-regional brain interaction with "the Kuramoto model of phase oscillators, a biologically-based model of coupled oscillatory systems" (Cabral et al. 2011, verbatim from the abstract) and *do* derive neurobiological implications from it (Breakspear et al. 2010). Large-scale resting-state dynamics are modeled with exactly this class of coupled-oscillator dynamics (Deco, Jirsa & McIntosh 2011). **So "wet neural nets / brains are well-described as coupled oscillators, and Kuramoto is a standard model for that" is LITERATURE-SUPPORTED.**
4. **Phase coding is documented** (O'Keefe & Recce 1993).

### The literature does NOT support (Tier 2 — ours, flagged as reframe):

1. **The specific 4:3:7 packaging.** No neuroscience source partitions brain dynamics into "4:3:7" or into A-N operator classes. This is the framework's vocabulary, full stop.
2. **The K_c = 0.20 anchor as a *biological* energy floor.** K_c≈0.20 is a property of *our specific N=4 srmech simulation* (Tier 1), not a measured biological coupling threshold. The literature studies critical coupling in the Kuramoto model abstractly; it does not assign 0.20 to any cnidarian or cortical system. Reading K_c as "the biological energy floor for the operational core" is a Tier-2 reframe.
3. **The RBS-NN two-tier reading** (Tier 1 = "4-core / frequency-topology," Tier 2 = "the 7 / information-content," Class-K as the lift). This is the framework's architecture, not a neuroscience model.
4. **"Cortex *simulates* a discrete cyclic operational core."** The literature documents oscillations, phase coding, and synchronization as *real* phenomena; it does **not** frame cortex as "simulating" an upstream discrete-cyclic substrate. That up/down-stream ("shadow") ordering is the framework's reading (F120/F121 §7), not a neuroscience claim.
5. **The cnidarian "multiples of four" as an A+B+H+N operational unit.** Radial symmetry in cnidarian pacemaker organization is a biological observation; mapping it onto a specific operator quartet is form-iso speculation that F121 §9 already flags as such. *(The cnidarian pacemaker observation itself was cited in F118/F121 to a PLOS ONE box-jellyfish paper; that specific source is **not** re-verified here — see §3 FLAGGED — so this supplement does not vouch for it.)*
