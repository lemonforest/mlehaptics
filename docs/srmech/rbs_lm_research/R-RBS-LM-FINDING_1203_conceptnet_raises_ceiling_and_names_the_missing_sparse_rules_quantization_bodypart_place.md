## Reading — the ceiling rose, and three missing rules (one load-bearing) surfaced

1. **The ceiling is raised.** Layering ConceptNet nearly doubles coverage (35%→69%). The determinative dictionary should be **grammar cues ∪ ConceptNet IsA** (each covers what the other misses) — a concrete upgrade to F1201/F1202's lexicon.
2. **The missing ruleset parts, ranked:**
   - **① MASS/COUNT (quantization) — the load-bearing gap.** Our cues have NO abstract-positive signal, so abstracts collapse into "thing" (the 140+84 cells). The framework-resonant core is the mass/count determinative: English marks **count** (a/an + plural → discrete/concrete) vs **mass** (some/much + no-plural → continuous/substance/abstract). That is the *discrete-vs-continuous* axis the whole substrate arc turns on ([[stance_bit_exact_is_phase_locked_cyclic_slots_not_flat]]), grammaticalized as the a/some determinative — and we were not harvesting it. Adding the mass/count cue (does the lemma take "a/an" + occur plural, vs take "some/much" + never plural) is the single highest-value missing rule.
   - **② BODYPART / PART-OF (bridging) — a missing CLASS.** eyes/head/voice/mouth/hand/side/top are a distinct determinative class our flat 4-way scheme lumps into "thing"; the PART-OF relation (a body-part is *part-of* a person) is the bridging rule (F1200) and is *exactly* why F1197's "hand" broke coref (it's a thing owned by a person, taking possessives "his hand"). Add BODYPART as a class + a PART-OF gate.
   - **③ PLACE positive detection.** court/sea/kingdom/streets → "thing" because we only had the "where" relativizer + -land suffix; add locative-preposition cues (in/at/to X, come/go to X).
3. **Honest — the oracle is a noisy ceiling.** person→thing (mother/brother/uncle/child/people) is our grammar cue being *right* and ConceptNet's kinship IsA failing to reach the person anchor. So ~a third of the "disagreements" are oracle noise, not our gaps — the layered lexicon must trust the grammar cue for kinship/relational terms. ConceptNet raises coverage but is not ground truth.
