## The reading (what the Laplacian shape says)
- **The Laplacian IS the right instrument, three ways.** `magnetic_laplacian` (directed) would lift λ_min off 0 on a directed cycle (gauge-invariant flux); `signed_laplacian` (+is-a/−is-not-a) lifts λ_min off 0 on a frustrated mixed-sign cycle; SCC/Tarjan gives the combinatorial cycles. Together they are a complete **is-a consistency audit**.
- **simplewiki's is-a knowledge is globally consistent.** Acyclic (no "X is a Y is a X"), zero transitivity violations (no "X is-a Y is-a Z but X is-not-a Z"), and signed-balanced (λ_min=0 → the +/− assertions are 2-colorable → no frustrated loop). Three independent structural tests all say **consistent**.
- **The audit caught its own extraction bug** — the honest read-independent-check moment. Naive first-token node keys produced false cycles (common words like `to`/`united` collide with titles that start with them); auditing them showed 100% were artifacts. Clean full-title keys → acyclic. The cycle detector is a *self-validating* extraction check: spurious cycles flag extraction errors, not corpus contradictions.
- **The "X is not a Y" shapes ARE present — and they are dual-SENSE, not error.** 672 explicit negations exist; 9 concepts are asserted BOTH is-a and is-not-a the same genus. These are **sense-disambiguations**: *Brown is a color* (pigment) but *is not a spectral color*; *a prefix is a word-part* but *is not a word*; *chemistry is a science* but *is not an exact science*. The signed Laplacian treats a same-edge +/− as **net-neutral (balanced)** — which is exactly correct: a dual-sense annotation is not a logical frustration, it is two senses. Only a mixed-sign *cycle* (transitivity break) would be frustration, and there are none.
