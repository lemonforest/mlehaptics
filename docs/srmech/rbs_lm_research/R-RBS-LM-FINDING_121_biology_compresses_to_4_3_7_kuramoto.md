## §2 Why Kuramoto math forces the 4-packaging

Kuramoto coupled-oscillator dynamics:

$$\frac{d\theta_i}{dt} = \omega_i + \frac{K}{N}\sum_j \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i)$$

Each oscillator's phase $\theta_i$ has no absolute meaning — it only
exists RELATIVE to the other phases in the ensemble. There is no
"anchor sitting still" + "operations happening to it" because:

- The anchor IS the phase reference (defined collectively by the
  ensemble)
- The operations (binding-via-coupling-K, doing-via-phase-shift,
  moving-via-frequency-ω-modulation) ARE the relative phase
  relationships
- Anchor and operations are inseparable in this substrate

Order parameter:

$$r \cdot e^{i\psi} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_j e^{i\theta_j}$$

The order parameter captures both the synchronization magnitude (r)
and the collective phase ($\psi$). Both are properties of the
ENSEMBLE, not of any individual oscillator.

**Implication**: in any coupled-oscillator substrate, the smallest
functional unit must include both anchor and operations. That's the
"4" = A + B + H + N as one inseparable operational core.
