## The reading (the design, and two honest catches)
- **Operators declared, operands by meaning — the router IS that memory made operational.** Intent is carried by closed-class operator frames: define-interrogatives (`what is / define / describe / explain…`), self-verbs (`remember/recall/forget/ingest/save` — the seed of siona's own CLI surface, INFER-4), the address (`siona …`), imperatives (`list/compute/register/…`), and the **operand shape** (F1009: numbers/bytes present = strong tool-call evidence — so `what is the gcd of 1071 and 462` correctly routes tool-call despite the define frame, while `what is a laplacian` routes define). Continue is the **default** — no operator frame, no operands, i.e. a dangling content prefix. This is legitimately rule-declared per `[[feedback_operators_declared_operands_by_meaning]]` (operators are reserved keywords; F770 showed you cannot derive them from meaning) and does NOT conflict with F768 (which replaced a *content* stoplist with a measured gate).
- **Honest catch #1 — the grounding threshold was measured and REJECTED.** The first cut routed no-operand utterances by max-sim to the 347-tool index ≥ THR. Measurement: tool-asks 0.40–0.56 vs continues 0.35–0.45 — **overlapping** (max-of-347 is inflated for short queries). All 4 continues misrouted. Rather than tune the threshold, the signal was rejected outright and replaced with the declared imperative operators — the correct move per the structure-check discipline (a non-separable signal doesn't become separable by tuning).
- **Honest catch #2 — the bag bit a THIRD time, inside the continue handler.** Hand-rolled trigram binding `bind(bind(a,b),ROLE)→next` with commutative Klein-4 bind made `(lives,in)→the` **alias** to `(in,the)→lives` (self-inverse XOR algebra: `lives⊗in⊗the ⊗ in⊗the = lives`), so "the pope lives in the" read `lives` instead of `vatican`. The substrate's own **position-keyed** `cs.encode_context(window)` (F838) is the fix — order enters via position keys, the aliasing vanishes, and `vatican` reads correctly. Introspect-before-dispatch: the right op was already shipped.
- **One dispatcher, one substrate.** The dispatch demo is the rc1-gate shape in miniature: a remembered fact becomes continuation content; a tool result lands in the same memory; `siona show your working memory` reads both back. INFER-5 (multi-turn coherence) is this loop sustained; INFER-4 is the self-verb surface formalized.
