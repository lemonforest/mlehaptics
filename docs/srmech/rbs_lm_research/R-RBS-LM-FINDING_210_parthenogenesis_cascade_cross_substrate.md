## §1 The biology (sourced) — the minimal trigger of parthenogenetic development

Parthenogenesis is development from an egg **without fertilization**. Stripped to its minimal cascade, the trigger is **egg activation minus the sperm**:

1. **A self-initiated Ca²⁺ wave / oscillation.** A large, propagating intracellular Ca²⁺ rise is the near-universal activator of the egg across animal species, and the Ca²⁺ increase alone is **sufficient** to cause "all the concomitants of egg activation" — calcium ionophore (no sperm) "can trigger the transient and produce parthenotes" (Whitaker 2006, *Physiol. Rev.* 86:25–88). The external agent (the sperm-borne factor, PLCζ in mammals) is **not** what the cascade fundamentally requires; it is one way to push the egg past its own threshold. In *Drosophila* the trigger is not the sperm at all: "*Drosophila* do not require fertilisation to trigger activation" — passage through the female reproductive tract drives a **single Ca²⁺ wave** via osmotic swelling / mechano-sensitive channels, which "co-ordinates key developmental events including resumption of the cell cycle" (York-Andersen et al. 2015, *Biology Open* 4:553–560). This is the cleanest natural existence-proof that the trigger is **internal / self-initiated**, not the external partner.
2. **Completion of meiosis II.** The Ca²⁺ signal relieves metaphase-II arrest by activating the **anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C)**, which drives cyclin-B degradation and lets the cell cycle proceed (Whitaker 2006). The arrested egg **finishes the division it had paused**.
3. **Diploidy restoration.** Lacking a paternal genome, the parthenote restores diploidy from **its own** material — **automixis**: terminal/central fusion of meiotic products, or gamete duplication (a round of chromosome replication, or a mitosis, without the corresponding division). Terminal fusion is "the only confirmed mechanism of facultative parthenogenesis in vertebrates" (Lutes-style automixis literature; confirmed genome-wide in king-cobra facultative parthenogenesis, Card et al. 2021, *PMC8012631*; automixis modes reviewed in Engelstädter 2017, *PMC5499200*).
4. **Cell-cycle resumption → development proceeds.** Once committed, the embryonic program runs.

**The abstraction.** Strip the species detail and the cascade is: a system held in a **metastable, arrested state** is pushed past a **threshold by an internal/self-supplied perturbation** (not an external partner); the crossing **commits** it (all-or-none — the Ca²⁺ transient is *sufficient*); it then **completes a division/replication it had paused** and **restores its own full state from its own material**, after which the program runs autonomously. In one phrase: a **self-initiated symmetry-break / autocatalytic completion that needs no external trigger.** Two abstract features are load-bearing: (i) the trigger is a **latching threshold-crossing** (a zero that, once passed, commits and does not slide back); (ii) the restoration is **autocatalytic / self-supplied** (the system completes itself from itself).

## §4 Pre-stated falsifiers (what would break the cross-substrate match)

- **The latch falsifier (already wired into the smoke).** If egg activation were empirically a **freely reversible** crossing — the egg sliding back and forth across the activation threshold without committing — the **Class K latching** reading would fail (it is the `reversible_control_latched = False` case). The match relies on activation being **all-or-none / committed**; reversible activation falsifies it. *(Whitaker 2006 supports all-or-none: the Ca²⁺ transient is sufficient and drives the program forward via irreversible cyclin degradation.)*
- **The "no external trigger" falsifier.** If parthenogenetic activation **always** required an external chemical/physical agent the egg could not supply itself, the "self-initiated, needs-no-external-trigger" abstraction would collapse toward the **CA stretch** (externally authored). *Drosophila* (self-triggered by tract passage; York-Andersen 2015) and ionophore parthenotes (Whitaker 2006) are the evidence the trigger is **internal/self-suppliable**; a finding that natural parthenogenesis is impossible without an irreducibly-external agent would falsify it.
- **The metastable-barrier falsifier (for the STRONG physics matches).** The vacuum-decay / crystallization match **requires a metastable state behind a barrier with a critical-threshold crossing**. If the analogy were pushed onto a process with **no barrier / no critical size** (a smooth, gradient-following relaxation — i.e. a *second-order* transition with no nucleation), the "latching crossing" feature would be absent and the match would degrade to PARTIAL/STRETCH. The match is specifically to **first-order / nucleated** transitions; a barrier-free reading falsifies it.
- **The single-axis (C) falsifier.** If diploidy restoration in parthenotes routinely required **two opposite-handed** genomic contributions (a genuine mirror pair) rather than self-supplied single-handed material via automixis, the **one-homochiral-axis (Class C)** reading (F176) would be contradicted. Automixis (terminal fusion / gamete duplication from the egg's own meiotic products; Card et al. 2021; Engelstädter 2017) is the evidence diploidy is restored **single-handedly**.
