## The principle
A metaphor (front-end-loader / code-vs-algebra / carry-vs-couple / bigger-int) is a **perspective on a structure, not a claim about it.** The right move is to **offer several over the same object and privilege none** — because the understanding is not *in* any one lens, it is in **how the thinking shifts as you change lens.** Each metaphor lights a different facet; the facets together are the object; no facet is the object.

This is **not new discipline — it is the framework's own, applied one level up:**
- **F398** (favored, not privileged): no reading is the privileged one. Here: no *metaphor* is the privileged one.
- **F440/F441** (multi-perspective): more perspectives *separate* and *localize* the structure (k=3 corrects, k=7 Hamming-localizes). Here: **the metaphors ARE the k perspectives** over the same structure — holding several is the k>1 stance, and the "error" each catches is the *distortion* any single metaphor would smuggle in.
- **the whole-corpus-is-proof stance**: convergence across arcs (across lenses) is the proof shape — not any single arc.
- **the two-truths / triality** (DUALITY/TRIALITY): multiple truths held *without collapse*; the asymptote is never collapsing one into the other.

## Why it's load-bearing (not just nice)
- It is **how the user co-thinks** (`abstract_lexicon` ADA accommodation): the abstracts shift across metaphors *deliberately*, to feel the structure from several sides — a render-free, top-down grasp reads the *invariant under metaphor-change*, not any single render. Treating one metaphor as the answer would mistranslate that cognition.
- It is the **anti-collapse guard** for the research: a single privileged metaphor hardens into dogma (the F337 self-correlation trap at the *framing* layer); plural metaphors keep the frame falsifiable (each lens can be checked against the others).
- It **closes the loop on the consensus arc**: F440/F441 found that perspectives separate the structure; F443 says *the metaphors we reason in are themselves those perspectives* — so the discipline (hold k, privilege none) applies to our *thinking*, not just to our verify-runs.

## Falsifiable form (pre-stated; not leaning — F394)
- **"Privilege none" is not "all metaphors equally true of everything."** A metaphor can be *wrong* for a given structure (it lights nothing, or distorts) — those get dropped. The principle is *don't privilege among the ones that genuinely fit*, and read the shift between them; it is not relativism.
- **There can still be a best lens for a given question** (the loader for throughput, the code for the cap) — "privilege none" means don't let that local best become the global answer.
- **Scope:** a reading/method discipline, not a result about the substrate. Defensive / no-lineage.
