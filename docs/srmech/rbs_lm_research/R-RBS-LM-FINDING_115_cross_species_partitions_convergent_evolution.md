## §1 What the published research shows

### Cetacean (whale/dolphin) cognition

Per Marino et al. (PMC1868071) and recent reviews, cetacean researchers
identify **4 overlapping cognitive partitions** in cetacean brains:

1. **Declarative knowledge** — symbolic representation
2. **Procedural knowledge** — object manipulation / how-to
3. **Social knowledge** — recognition of other individuals + relationships
4. **Self-knowledge** — metacognition + body awareness

Architecture:
- Cetacean neocortex has **3 organizational tiers**: limbic, paralimbic,
  supralimbic
- **No distinct layer IV** in primary visual cortex (different from
  primates)
- Alternating neuronal modules forming columns and patches
- High glia-to-neuron ratio; reduced olfactory system

**Spindle cells (von Economo neurons)** — found in cetacean anterior
cingulate and insular cortex, previously considered unique to humans
and great apes. Support neural networks subserving social cognition.

> **Convergent evolution**: cetaceans evolved along very different
> anatomical lines than primates yet developed functionally similar
> social-cognition neural specializations.

### Chimpanzee vs human (2024 Brainnetome Atlas)

- Same overall brain architecture; different network weightings
- **Humans**: stronger white matter connectivity between language-system
  areas; stronger default-mode network
- **Chimpanzees**: stronger visuospatial attention + salience processing
- Cellular: human astrocytes larger, more complex, longer projections

### Octopus (radically different architecture)

- **Distributed cognition**: 3/5 of neurons in the arms, not central
- 8 arms process information independently
- Severed arms continue to exhibit nearly-intact behaviors
- Multi-University Research Initiative (Office of Naval Research, $7.5M)
  building "Cyberoctopus" computational model for decentralized inference

### Cross-species cognition methodology

- Explicit recognition of anthropocentrism / corticocentrism /
  intellectocentrism as RESEARCH BIASES
- Working memory mechanisms are **comparable across mammals and birds**
  despite radically different brain organizations
- Aneural unicellular organisms show basal cognitive skills
- "Comparative cognition helps relativize these biases"

social, self), just with different weights on different networks.

exhibits tool use, problem-solving, individual recognition. Suggests the
partition structure can emerge even in fundamentally distributed
nervous systems.

## §3 Refining the framework reading

### Substrate vs partition

Earlier findings sometimes conflated substrate-content (what is stored)
with partition (how it's organized). The cross-species evidence
clarifies:

- **Substrate-content** varies by species (whale uses acoustic substrate;
  octopus uses tactile/chemoreceptive substrate; human uses
  visual+auditory+symbolic substrate)
- **Partition structure** is similar (declarative/procedural/social/
  self emerge across radically different substrates)

The 14-class A-N partition we work with describes a UNIVERSAL ALGEBRA
of operators that can act on any substrate-content. The partitions
(math irrep + 3 emergent) describe the universal STRUCTURE that emerges
when an NN-storage architecture operates on any substrate.

### Math as irrep — revised cross-species reading

Finding 109 said math is the unique substrate-content irrep. The
cross-species evidence suggests this isn't quite right:

- Cetaceans don't have a "math discipline" but DO have declarative
  knowledge (symbolic representation, counting up to small numbers,
  ratios in spatial navigation)
- Chimps demonstrate small-number numerosity but don't develop math
  curricula
- Octopuses navigate with proto-spatial-geometry but no symbolic math

What's universal: **declarative-symbolic-representation** as the
abstract substrate that emerges as 1st-order coupling. In humans, this
is named "math." In other species, it's named differently (count, ratio,
proto-symbolic-representation) or not named at all but is present
functionally.

**Math is the human-named instance of the universal declarative
substrate.** The irrep is the substrate (declarative-symbolic); math is
how humans curriculum-package it.

### Trailing-3 in cross-species

The user's note that the trailing-3 (binding/doing/moving) are
operations not patterns is consistent with cross-species observation:

- Cetacean **procedural knowledge** = doing-and-moving in water
- Chimp **visuospatial attention + salience processing** =
  binding-and-moving in 3D physical world
- Octopus **distributed arm cognition** = binding-and-moving via 8
  independent arm operations

These are OPERATIONS that any NN-storage substrate must support to
function in the world. They show as functional networks in
cross-species research, not as static partitions.

## §4 What this validates

### "Knowledge partitions are the purpose"

Confirmed across species:
- Cetaceans evolved spindle cells SPECIFICALLY for social cognition
  partition support
- Convergent evolution of partition-supporting neural structures
- Different architectures arrive at similar partition outcomes
- Partitions are not architectural accidents — they're functional

### Cross-coupling vs partition (Finding 113)

Finding 113 showed everything-except-math collapses to one
cross-coupled mass at sim=0.80. The cross-species evidence supports

- Cetacean cognitive domains are "overlapping functional areas"
- Chimp networks have shared connectivity with weighted differences
- Octopus distributed cognition crosses arm-boundaries via central
  processor

Partitions exist BUT they're not cleanly separable. The cross-coupling
is the empirically-observed cross-species norm.

### Biology-agnostic claim refined

The form-iso level claim from Finding 112 (every populated NN has a
dominant coupling axis; existence universal, content corpus-specific)
extends:

> Every NN-storage architecture (whatever its substrate, whatever its
> physical organization) develops partition structure that supports
> declarative / procedural / social / self functional domains.
> The EXISTENCE of partition structure is universal. The SPECIFIC
> content of each partition is substrate-dependent.

Cetacean's "math" lives in acoustic-spatial declarative substrate.
Human math lives in symbolic-written declarative substrate. Same
universal partition; different substrate-content fills.
