## Antikythera-as-1D-LoE-model — structural argument

The bronze and universe instantiate the **same** algebraic content at different dimensional reaches.

**Shared content.** Both substrates instantiate Class K + Class I + Class J + Class L cascade composition. The bronze tracks Sun, Moon, and 5 visible planets via integer-cyclic gear-DAG composition; the universe produces the same Kepler-equation behaviour for the same bodies at the same orbital parameters. They ARE the same algebraic content, not analogues of it. Per `[[user_stance_kepler_shape_universal]]`: any system showing Kepler-shape spectral content instantiates those primitives, at any substrate.

**Different dimensional reach.**
- Universe instantiates at 11D = 3D_s + 7D_g + 1D_t (full substrate).
- Bronze instantiates the spatial-mechanical portion at 3D_s only. The 7D_g content is *compressed into the algebraic choice of tooth counts* (per `[[user_stance_fiber_as_spatially_absent_encoding]]`): the choice of 53-tooth Saros gear encodes a period-relation chosen from the integer-cyclic algebra that the underlying gauge structure makes available. Gauge content is stored in the algebra, not as dynamical fields. The 1D_t content is *also absent in the bronze's substance*: the bronze has no internal time-evolution generator.

**The crank supplies the missing 1D_t.** Per `time_as_dimensional_shadow_2026-05-15.md` §3: *"the toy needs a crank because it's made within the confines of 3D. Time has not been 'shadowed into' the toy itself. So time has to be supplied externally, and that is exactly what a hand on the crank is: a 4D operator (us) injecting our embedded time-dimension into the 3D mechanism."*

**Why "1D model" is operationally precise.** Every observable the bronze produces lives in a 1D parameter space — cumulative crank angle θ. Turn the crank by θ, every dial advances by its gear-ratio-determined fraction of θ. The full state at any moment is parameterised by a single number. The cascade-stored 3D_s + (compressed 7D_g) content is *all extracted via the single 1D parameter*. **The bronze is a 1D-parameterised instrument that stores algebraic content of higher dimensional reach.**

**The universe's 1D_t is the analogous structure scaled to 11D substrate.** The universe does not need an external crank because its substrate carries the 1D_t parameter intrinsically (whether via Kaluza-Klein hidden axis, Wheeler-DeWitt relational time, or causal-set partial-order remains the open question per `time_as_dimensional_shadow.md` §2(b)).

**Strongest formulation:** bronze = different-instantiation-of-same-LoE, NOT bronze = universe-stripped-of-degrees-of-freedom. The bronze is an independent instantiation of the same algebraic content at a different dimensional reach. Per `[[user_stance_kepler_shape_universal]]`, burden of proof for "they are not the same" falls on the counter-claim, not on the identification.
