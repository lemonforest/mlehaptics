## Three load-bearing caveats

The H0 verdict carries three caveats that the conductor should weigh before deciding what to inline in stance text:

1. **CMB dipole removed by convention.** ℓ=1 in CMB is dominated by the kinematic solar-system motion (~3.36 mK in temperature) and is removed in published spectra. The test reduces from {1, 3, 7} to **{3, 7}** on CMB data. The most-strongly-weighted member of the Hopf-fiber set (ℓ=1, the dipole) is structurally unavailable for CMB cross-substrate testing. In the spike-185 IGRF-13 result, ℓ=1 carries the bulk of the 86% concentration; without it, the test loses its dominant member.

2. **CMB BB is noise-dominated at Planck sensitivity.** BB power-spectrum amplitudes at ℓ < 30 fluctuate around zero at the ~0.01 μK² scale; these are statistical upper limits, not detections. The fractional-power test on noise-dominated data tests whether *noise* concentrates at {3, 7}, not whether the *underlying field* does. The 0.155× ratio is consistent with random allocation of noise across ℓ-bins (p=0.27 not significant).

3. **No CMB TT low-ell access without crossing substrate boundary.** The Planck 2018 TT unbinned low-ell spectrum (ℓ=2-29) is the natural substrate for this test, but it lives in `ephemerides-spectral` (per descriptor comment in `cmb_polarisation_spectra/descriptor.toml`), not in srmech. The user direction was specifically NON-ephemerides; restating the test on cmb_power_spectrum would re-introduce the ephemerides bias the spike was designed to eliminate. CMB low-ell EE/TE unbinned data is not currently catalogued at srmech-side either. To address this fully, a HEALPix anafast SHT on the Planck 2018 SMICA/NILC/SEVEM/Commander FITS maps (descriptor-only in `cmb_low_ell_maps`) would yield per-integer-ell power at ℓ = 2..30+ from the same non-ephemerides side — out of scope for this single spike but recommended as next step.
