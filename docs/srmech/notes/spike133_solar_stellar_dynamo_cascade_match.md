### §1.1 Class L — Convective-shell MHD Laplacian (substrate-specific operations: plasma MHD, tachocline, differential rotation)

The solar dynamo solves the **Navier-Stokes + induction + thermal-convection MHD system** in a rotating spherical shell — but the shell is fundamentally different from the geodynamo's. - **Long magnetic cycles**: 0.15 ≲ Ro_f ≲ 0.65
- **Short cycles**: Ro_f ≲ 0.42
- **Steady (anti-solar-like)**: Ro_f ≳ 1

The plasma-MHD substrate occupies a **convective spherical shell bounded by tachocline + photosphere** — no inner solid core required. Per **Wang, Jiang, Luo 2025** ([arXiv:2506.01416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01416)), surface flux transport simulations reproduce *"axial dipole strength, polar field reversal timing, and magnetic butterfly diagram"* for cycles 24–25 in good agreement with SDO/HMI observations — the same toroidal/spheroidal decomposition operation that Aubert et al. 2025 (Spike #131 §1.3) applied to the geodynamo, but on plasma-MHD substrate.
