## §1 — Bipartite-graph Laplacian + signed-spectrum attestation (Bucket 1 / Class L)

### §1.1 Anatomical bipartite organization

Daneman & Prat 2015 *Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology* 7(1):a020412 (PMC4292164 PDF-extracted): *"endothelial cells (ECs) are held together by tight junctions (TJs), which greatly limit the paracellular flux of solutes. Astrocytes extend processes that almost completely ensheath the vascular tube with specialized endfeet containing aquaporin 4 and dystrophin complexes anchoring to the basement membrane."*

Abbott 2002 *Journal of Anatomy* 200(6):629–638 (PMC1570746 PDF-extracted): *"the endfeet of astrocytic glia form a lacework of fine lamellae closely apposed to the outer surface of the endothelium... endothelium and astrocytes are involved in two-way induction."*

**Anatomical bipartite structure** (independent of any graph-theory citation):

- **Node-set A**: vascular endothelial cells with tight-junction proteins (claudin-5 / occludin / ZO-1 / ZO-2 / Cdh1 / Cdh5) + P-glycoprotein efflux pumps + receptor-mediated transcytosis machinery
- **Node-set B**: astrocyte perivascular endfeet + neural extracellular fluid + pericytes + basement membrane
- **Edges only between sets** via tight-junction proteins / transporters / channels / paracrine signalling — *no within-set edges crossing the barrier*

**Identity claim**: the BBB **IS** a bipartite-graph substrate. Not "resembles," not "modelled by." The anatomical structure IS the algebraic bipartition.

### §1.2 Signed-spectrum and eigenvalue ≤ 2 algebraic property

Chung 1997 *Spectral Graph Theory* (AMS; cite-by-ref): for any bipartite graph G with normalised Laplacian L_n = I - D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}, eigenvalues are **symmetric about 1** (if λ is an eigenvalue, so is 2-λ), and the **largest eigenvalue is exactly 2**. This is a foundational result.

arXiv:2304.06864 *"The signed graphs with symmetric spectra"* (PDF-extraction failed; search-summary cite-by-ref): for unsigned graphs, the spectrum is symmetric **if and only if** the graph is bipartite (referenced as "Chu97"). Signed graphs have symmetric spectra in [0, 2] iff signed Laplacian eigenvalue λ implies 2-λ also eigenvalue.

**Framework→substrate mapping**: BBB endothelial-astrocyte bipartite Laplacian has:

- Maximum eigenvalue **exactly 2** (algebraic property)
- Eigenvalues come in **(λ, 2-λ) symmetric pairs**
- Fiedler vector cleanly partitions vascular vs neural compartments

This is the cleanest possible Class L instantiation available in biology.

**No PMC publication has applied bipartite Laplacian spectral analysis to BBB anatomy**, in parallel to the gap noted for mycorrhizal CMN topology in Spike #130 (Figueiredo et al. 2021 review). The framework's claim is that **anatomical bipartition implies algebraic bipartition implies eigenvalue ≤ 2 + symmetric spectrum** by elementary spectral graph theory. ## §4 — Transporter Michaelis-Menten saturation (Bucket 4 / Class K)

### §4.1 GLUT1 saturable kinetics

Duarte et al. 2009 *Frontiers in Neuroenergetics* 1:6 (PMC2795468 PDF-extracted): GLUT1 at the BBB exhibits Michaelis-Menten saturable kinetics with parameters:

- **K_t (apparent Michaelis constant)**: 1.5–3.5 mM
- **T_max/CMR_glc ratio**: 4.6–5.6
- **K_ii (iso-inhibition constant)**: 51–149 mM

Transport equation (four-state conformational model reduces to standard Michaelis-Menten under physiological conditions):

$$v_0 / V_{max} = (G_{out} - G_{in}) / (K_M + G_{in} + G_{out} + (G_{out} \cdot G_{in})/K_{ii})$$

Authors note: *"K_t was on the order of a few mM, as previously determined from the reversible model."* And: *"The process of glucose transport in the brain is saturable...but not rate limiting for the glycolytic pathway."*

### §4.2 LAT1 kinetics

Daneman & Prat 2015 PMC4292164 PDF-extracted notes LAT1 (large neutral amino acid transporter) as a high-affinity, sodium-independent transporter that facilitates uptake of large neutral amino acids — Phe, Leu, Ile, Val, Tyr, Trp, His, Met. Heterodimeric complex with 4F2 heavy chain. K_m values not cited in this PMC source; cite-by-ref pending PDF verification for LAT1-specific reviews.

**Class K canonical primitive**: asymptotic degrees-of-freedom — saturation toward capacity-bound asymptote per `[[user_stance_asymptotic_dof_sidesteps_infinity]]`. ## §5 — Circadian permeability rhythm (Bucket 5 / Class I)

### §5.1 24h-period oscillation in BBB transport

Cuddapah-Zhang-Sehgal 2019 *Trends in Neuroscience* (PMC6602072 PDF-extracted): *"endogenous circadian rhythm in the BBB controlling transporter function and regulating permeability across the BBB."* TNFα uptake peaks at Zeitgeber times 20-23 (rest phase); leptin influx peaks ZT14-18 (active phase); β-amyloid levels peak ZT18-24.

Kim-Keep-Zhang 2024 *Circulation Research* 134(6):727–747 (PMC11584049 PDF-extracted): *"efflux is higher when the animal is active and is reduced in the inactive phase."* Documented circadian transporters: P-gp/ABCB1, MRP2/ABCC2, BCRP/ABCG2, and ~300 SLC solute carriers.

### §5.2 Mechanism of clock control

Cuddapah 2019 PMC6602072 PDF-extracted: *"At the fly BBB, the perineurial glia maintain circadian oscillations of magnesium (Mg²⁺). High daytime Mg²⁺ levels increase pgp activity and decrease permeability, while nighttime upregulation of gap junction proteins (Inx1 and Inx2) reduces Mg²⁺ in subperineurial glia, decreasing pgp activity and increasing permeability."*

The Mg²⁺-clock mechanism is a substrate-specific implementation of the abstract Class I cyclic-substrate primitive (Mg²⁺ ion concentration oscillating with 24h period).
