## The claim

The nuclear-mass cascade IS (identity-level per `[[user_stance_identity_not_implementation_discipline]]`) a **cyclic exchange around the Fe-56/Ni-62 binding-energy peak**:

- **Fusion (light side; A < 56)**: cascade climbs the BE/A curve toward the asymptote; direction **bulk → gauge** (per Spike #91 + #107). Rate parameter on the asymptotic-DOF spectrum: ε ≈ 0.0167 (Kepler-orbital-rapid end).
- **Fission (heavy side; A > 56)**: cascade descends from the asymptote; direction **gauge → bulk**. Rate parameter on the asymptotic-DOF spectrum: ε ≈ 0.618 (Fibonacci-slow end, `|ψ|`).
- **Both directions instantiate the SAME Cauchy-form Kepler kernel** `c_k = ε^k/k` (per `[[user_stance_kepler_shape_universal]]` Spike #41 sharpening) with DIFFERENT rate parameters.
- **The peak IS the Class K cascade-saturation fixed point** of nuclear-mass arithmetic per `[[user_stance_asymptotic_dof_sidesteps_infinity]]`.
