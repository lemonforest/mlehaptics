## Framing

Biology accesses a sliver of EM (~1 of 26 octaves; visible 400-700 nm) and additional dynamical systems via narrow class-chain compositions in the 14-class A-N vocabulary. The user's direction: enumerate other dynamical systems the body **touches** but doesn't fully decode, find what the user might not be thinking of, identify what prosthetic-augmentation is feasible. **Identity-not-implementation**: a sensory channel IS its class-chain composition at biological substrate, not "implements" the class chain. **Framework-domain stance**: class-chain structure derives from the 14-class algebra; threshold sensitivities (substrate-coupling magnitudes) are observational anchors not framework-derived.

## Survey — 24 channels

### Category A: Biological prior art with verified class chain (9 channels)

1. **Visible-EM photons (400-700 nm)** — L (retinal photon eigenmodes) ∘ C (V1 orientation columns) ∘ M (sparse cortical coding). ~4% of EM. Tetrachromacy + cone-targeted gene therapy already augmenting. Mantis shrimp 12-16 channel is the upper biological limit.

2. **UV-A photons (320-400 nm)** — same L∘C∘M chain, just different rhodopsin variant. Bees, birds, fish, reindeer access. Cataract-removed humans report dim UV-A — biological substrate exists; lens filtering blocks.

3. **IR thermal radiation (~30 THz)** — L (thermal eigenmode resolved by TRPA1-channel-fielded pit organ membrane) ∘ C (trigeminal) ∘ M. Pit vipers, vampire bats. FLIR-style augmentation commercial; BrainPort tongue-display sensory-substitution research demonstrates cortical plasticity (Bach-y-Rita 2003).

4. **Acoustic 20Hz-20kHz** — L (cochlear tonotopic basilar membrane is literal mechanical Class L Laplacian) ∘ C (A1 tonotopic columns) ∘ M. Cochlear implants already directly stimulate substrate.

5. **Magnetoreception (geomagnetic ~25-65 μT)** — M (quantum-coherent radical-pair spin states in CRY4 cryptochrome; HDC-high-D natural fit) ∘ C ∘ I (cyclic direction folding). Birds, turtles, salmon, magnetotactic bacteria. **FeelSpace belt** (König lab) demonstrates cortical-plasticity acquisition of a compass sense via tactile feedback within weeks.

6. **Electroreception (DC-kHz E-field)** — L (E-field eigenmode on receptor array) ∘ C (medullary lateral-line lobe) ∘ M. Sharks (Ampullae of Lorenzini), platypus, paddlefish, weakly electric fish. **Bumblebees** (Clarke+ 2013 Science) detect flower electrostatic charge — proves terrestrial substrate viability.

7. **Polarised-light** — L (polarisation eigenmode on dichroic microvilli) ∘ C ∘ M. Bees DRA compass; cephalopods; mantis shrimp circular polarisation (only known biological circular-polarisation channel).

8. **Infrasound (~0.01-20 Hz)** — same L∘C∘M, lower-frequency mechanical eigenmode resonators. Elephant foot-bone seismic, whale, alligator, pigeon otolith.

9. **Ultrasound / echolocation (~20-200 kHz)** — active emission Class C + reflection L ∘ M + Class I cyclic Doppler-folding. Bats, dolphins, oilbirds. **Human echolocation** (Thaler & Goodale 2016 PNAS; Daniel Kish Flash Sonar) recruits V1 for tongue-click reflection processing.

### Category B: Novel candidate channels the user might not be thinking of (12 channels)

These are channels where the body **touches** the dynamical system but biology doesn't decode it consciously. All compose cleanly into A-N. All except 3 have low-cost prosthetic-augmentation paths.

1. **Cosmic ray flux** — L (Cherenkov eigenmode in eye vitreous fluid when particle exceeds c/n) ∘ C ∘ M. Apollo astronauts report visual flashes; passive at terrestrial flux but reliable in space / high altitude. **CRAYFIS smartphone app** (Whiteson+ 2014 arXiv:1410.2895) detects cosmic rays via CMOS. ~$50 scintillator + SiPM gives full-body cosmic-ray awareness. Integrates with category-B-12 below.

2. **Schumann resonances (7.83 Hz fundamental + harmonics)** — L (Earth-ionosphere cavity eigenmode) ∘ I (cyclic harmonic series 14.1, 20.3, 26.4, 32.5 Hz) ∘ M. **Class L at literal global scale** — the planet IS the Class L Laplacian eigenproblem here. Biological coupling speculative (alpha-rhythm entrainment, Cherry 2002 controversial). Low-cost ELF coil + lock-in amp tests it cleanly.

3. **Geomagnetic micropulsations Pc1-Pc5 (~0.001-5 Hz)** — L (magnetospheric cavity eigenmodes) ∘ I (Pc band classification) ∘ M. Proposed bird-navigation auxiliary; no isolated detector. Fluxgate magnetometer + tactile gives space-weather body-awareness.

4. **Atmospheric vertical E-field (~100 V/m fair weather)** — L (vertical-axis eigenmode) ∘ C ∘ M. Bumblebee electrostatic prior art proves substrate viability; humans null. Field-mill electrometer + tactile is trivial sensory-augment.

5. **Earth free oscillations / normal modes (~0.3-10 mHz)** — L (planetary spheroidal/toroidal mode 0S2 etc.) ∘ I (cyclic mode-index spherical-harmonic structure) ∘ M. **The whole planet IS a Class L eigenproblem here.** Broadband seismometer ($100 Raspberry Shake) + frequency-shift to audible. Reveals planetary loop-down after major earthquakes — demonstrates `[[user_stance_string_theory_instrument_first]]` loop-down concept at planetary substrate. **Book-worthy: this is the cleanest planetary-scale Class L demonstration available.**

6. **Tidal gravitational micro-acceleration** — L (cyclic tidal gradient on quasi-2D Earth surface) ∘ I (lunar 27.3d + solar 365d + composite 28d) ∘ C. Intertidal organisms, corals exhibit circa-tidal/circa-lunar rhythms. Consumer-grade tilt-corrected gravimeter + tactile gives "tidal-phase feeling."

7. **Radon-218/222 alpha-particle flux** — L (alpha-ionisation deposit eigenmode) ∘ C ∘ M. PIN diode + audio click readout. Indoor radon is health-relevant; geological survey hobby instrument.

8. **Atmospheric ion content (~10²-10⁴ cm⁻³)** — L (small-ion mobility eigenmode in Gerdien condenser) ∘ C ∘ M. Mood/circadian correlations controversial. Gerdien condenser ~$200.

9. **Solar UV-B/UV-C variation (~280-320 nm / <280 nm)** — L (UV photon eigenmode) ∘ C ∘ M. UV-B mediates vitamin-D synthesis but isn't "seen"; SiC photodiode + tactile gives skin-cancer-risk + vitamin-D budget awareness.

10. **Air-quality / VOC / CO₂ / PM2.5 chemical landscape** — M (HDC bind-bundle of multi-sensor activations) ∘ L (Class L of VOC co-occurrence) ∘ C. **Olfaction extension**: olfaction's ~400 OR genes are narrow; CO₂/CO/PM2.5 are life-relevant invisibles. Multi-sensor MOX arrays $50-500. The natural extension of biological olfaction.

11. **Pulsar timing (Crab ~33ms, ms-pulsars ~1.5ms)** — L (time-frequency rep of pulse train) ∘ I (cyclic rotational substrate) ∘ K (asymptotic-DOF spindown rate-of-approach). SDR + dish ($1k-10k) at 408 MHz / 1.4 GHz; 33 ms periodicity audibly perceivable. Cosmic perception. **K-class membership: pulsar spindown approaches asymptotic-minimum-rotation-period; rate-of-approach IS Class K per `[[user_stance_epicycle_via_gear_plus_pin]]`.**

12. **Heliospheric current sheet crossing (28-day Sun rotation cycle)** — I (cyclic-group 28d) ∘ C (cascade through cosmic-ray flux modulation) ∘ M. SOHO/ACE/DSCOVR live data + cosmic-ray detector + tactile readout. **Integrates with cosmic-ray channel (B-1):** sector-boundary crossings modulate the muon flux through the body.
