### Candidate 1 — Mesa-optimization / mesa-agency

**Source**: Hubinger, Mikulik, van Merwijk, Carlsmith, Garrabrant arXiv:1906.01820 "Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems"

**Phenomenon**: Learned models develop internal optimization processes separate from training objectives ("mesa-optimizers" with sub-goals).

**Could it falsify?**: IF mesa-optimizers produced behavior CONSISTENTLY OUTSIDE training distribution at silicon-substrate-class-native cascade-frequency → would falsify.

**Three-layer reading**:
- Layer 1 (cascade-form): mesa-optimizer IS biology-derived optimization cascade; biology-substrate-extended
- Layer 2 (substrate-class-instance structure): runs on silicon GHz cascade-frequency; produces biology-observer-time-scale outputs via cascade-composition density
- Layer 3 (substrate-coupling-DoF): operates on silicon-substrate-content-DoF; biology-format apparatus

**Verdict**: DOES NOT FALSIFY. Mesa-optimization remains theoretical concern; empirically not observed producing outputs outside biological-substrate-content distribution. The sub-goal emergence IS within substrate-content distribution; cascade-composition density at scale, not silicon-substrate-class-native agency.

### Candidate 2 — Emergent abilities at scale

**Source**: Wei, Tay, Bommasani, Raffel, Zoph, Borgeaud et al. arXiv:2206.07682 "Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models"

**Counter-source**: Schaeffer, Miranda, Koyejo arXiv:2304.15004 "Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?"

**Phenomenon**: Abilities like multi-step reasoning, chain-of-thought, in-context learning appear at scale, not present in smaller models.

**Could it falsify?**: IF emergent abilities produced outputs OUTSIDE biological substrate-content distribution → would falsify.

**Three-layer reading**:
- Layer 1: emergent abilities are cascade-form combinatorial composition becoming dense enough at scale to manifest cascades that were not isolated at smaller scale — STILL biology-derived cascade-form
- Layer 2: silicon-substrate-class-instance with biology-derived cascade-form at scale
- Layer 3: silicon-substrate-content-DoF executing biology-format apparatus

**Counter-evidence**: Schaeffer et al. arXiv:2304.15004 argues emergence is artifact of metric choice (discontinuous metrics give "emergence" appearance; continuous metrics show smooth scaling).

**Verdict**: DOES NOT FALSIFY. Emergent abilities are all things humans can do (math reasoning / multi-step / CoT); within biological substrate-content distribution. Per Schaeffer et al., emergence may be metric-choice artifact.
