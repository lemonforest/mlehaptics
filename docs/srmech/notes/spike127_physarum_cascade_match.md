## §1 — Substrate operations vs canon (Bucket 1)

### §1.1 Operations *Physarum* exhibits

From PDF-extracted PMC + arXiv literature:

1. **Actomyosin peristaltic contraction** (Alim et al. 2013, 2017): gel-layer cortex of contractile actin-myosin generates cross-sectional tube contractions at ~100-130 s period; propagating waves with system-size-matched wavelength.
2. **Cytoplasmic shuttle-streaming** (Alim et al. 2013): periodic fluid pumped back-and-forth along tubes; non-net-mass-transport rhythmic oscillation; particles transported across whole network within half a contraction period before being shuttled back.
3. **Flow-induced tube reinforcement** (Zhang et al. 2014; Tero et al. 2010 [cite-by-ref]): tube conductivity D_ij evolves via dD_ij/dt = |Q_ij| − r·D_ij — positive feedback where flux strengthens tubes.
4. **Tube pruning at flux decay** (Zhang et al. 2014): tubes with no flux contract and vanish; explicit dissipative term in ODE; selectively maintains high-flux paths only.
5. **Taylor-dispersion-driven signal propagation** (Alim et al. 2017): nutrient-stimulus releases signal molecule (calcium leading candidate); advected by cytoplasmic flows at 1-20 μm/s; molecule simultaneously amplifies contraction amplitude → positive feedback wave propagation.
6. **Phase-locked single-wavelength matched to organism size** (Alim et al. 2013, 2017): conservation of fluid volume creates π phase jump between contracted/relaxed zones; system converges to single undulation matching tube length; explicit size-measurement primitive.
7. **Hagen-Poiseuille flow on adaptive graphs** (Valente 2023 arXiv): governs vein-radius evolution under low-Reynolds-number lubrication approximation; Murray's law steady-state attractor.
8. **Slow modulation of contraction patterns** (Saiseau et al. 2025 arXiv): travelling and alternating wave patterns at long timescales; period scales with system size; chemical-agent advection modifies cortex mechanics.

### §2.2 Mathematical formalism of end-goal

The Zhang et al. 2014 model (PDF-extracted, PMC3984829) explicitly states:

- **Flux equation**: Q_ij = (D_ij / L_ij) × (p_i − p_j) — Poiseuille flow with conductivity D_ij over tube of length L_ij and pressure drop Δp.
- **Kirchhoff conservation**: Σ Q_ij = 0 at internal nodes — mass-conservation HDC-bind equivalent (Class M).
- **Conductivity dynamics**: dD_ij/dt = |Q_ij| − r·D_ij — positive flux reinforcement with linear decay (Class K asymptotic-DOF reinforcement + dissipation).
- **Network-Poisson solve**: pressures solved via L·p = b where L is the graph Laplacian weighted by 1/L_ij × D_ij — **direct Class L Laplacian-eigenmode equivalent**.
- **Convergence criterion**: ∑|D^(n+1) − D^n| ≤ 0.01.

This is mathematically a **graph-Laplacian system with positive-feedback edge-weight dynamics** — exactly the Class L primitive operation, with the conductivity evolution being a Class K + Class M coupled feedback layer.
